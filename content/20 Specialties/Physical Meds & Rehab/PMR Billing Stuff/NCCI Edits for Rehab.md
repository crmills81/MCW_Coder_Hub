## Definition
National Correct Coding Initiative (NCCI) edits prevent payment for inappropriate code combinations.

## Types of Edits
1.  **Procedure-to-Procedure (PTP):** Two codes cannot be billed together unless a modifier is used.
2.  **Medically Unlikely Edits (MUE):** Maximum units billable per day.

## Common PM&R PTP Edits
| Column 1 (Payable) | Column 2 (Bundled) | Modifier Allowed? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **97140** (Manual) | **97124** (Massage) | No (Mutually Exclusive) |
| **97110** (Ther Ex) | **97530** (Ther Act) | Yes (if distinct) |
| **20552** (Trigger Pt) | **97140** (Manual) | Yes (if distinct site) |

## MUE Limits
- **97140:** Typically max 8 units/day per provider.
- **97110:** Typically max 8 units/day per provider.
- **Exceeding MUE:** Requires documentation justification and often modifier **59** or **XS**.

> [!DANGER] Same Day Therapy
> You cannot bill 97124 (Massage) and 97140 (Manual Therapy) for the same body part on the same day. They are mutually exclusive.
