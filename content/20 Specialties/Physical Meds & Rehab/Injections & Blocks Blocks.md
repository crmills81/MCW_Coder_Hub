## Common Codes
- **20552**: Trigger point injection (1 or 2 muscles).
- **20553**: Trigger point injection (3 or more muscles).
- **64450**: Peripheral nerve block (e.g., Occipital).
- **64483+**: Spinal injections (Epidurals/Transforaminal).

## Drug Coding
- Most payers bundle the drug (Lidocaine/Marcaine) into the procedure.
- **Steroids** (e.g., Depo-Medrol) are often billed separately with **HCPCS Level II** (e.g., J1030, J3301).
- Use **Modifier JW** for wasted drug amount.

## Imaging Guidance
- Fluoroscopy or Ultrasound guidance often requires separate codes (e.g., **76942** for US).
- Must save permanent images to support billing guidance.
