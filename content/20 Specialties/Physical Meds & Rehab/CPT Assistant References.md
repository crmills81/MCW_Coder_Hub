# CPT Assistant References for PM&R

**Tags:** #coding/cpt #resources #ama

## Overview
CPT Assistant is the AMA's official publication providing guidance on CPT code usage. These references are authoritative for coding decisions.

## Access
- **Subscription Required:** Available through AMA website or AAPC
- **Searchable Database:** cptcodebook.com (AMA official)
- **Alternative:** AAPC Knowledge Center (requires membership)

## How to Cite CPT Assistant
CPT Assistant, [Month Year], [Topic] Example: CPT Assistant, January 2021, E/M Office Visits

> [!TIP] Audit Defense
> CPT Assistant references can be used as supporting documentation during audits. Keep PDFs of relevant articles in your compliance files.
