## Required Specificity

### Laterality
- **1** = Right
- **2** = Left
- **3** = Bilateral
- **0** = Unspecified (avoid when possible)

### Episode of Care
- **A** = Initial encounter
- **D** = Subsequent encounter
- **S** = Sequela

> [!WARNING] Unspecified Codes
> Avoid codes ending in "0" or "9" when more specific information is available. Unspecified codes are audit triggers.

## Common Combinations
[[M54.16]] (**Low back pain with radiculopathy**) + [[G55.1]] (**Nerve root compression**)

> [!TIP] Injury vs. Chronic
> Use injury codes (**S-codes**) for acute trauma. Use M-codes for chronic/overuse conditions.
