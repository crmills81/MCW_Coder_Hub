## Documentation Requirements

> [!WARNING] Imaging Guidance
> Fluoroscopy or CT guidance is **required** for most spinal injections. Document:
> - Level(s) injected
> - Contrast confirmation (if used)
> - Images saved to permanent record
> - Needle placement verified

## Coding Tips

### Level Counting
- **Cervical:** C1-C7
- **Thoracic:** T1-T12
- **Lumbar:** L1-L5
- **Sacral:** S1-S5

### Add-On Codes
- Add-on codes (**+**) cannot be billed alone
- Must be billed with primary procedure code
- No modifier -[[-51]] on add-on codes

### Same Day Limitations
- Maximum 4 levels typically allowed per session
- Multiple spinal regions same day may require modifier **[[-59]]** or **[[-XS]]**

> [!TIP] Drug Billing
> Steroids are billed separately with HCPCS J-codes:
> - **J3301:** Triamcinolone acetonide (10mg)
> - **J1030:** Methylprednisolone acetate (40mg)
> - Use **[[-JW]]** modifier for wasted amount
