## Musculoskeletal (Chapter 13)
- **Laterality:** Must specify Left, Right, or Bilateral.
- **Episode of Care:** Initial (A), Subsequent (D), Sequela (S).
- **Specificity:** "Low back pain" (**[[M54.50]]**) vs "Lumbar radiculopathy" (**[[M54.16]]**).

---

## Neurological (Chapter 6)
- **[[Stroke]]:** Requires combination codes for side and dominance (**e.g., [[I69.351]]**).
- **[[peripheral neuropathy|Peripheral Neuropathy]]:** Must link to underlying cause (**e.g., Diabetic [[G62.9]] + [[E11.40]]**).

---

## EMG and Nerve Conduction Studies

### CPT Range
- **[[95860]] - [[95877]]**: Needle EMG
- **[[95905]] - [[95913]]**: Nerve Conduction Studies (NCS)

### Coding Rules
1. **NCS:** Coded by the **number of studies**, not nerves. One study = one nerve/muscle group tested.
2. **EMG:** Coded by **limbs** and whether paraspinal muscles were tested.
   - **[[95870]]**: 1 limb
   - **[[95871]]**: 2 limbs
   - **[[95872]]**: 3 limbs
   - **[[95873]]**: 4 limbs + paraspinals

### Documentation
- Must document specific muscles/nerve segments tested.
- Must include interpretation and report.
- **Modifier [[-26]]** (**Professional Component**) if equipment is owned by facility.

> [!NOTE] Same Day E/M
> You can bill E/M on the same day as **EMG** if the E/M is significant and separately identifiable. Use **Modifier -25** on the E/M code.

---

## Functional Limitations
- **Medicare** requires **G-Codes** and **Severity Modifiers** for outpatient therapy claims **(PT/OT/SLP**).
- Physicians **do not bill G-codes on professional claims** but must document the functional limitation to support medical necessity.

---

## Common PM&R Diagnoses
- **[[M54.50]]:** Low back pain, unspecified.
- **[[M25.561]]:** Pain in right knee.
- **[[G89.29]]:** Other chronic pain.
- **[[Z47.1]]:** Aftercare following joint replacement surgery.

> [!WARNING] Unspecified Codes
> Avoid "**Unspecified**" codes (**ending in 9 or 0)** whenever possible. They are **high-risk** for audits. Document laterality and specificity.
