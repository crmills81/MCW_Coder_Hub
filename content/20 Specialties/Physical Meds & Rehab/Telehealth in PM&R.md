## POS and Modifiers
- **POS 02:** Telehealth provided other than in patient's home.
- **POS 10:** Telehealth provided in patient's home.
- **Modifier 95:** Synchronous telemedicine service (real-time audio/video).

## Billable Services
- **E/M Visits:** 99202-99215 allowed via telehealth.
- **Therapy Codes:** Some 97xxx codes allowed via telehealth if interactive communication is used.
- **Injections:** Cannot be performed via telehealth.

## Documentation
- Must document consent for telehealth.
- Must document location of patient and provider.
- Must document technology used (e.g., Zoom for Healthcare, Doxy).

> [!WARNING] Payer Variability
> Medicare telehealth flexibilities change frequently. Commercial payers vary widely. Always verify current policy before billing.
