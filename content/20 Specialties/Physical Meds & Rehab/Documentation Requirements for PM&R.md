## The "Gold Standard" Note
1. **Subjective:** Patient's report of function/pain (VAS scale).
2. **Objective:** ROM, Strength (5/5 scale), Gait analysis, Special tests.
3. **Assessment:** Diagnosis + Functional Limitation.
4. **Plan:** Specific interventions + Frequency/Duration.

## Medical Necessity
- Why does *this* patient need *this* treatment?
- Must show **Functional Progress**. If plateaued, why continue?
- Goals must be **SMART** (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound).

## Red Flags
- Cloned notes (copy/paste).
- Lack of signature/date.
- Treatment without a signed Plan of Care (POC).
- Billing timed codes without time logs.
