## Guidelines
Follows standard **2021/2023 E/M Changes** (**CPT [[99202]]-[[99215]]**).

## Selection Criteria
1. **Medical Decision Making (MDM):** Complexity of problems, data reviewed, risk.
2. **Time:** Total time spent on the day of the encounter (**including chart review, documentation**).

## Rehab Nuances
- **Functional History:** Crucial for MDM. Document impact on ADLs (**Activities of Daily Living**).
- **Plan of Care:** Must be specific. "Continue PT" is insufficient.
- **Co-morbidities:** PM&R patients often have complex neuro/msk overlap. Capture these in ICD-10 to support MDM level.

## Split/Shared Visits
In facility settings (**SNF, Hospital**), rules apply for visits shared between MD and NP/PA. Requires >50% time by billing provider + modifier **FS**.
