## Overview
Place of Service (POS) codes indicate where the service was rendered. This significantly impacts reimbursement rates for PM&R services.

## Common POS Codes
| Code | Setting | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **11** | Office | Private physician office. |
| **22** | Outpatient Hospital | Hospital owned clinic. |
| **31** | SNF | Skilled Nursing Facility (Part A). |
| **32** | SNF | Skilled Nursing Facility (Part B). |
| **51** | Psych Hospital | Inpatient Psychiatric Facility. |
| **61** | Comprehensive Rehab | Inpatient Rehab Facility (IRF). |

## Impact on Billing
- **Office (11):** Higher physician reimbursement.
- **Facility (22, 31, 32):** Lower physician reimbursement; facility bills the overhead.
- **Therapy Thresholds:** Medicare therapy caps may apply differently based on setting.

> [!WARNING] Incident-To Rules
> Incident-to billing is generally **not allowed** in SNF or Hospital settings. It is primarily an Office (POS 11) benefit.
