## Overview
These codes represent therapeutic procedures. They are divided into **Timed** and **Untimed** codes.

## Untimed Codes (1 unit per session)
*Regardless of time spent.*
- **[[97010]]**: Hot/Cold packs
- **[[97012]]**: Mechanical traction
- **[[97014]]**: Electrical stimulation (unattended)
- **[[97024]]**: Diathermy

## Timed Codes (1 unit = 15 mins)
*Requires direct one-on-one contact.*
- **[[97110]]**: Therapeutic exercise
- **[[97112]]**: Neuromuscular reeducation
- **[[97140]]**: Manual therapy
- **[[97530]]**: Therapeutic activities

> [!WARNING] 2024 CPT Changes
> CPT frequently updates the [[97000]] series. Always verify current year codes. Some codes have been deleted or merged.
