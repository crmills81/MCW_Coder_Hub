#### 🏥 Inpatient Professional Fee Overview

**Tags:** #coding/inpatient #profee #overview

## ❤️‍🩹Definition
Professional Fee (ProFee) coding refers to physician services billed on the CMS-1500 form (or electronic equivalent) for care provided in hospital/inpatient settings.

## ⚕️ Key Distinctions

| Setting | Billing Entity | Form |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Professional** | Physician/Provider | CMS-1500 (837P) |
| **Facility** | Hospital | UB-04 (837I) |

## 😷Inpatient ProFee Scope
- Initial hospital care
- Subsequent hospital visits
- Critical care services
- Consultations
- Discharge services
- Prolonged services

## 👩🏾‍⚕️Provider Types
- **Attending Physician:** Primary managing provider
- **Consulting Physician:** Provides specialty input
- **Hospitalist:** Manages inpatient care
- **Intensivist:** Manages ICU/critical care
- **Surgeon:** Pre/post-op management

> [!WARNING] Global Period
> Surgical procedures have a global period (0, 10, or 90 days). E/M services during global period may require modifiers.
