## Overview
Critical care services are for patients who are critically ill or injured.

## Primary Codes

| CPT | Description | Time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **[[99291]]** | Critical care, first 30-74 min | 30-74 min |
| **[[99292]]** | Critical care, each addl 30 min | Each 30 min |

## Critical Care Definition

> [!IMPORTANT] CMS Criteria
> Patient must have:
> - Vital organ system failure
> - High risk of mortality
> - Requires constant physician attention

## Billable Activities

### Included in Critical Care Time
- Bedside care
- Review of labs/imaging
- Documentation
- Communication with family (about care)
- Care coordination

### NOT Included
- Procedures with separate CPT codes
- Teaching activities
- Travel time
- Administrative tasks
