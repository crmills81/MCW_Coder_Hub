# External Links & Authoritative Resources for Inpatient ProFee Coding (2026)

This master reference directory compiles essential federal, commercial, contractor, and professional association web portals for Inpatient Professional Fee (ProFee) coding, billing compliance, Relative Value Unit (RVU) verification, and audit defense.

---

## 🏛️ 1. Federal & CMS Regulatory Portals

Official Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) and Department of Health & Human Services (HHS) repositories establishing baseline billing, medical necessity, and payment regulations.

| Resource Name | Direct Access URL | Operational Use & Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **CMS Main Portal** | [cms.gov](https://www.cms.gov) | Official home for Medicare, Medicaid, and federal health program announcements. |
| **Medicare Internet-Only Manuals (IOM)** | [cms.gov/regulations-and-guidance/guidance/manuals/internet-only-manuals-ioms](https://www.cms.gov/regulations-and-guidance/guidance/manuals/internet-only-manuals-ioms) | **Pub. 100-04 (Claims Processing), Ch. 12:** Physician/NPP E/M (§30.6), Split/Shared (§30.6.13), Critical Care (§30.6.12), Teaching (§100).<br>**Pub. 100-02 (Benefit Policy), Ch. 15:** Covered medical services & medical necessity. |
| **Medicare Coverage Database (MCD)** | [cms.gov/medicare-coverage-database](https://www.cms.gov/medicare-coverage-database) | Search national coverage policies (NCDs), regional policies (LCDs), and Local Coverage Articles (LCAs). |
| **CMS Physician Fee Schedule (PFS) Lookup Tool** | [cms.gov/medicare/physician-fee-schedule/search](https://www.cms.gov/medicare/physician-fee-schedule/search) | Web-based tool to check pricing, non-facility vs. facility RVUs, global surgery days, and bilateral indicators for CPT/HCPCS codes. |
| **PFS Final Rule & RVU Raw Files** | [cms.gov/medicare/payment/fee-schedules/physician/pfs-relative-value-files](https://www.cms.gov/medicare/payment/fee-schedules/physician/pfs-relative-value-files) | Download official CY 2026 RVU files (e.g., `PPRRVU2026`, `GPCI2026`, Conversion Factor updates). |
| **National Correct Coding Initiative (NCCI)** | [cms.gov/medicare/coding-billing/ncci-medicare](https://www.cms.gov/medicare/coding-billing/ncci-medicare) | Practitioner PTP (Procedure-to-Procedure) code pair edits, Medically Unlikely Edits (MUEs), and the annual *NCCI Policy Manual for Medicare Services*. |
| **Medicare Learning Network (MLN)** | [cms.gov/outreach-and-education/medicare-learning-network-mln/mlnproducts](https://www.cms.gov/outreach-and-education/medicare-learning-network-mln/mlnproducts) | Official MLN educational booklets, fact sheets, and MLN Matters® articles covering inpatient E/M and modifier rules. |
| **Federal Register (CMS Proposed & Final Rules)** | [federalregister.gov/agencies/centers-for-medicare-medicaid-services](https://www.federalregister.gov/agencies/centers-for-medicare-medicaid-services) | Official daily publication of federal rules, including annual IPPS and MPFS regulatory overhauls. |
| **HHS Office of Inspector General (OIG)** | [oig.hhs.gov](https://oig.hhs.gov) | OIG Work Plan updates, active audit targets, Inpatient E/M overpayment reports, and Special Fraud Alerts. |

## 📬 7. Recommended Email Subscriptions & Listservs

Maintain up-to-date awareness of mid-year coding updates, NCCI quarterly edits, and MAC payment moratoriums:

1. **CMS MLN Connects®**: Weekly electronic newsletter providing official Medicare policy releases, rule revisions, and payment updates ([Subscribe Here](https://www.cms.gov/outreach-and-education/medicare-learning-network-mln/mlnconnects)).
2. **Regional MAC Email Lists**: Register on your local MAC website (e.g., Noridian, NGS, Palmetto) for specialty-specific listserv alerts (Part B Medical Review).
3. **Federal Register Daily Table of Contents**: Subscribe to CMS agency announcements for annual IPPS and MPFS rule releases.
4. **OIG Notification Service**: Receive alerts whenever new audit reports, Inpatient E/M reviews, or Corporate Integrity Agreements (CIAs) are published ([OIG Subscribe](https://oig.hhs.gov/newsroom/stay-connected/)).
5. **AAPC Coders’ Spotlight & Specialty Newsletters**: Periodic regulatory analysis covering emerging inpatient guidelines and modifier applications.
