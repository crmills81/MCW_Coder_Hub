## Two-Midnight Rule

> [!IMPORTANT] Inpatient Admission Criteria
> - **Inpatient:** Expected stay ≥2 midnights
> - **Observation:** Expected stay <2 midnights
> 
> ##### Exceptions
>- Procedures on inpatient-only list
>- Death before 2 midnights
>- Transfer to another hospital

## Inpatient-Only Procedures

| Category | Examples |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Cardiac** | CABG, valve replacement |

> Always verify patient status before billing. Wrong status = denial.

## Split/Shared (2024+)

> [!IMPORTANT] Substantive Portion
> - More than half of total time, OR
> - Most of the MDM
> - Modifier FS required when physician bills

## Critical Care Policies

| Requirement | Detail |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Minimum Time** | 30 minutes |
| **Documentation** | Total time required |
| **Bundled Services** | Cannot bill separately |
| **Same Day E/M** | Modifier 25 on E/M |

## Appeals Process
1. Redetermination (120 days)
2. Reconsideration (180 days)
3. ALJ Hearing (60 days)
4. Medicare Appeals Council (60 days)
5. Federal Court (60 days)
