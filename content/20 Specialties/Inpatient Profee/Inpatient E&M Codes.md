## Overview
Inpatient E/M codes cover hospital visits for admitted patients. Updated significantly in 2023.

## Initial Hospital Care (99221-99223)

| Code | MDM Level | Typical Time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **[[99221]]** | Low | 40 min |
| **[[99222]]** | Moderate | 60 min |
| **[[99223]]** | High | 80 min |

> [!IMPORTANT] First Visit Only
> Initial hospital care codes are billed **once per admission** by the admitting physician/specialty.

> - Includes final exam, paperwork, prescriptions
> - Cannot bill other E/M same day (except critical care)

## Code Selection Criteria

### Medical Decision Making (MDM)
1. **Problems:** Number and complexity
2. **Data:** Amount and complexity reviewed
3. **Risk:** Risk of complications/morbidity

### Time
- Total time spent on date of encounter
- Includes: Face-to-face, documentation, care coordination
- Excludes: Travel, teaching (for teaching physicians)

> [!TIP] Time Documentation
> If using time for code selection, document total time and activities performed.
>

## 📊 MDM Quick Reference

> [!INFO] 2 Out of 3 Rule
> To qualify for an MDM level, **2 of the 3 elements** (Problems, Data, Risk) must meet the threshold.

| Level | Problems | Data | Risk |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Straightforward** | 1 Self-limited | 1 Category | Minimal (OTC) |
| **Low** | 1 Stable Chronic | 2 Categories | Low (Rx, PT) |
| **Moderate** | 1 Acute Complicated | 3 Categories | Moderate (IV, Hosp) |
| **High** | 1 Life-Threatening | 4 Categories | High (Emergency) |

**Data Categories:**
1. Tests/Docs Reviewed
2. Independent Historian
3. External Provider Discussion
4. External Record Review

**Risk Examples:**
- **Low:** Prescription management
- **Moderate:** Hospital admission, Major surgery
- **High:** Life-threatening illness
