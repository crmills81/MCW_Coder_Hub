>[!Note] DEFINITION of [[hemilaminectomy]]
>**[[hemilaminectomy]]** is a **spinal decompression surgery in which part of the lamina** (**the flat bony arch covering the back of the spinal canal**) is removed on one side of the vertebra, while the midline structures, including the spinous process and the opposite lamina, are preserved. It differs from a **full [[laminectomy]]**, which removes the whole lamina and often the spinous process for wide bilateral **[[decompression]]**. In **CPT** it is grouped with **[[laminotomy]]**, and the surgeon may add a **partial [[facetectomy]], [[foraminotomy]],** and/or **excision** of a herniated disc. The goal is to enlarge the space around the neural elements (**spinal cord, cauda equina, or nerve roots**) that are compressed by a **herniated disc, bone spur, thickened ligament,** or **[[stenosis]]**. It is therapeutic and always pathological in intent, since it treats nerve **[[compression]]** rather than a normal anatomic variant. The conditions you'll see it for are **lumbar spinal [[stenosis]] ([[M48.061]], [[M48.062]]), disc displacement with [[radiculopathy]] ([[M51.16]], M50.12-), disc disorder with [[myelopathy]] ([[M51.06]]), and cauda equina syndrome ([[G83.4]])**. It is often confused with **[[laminectomy]]** (CPT **[[63045]]-[[63048]]**, both laminae and the spinous process, counted by vertebral segment) and with **[[discectomy]]** (removal of the disc only), but a **[[hemilaminectomy]]** is defined by the one-sided bone removal needed to reach the canal.

>|**[[lamin-]]** (lamina)|Latin _lāmina_ (LAH-mih-nah)|"**thin plate**," "**thin layer**," "**flat bony arch of a vertebra**"|
>|**[[-ectomy]]**|Greek _ektomē_ (ek-toh-MAY), from _ek_ (ek, "**out**") + _temnein_ (TEM-nine, "**to cut**")|"**excision**," "**cutting out**," "**surgical removal**" — surgical suffix|
>
> The word is a modern medical compound formed in English from Greek and Latin parts as *[[hemilaminectomy]]* (**noun**), built on the older term *[[laminectomy]]*, which uses the Latin *lamina* and the Greek *ektomē* ("**excision**"). The first English use of the full compound is not verified here. Literally it means **"half-lamina cutting out."** The Greek root *temnein* ("**to cut**") connects [[hemilaminectomy]] to the entire **[[-tomy]] / [[-ectomy]] family**: ***[[laminotomy]]*** (**[[lamin-]] + [[-tomy]] → cutting into the lamina**), ***[[tonsillectomy]]*** (**[[tonsill-]] + [[-ectomy]] → excision of the tonsils**), and *[[discectomy]]* (**[[disc-]] + [[-ectomy]] → excision of a disc**). The Greek prefix *[[hemi-]]* is productive in medical terminology, appearing in ***[[hemiplegia]]***, ***[[hemicolectomy]]***, ***[[hemithyroidectomy]]***, ***[[hemianopia]]***, and ***[[hemisphere]]***.

> [!success]+ 🔀 ALIASES / ALTERNATE TERMS
> 
> - **Hemilaminectomies** _(plural form; the same procedure at more than one level or side)_
> - **[[Laminotomy]]** _(closely related and often interchangeable term in CPT; strictly, a laminotomy is a small opening in the lamina, while a hemilaminectomy removes the half-lamina; both are indexed to CPT [[63020]]-[[63035]])_
> - **[[hemilaminotomy]]** _(variant of laminotomy emphasizing one side)_
> - **Unilateral laminotomy** _(descriptive synonym used in operative reports and the literature)_
> - **Partial laminectomy** _(clinical synonym; removal of only part of the lamina; do not code as a full [[laminectomy]])_
> - **[[Microdiscectomy]]** _(related procedure often done through a hemilaminectomy window; reported with the same laminotomy codes when excising a herniated disc)_
> - **Minimally invasive hemilaminectomy** _(technique subtype — tubular retractor approach through a small incision; CPT [[63030]] includes open or endoscopically assisted approach)_
> - **Lumbar hemilaminectomy** _(anatomic subtype — most common; CPT [[63030]], [[63035]])_
> - **Cervical hemilaminectomy** _(anatomic subtype — CPT [[63020]], [[63035]])_
> - **Thoracic hemilaminectomy** _(anatomic subtype — CPT [[63046]] or [[63055]] depending on the approach)_
> - **Reexploration hemilaminectomy** _(revision subtype — reexploration for recurrent HNP or scar; CPT [[63040]] cervical, [[63042]] lumbar)_

> [!danger]+ 🔗 RELATED TERMS
> 
> - **[[Laminectomy]]** — the broader operation that removes the entire lamina (and usually the spinous process) for bilateral central [[decompression]], as opposed to the one-sided [[hemilaminectomy]]
> - **[[Lamina]]** — shares the *[[lamin-]]* root; the thin bony plate of the vertebral arch that forms the posterior wall of the spinal canal
> - **[[Laminotomy]]** — creation of an opening in the lamina; in CPT the codes [[63020]]-[[63035]] describe both laminotomy and hemilaminectomy
> - **[[Laminoplasty]]** — cervical procedure that reshapes and hinges the lamina rather than removing it; CPT [[63050]]-[[63051]]
> - **[[Foraminotomy]]** — widening of the neural foramen to free the exiting nerve root; often performed with a hemilaminectomy
> - **[[Facetectomy]]** — removal of part of the facet joint, usually medial or partial, to decompress the lateral recess
> - **[[Discectomy]]** — removal of herniated disc material, often through a hemilaminectomy window; a related but separate component
> - **[[Spinal stenosis]]** — narrowing of the spinal canal or foramina compressing neural elements; a leading indication (M48.0-)
> - **[[Radiculopathy]]** — nerve root dysfunction from compression or irritation; key driver of laminotomy-type surgery (M51.1-, M50.1-)
> - **[[Myelopathy]]** — spinal cord dysfunction; with M51.0- or M50.0-, a trigger for urgent decompression
> - **[[Cauda equina syndrome]]** — compression of the lumbosacral nerve roots, a surgical emergency ([[G83.4]])
> - **Post-laminectomy syndrome** — persistent pain after spine surgery ([[M96.1]])
> - **[[Arthrodesis]]** — spinal [[fusion]]; NCCI bundles [[63030]], [[63042]], and [[63047]] into [[22633]] at the same interspace
> - **MRI / CT myelogram** — key imaging tools for planning and confirming the compressive lesion

> > ⚠️ **Coding Note:** The **CPT code** is chosen by the work done, not by the word used in the operative report. **Partial lamina removal** on one side with **[[decompression]], partial [[facetectomy]], [[foraminotomy]], and/or disc [[excision]]** goes to **[[63020]]-[[63035]]**, counted per interspace. A **full [[laminectomy]]** with **[[facetectomy]]** and **[[foraminotomy]]** goes to **[[63045]]-[[63048]]**, counted per vertebral segment, and these codes involve removing both laminae and the spinous process. Do **not** report both a **[[laminotomy]]** code and a **[[laminectomy]]** code at the same level. Both can be reported at different levels when the documentation supports separate medical necessity, with an appropriate modifier such as **[[-59]]** or **[[-XS]].** **[[63035]]** and **[[63048]]** are **add-on codes** and are never reported alone. When the procedure is done bilaterally, **CPT** directs modifier **[[-50]]** for the **[[laminotomy]]** codes. **NCCI** bundles **[[63030]], [[63042]]**, and **[[63047]]** into **[[22633]]** at the same interspace, so a **[[fusion]]** at the same level will absorb the **[[decompression]]**. For the diagnosis, link M51.1- (**[[radiculopathy]]**) or M51.0- (**[[myelopathy]]**) instead of M51.2- when the neural deficit is documented, and use the **neurogenic [[claudication]]** code (**[[M48.062]]**) when it is documented. For **inpatient profee**, watch for **[[-22]]** (**increased procedural services**), **[[-62]]** (**co-surgeons, common with a spine/neurosurgery team**), **[[-80]]** or **[[-82]]** for assistant surgeons, and **[[-78]]** for an unplanned return to the OR for **[[hematoma]]** or **CSF leak**. Documentation triggers for a query are "**[[laminotomy]],**" "**partial laminectomy,**" "**medial [[facetectomy]]**," "**[[foraminotomy]]**," and "**[[microdiscectomy]]**," because the number of levels, laterality, and whether the disc was excised change the code.
