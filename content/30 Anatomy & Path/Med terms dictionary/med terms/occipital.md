>[!note]+ Definition of occipital
>[[ob-|oc-]][[capit-]][[-al]] - back part of the head (referring to back part of head)
>1. ([anatomy](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/anatomy#English "anatomy")) Of, pertaining to, or located within or near the [occiput](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/occiput#English "occiput") (back of the head) or the [occipital bone](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/occipital_bone#English "occipital bone").
___
>[!info]+ Etymology of occipital
>Borrowing from [Middle French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_French "w:Middle French") _[occipital](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/occipital#Middle_French)_, from [Medieval Latin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medieval_Latin "w:Medieval Latin") _[occipitālis](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/occipitalis#Latin "occipitalis")_, from _[occiput](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/occiput#Latin "occiput")_ (“the back of the head, occiput”) +‎ _[-ālis](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/-alis#Latin "-alis")_ (“-al”, adjectival suffix), equivalent of _[occiput](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/occiput#English "occiput")_ +‎ _[-al](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/-al#English "-al")_. [Doublet](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Appendix:Glossary#doublet "Appendix:Glossary") of _[occipitalis](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/occipitalis#English "occipitalis")_."occipital bone." - [enPR](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Appendix:English_pronunciation "Appendix:English pronunciation"): ŏk-sĭp'-ĭt-əl
