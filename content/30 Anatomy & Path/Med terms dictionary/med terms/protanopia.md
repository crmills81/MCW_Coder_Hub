>[!note]+ Definition of [[protanopia]]
>[[prot-]][[a-|an-]][[ops-]][[-ia]] - color blindness to red (the *first* of the primary colors) (condition of not seeing the first_)
___
>[!info]+ Etymology of [[protanopia]]
>early 20th century: from [proto-] ‘original’ (red being regarded as the first component of colour vision) + [an-]1 ‘lacking’ + [-opia]
