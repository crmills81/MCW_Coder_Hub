>[!Note] DEFINITION of [[facetectomy]]
>**[[facetectomy]]** is **the surgical resection or excision of one or more articular facets of a vertebra, specifically targeting the superior or inferior articular processes that comprise the zygapophyseal joint**. It is clinically distinct from a standard **[[laminectomy]]** or **[[laminotomy]]**, which resect the posterior laminar arch, although **[[facetectomy]]** is frequently performed concurrently to decompress neural structures trapped in the lateral recess or intervertebral foramen. Pathologically, **hypertrophic facet [[arthropathy]], subchondral [[sclerosis]], and [[osteophytosis]] narrow spinal conduits**, causing **compressive radiculopathy** or neurogenic claudication seen in **spinal stenosis** (**[[M48.062]]**) and **degenerative spondylolisthesis** (**[[M43.16]]**). Clinical variants include medial **facetectomy** (**which preserves joint integrity and spinal stability**) and complete **facetectomy** (**which intentionally destabilizes the motion segment and mandates concomitant instrumented spinal fusion**). Misidentification commonly occurs when coders conflate incidental medial undercutting performed during a disc exploration with a formal, stand-alone structural facet resection.

>[!info]+ ETYMOLOGY of [[facetectomy]]
>#French
>
>| Component | Origin | Meaning |
>| --- | --- | --- |
>| **[[facet-]]** | French *facette*, diminutive of *face*, from Latin *facies* ("face", "form") | "Little face" — designated in anatomy as the smooth, gliding planar articular process of a joint. |
>| **[[-ectomy]]** | Greek *ektomē* (*ek*, "out" + *tomē*, "a cutting") | Surgical excision, removal, or resection. |
>
>The word entered English in the mid-twentieth century as *facetectomy* (noun), combining the anatomical term *facet* (borrowed into English in the 1620s from French *facette*) with the classical Greek surgical suffix *-ektomē* — literally "**the cutting out of a little face**." The root connects directly to related anatomical and operative terms: **[[facet]], [[facetotomy]]** (incising a facet joint without full excision), and **[[arthrectomy]]** (excision of an entire joint).

> [!success]+ 🔀 ALIASES / ALTERNATE TERMS
> 
> - **Facetectomic** *(adjectival modifier used in operative approaches)*
> - **Excision of facet joint** *(lay surgical descriptor)*
> - **Medial facetectomy** *(partial resection of the medial articular border to relieve lateral recess stenosis)*
> - **Total facetectomy** *(complete removal of superior and inferior articular processes requiring stabilization)*
> - **Subtotal facetectomy** *(bone removal sparing sufficient joint capsule to preserve segmental stability)*
> - **Zygapophysectomy** *(formal anatomical designation of facet joint excision)*
> - **Bilateral facetectomy** *(decompression involving facet joints on both sides of a single vertebra)*
> - **Unilateral facetectomy** *(excision restricted to a single lateral aspect)*
> - **Lumbar facetectomy** *(site-specific decompression at L1–S1, code range **[[M48.061]]**–**[[M48.062]]**)*
> - **Cervical facetectomy** *(site-specific decompression at C1–C7, code range **[[M48.02]]**)*
> - **Thoracic facetectomy** *(site-specific decompression at T1–T12, code range **[[M48.04]]**)*
> - **Gill procedure** *(facetectomy with laminectomy for spondylolisthesis with pars defects, **[[M43.16]]**)*

_____

> [!danger]+ 🔗 RELATED TERMS
> 
> - **Facet preservation** — the opposite of **facetectomy**; conservative surgical sparing of facet joint architecture to prevent iatrogenic instability.
> - **Facetotomy** — shares the root; surgical cutting or division of a facet joint without extensive tissue excision.
> - **Laminotomy** — conservative fenestration of the lamina distinct from facet excision, coded under **[[63030]]**.
> - **Foraminotomy** — surgical enlargement of the intervertebral neuroforamen, frequently executed in tandem with medial facetectomy.
> - **Hypertrophic arthropathy** — mechanical degeneration and overgrowth of the articular surfaces causing lateral recess stenosis.
> - **Spondylotic** — descriptive of degenerative changes affecting vertebral bodies and zygapophyseal joints.
> - **Osteophytosis** — aberrant bone formation along joint margins leading to direct nerve root compression.
> - **Spinal Stenosis** — luminal narrowing of the central canal or lateral recess (**[[M48.062]]**).
> - **Degenerative Spondylolisthesis** — anterior translation of a vertebral body secondary to facet incompetence (**[[M43.16]]**).
> - **Lumbar Radiculopathy** — nerve root dysfunction causing dermatomal radiation of pain and weakness (**[[M54.16]]**).
> - **Computed Tomography Myelography** — radiographic exam visualizing bony nerve root entrapment within the lateral recess.

> > ⚠️ **Coding Note:** CPT codes **[[63047]]** and **[[63045]]** inherently include facetectomy, foraminotomy, and laminectomy when performed at the same spinal segment; unbundling these components as separate procedures violates National Correct Coding Initiative (NCCI) edits. Note that code **[[63047]]** is defined by the American Medical Association as unilateral or bilateral; therefore, reporting modifier **[[-50]]** on **[[63047]]** is incorrect and will cause claim denials. Conversely, laminotomy codes such as **[[63030]]** are inherently unilateral and require modifier **[[-50]]** when bilateral decompression is documented at the same interspace. In circumstances where facetectomy is performed strictly for surgical exposure during a posterior transforaminal interbody fusion (TLIF), the bony resection is considered inclusive to arthrodesis and cannot be independently reported unless distinct nerve root decompression for pre-existing radiculopathy is clearly documented and justified with modifier **[[-59]]** or **[[-XU]]**.
