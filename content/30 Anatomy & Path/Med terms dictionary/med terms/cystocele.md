>[!note]+ Definition of cystocele
> [[cyst-]]o[[-cel-]][[-e]] - Bladder hernia; A cystocele, also known as a prolapsed or dropped bladder, occurs when **the wall between a woman's bladder and the vagina weakens, causing the bladder to sag or bulge into the vagina**
___
>[!info]+ Etymology of cystocele
>From [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language "w:French language") _[cystocèle](https://en.wiktionary.org/w/index.php?title=cystoc%C3%A8le&action=edit&redlink=1 "cystocèle (page does not exist)")_, from [Ancient Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Greek "w:Ancient Greek") _[κύστις](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%CE%BA%CF%8D%CF%83%CF%84%CE%B9%CF%82#Ancient_Greek "κύστις")_ (kústis, “anatomical sac”). By [surface analysis](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Appendix:Glossary#surface_analysis "Appendix:Glossary"), _[cysto-](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/cysto-#English "cysto-")_ +‎ _[-cele](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/-cele#English "-cele")_.
