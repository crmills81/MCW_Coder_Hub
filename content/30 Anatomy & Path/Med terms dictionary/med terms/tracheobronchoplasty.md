>[!Note] DEFINITION of [[tracheobronchoplasty]]
>**[[tracheobronchoplasty]]** is **a reconstructive surgical intervention designed to restore structural integrity to the airway in patients suffering from excessive airway collapse, most commonly due to [[tracheobronchomalacia]] (TBM)**. Unlike **simple stenting**, which provides temporary support, this procedure involves the application of a **mesh** or **splint** to the **posterior membranous** wall of the trachea and main bronchi to prevent expiratory collapse. It is distinct from a **[[tracheostomy]]**, which bypasses the upper airway, as it addresses the underlying mechanical weakness of the airway wall itself. Pathologically, this is indicated when the airway lumen narrows by more than 50% during forced **expiration**, leading to **chronic cough, [[dyspnea]]**, and recurrent **infections**. Clinical subtypes are categorized by the extent of the airway involved, ranging from **localized tracheal involvement** (**[[J39.8]]**) to **diffuse tracheobronchial involvement** (**[[J98.09]]**).

>#greek
>
>| **Component** | **Origin** | **Meaning** |
>| :---: | --- | --- |
>| **[[trache/o-]]** | Greek, *tracheia*, "**rough**" | "**Windpipe**" — refers to the cartilaginous tube. |
>| **[[bronch/o-]]** | Greek, *bronchos*, "**windpipe**" | "**Airway**" — refers to the bronchial tree. |
>| **[[-plasty]]** | Greek, *plastos*, "**formed**" | Surgical repair or reconstruction. |
>
>The word entered English in the late 20th century as *[[tracheobronchoplasty]]* (**noun**), derived from the combination of anatomical roots and the surgical suffix. It describes the literal "**molding or shaping of the trachea and bronchi**" to restore patency. The root connects to related terms: ***[[tracheotomy]]*** (**cutting the trachea**) and *[[bronchoscopy]]* (viewing the bronchi).

> [!danger]+ 🔗 RELATED TERMS
> 
> - **[[Tracheostomy]]** — the creation of an opening in the trachea; distinct as it is a bypass rather than a reconstruction.
> - **[[Bronchoscopy]]** — endoscopic visualization; the primary diagnostic tool for identifying [[malacia]].
> - **[[Tracheomalacia]]** — softening of the tracheal cartilage; the primary pathology treated by **[[tracheobronchoplasty]]**.
> - **[[Bronchomalacia]]** — softening of the bronchial cartilage; often co-exists with TBM.
> - **Stenting** — internal support; often used as a trial before permanent **[[tracheobronchoplasty]]**.
> - **[[Dyspnea]]** — clinical symptom of airway collapse.
> - **Obstructive lung disease** — functional category of respiratory impairment.
> - **Chronic [[bronchitis]]** — **[[J42]]**; often a differential diagnosis for chronic cough.
> - **Asthma** — **[[J45.909]]**; often misdiagnosed in patients with TBM.
> - **COPD** — **[[J44.9]]**; frequently comorbid with airway malacia.
> - **Pulmonary function testing** — diagnostic tool to assess flow-volume loops.

> > ⚠️ **Coding Note:** **[[tracheobronchoplasty]]** is a **complex thoracic procedure**. When coding, ensure the documentation specifies whether the approach was **cervical** or **thoracic**, as this dictates the selection of **[[31780]]** vs **[[31781]]**. If **mesh** or **prosthetic material** is used, ensure the supply is captured. Always sequence the underlying diagnosis (e.g., **[[J98.02]]**) as the primary diagnosis. Use modifier **[[-22]]** if the procedure was significantly more complex than **standard due to extensive adhesions or anatomical deformity.**
