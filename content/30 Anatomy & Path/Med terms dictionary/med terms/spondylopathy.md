>[!Note] DEFINITION of [[spondylopathy]]
>**[[spondylopathy]]** is a **general term for any disease or disorder of the vertebrae and their joints, covering inflammatory, degenerative, traumatic, infectious, and metabolic processes of the spinal column**. In coding it is best known as the name of **ICD-10-CM block M45-M49**, which groups **[[ankylosing spondylitis]]** (**M45**), other **inflammatory spondylopathies** (**M46**), **[[spondylosis]]** (**M47**), other **spondylopathies** (**M48**), and **spondylopathies** in diseases classified elsewhere (**M49**). It differs from **[[spondylitis]]**, which means **vertebral inflammation** specifically, from **[[spondylosis]]**, which means **[[degenerative]]** spinal change, and from **[[dorsopathy]]**, the broader group (**M40-M54**) that also includes deformities and disc disorders. The mechanism depends on the type: **[[autoimmune]]** **[[inflammation]]** with new bone formation (**[[ankylosing spondylitis]]**), infection of the vertebra or disc (**[[osteomyelitis]], [[discitis]]**), **[[degeneration]]** of the **facet joints and discs** (**[[spondylosis]]**), fracture or collapse (**[[osteoporosis]], [[tumor]], fatigue**), or **ligament ossification** (**[[hyperostosis]]**). It is always pathological, though a degenerative form is also common with age. The forms you'll code most are **[[ankylosing spondylitis]]** (**M45.-**), **vertebral [[osteomyelitis]] and [[discitis]]** (**M46.2-, M46.4-**), **[[spondylosis]]** (**M47.-**), **[[spinal stenosis]]** (**M48.0-**), **collapsed vertebra** (**M48.5-**), and **[[spondylopathy]]**, unspecified (**[[M48.9]]**). It is often confused with **[[spondyloarthropathy]]** (**the inflammatory group, such as [[ankylosing spondylitis]], psoriatic and reactive arthritis**), and with **[[spondylolisthesis]]** (vertebral slippage, M43.1-, in a different block).

>[!info]+ ETYMOLOGY of [[spondylopathy]]
>#greek #new-latin
>
>|**Component**|**Origin**|**Meaning**|
>| :---:|---|---|
>|**[[spondyl-]]**|Greek _spondylos_ (SPON-dih-los)|"**vertebra**," "**spinal bone**"|
>|**[[-pathy]]**|Greek _-patheia_ (PATH-ay-ah), from _pathos_ (PATH-os)|Noun-forming suffix — "**disease**," "**suffering**," "**disorder of**"|
>
> The word entered English in the **19th century** (exact first-use date not verified) as *spondylopathy* (**noun**), a New Latin compound built from Greek ***spóndylos*** and ***-patheia*** — literally **"vertebra disease."** The Greek ***pathos*** ("**suffering, disease, feeling**") gives the combining form ***[[-pathy]]***, which medical writers attach to an organ to mean "**disease of that organ**." The **WHO** and **ICD-10-CM** use *spondylopathies* as the block title for **M45-M49**. The root ***spondylos*** ("**vertebra**") connects **[[spondylopathy]]** to the entire **[[spondyl-]] family**: ***[[spondylitis]]*** (**[[spondyl-]] + [[-itis]] → vertebral [[inflammation]]**), ***[[spondylosis]]*** (**[[spondyl-]] + [[-osis]] → vertebral condition, usually degenerative**), and *spondyloarthropathy* (**[[spondylo-]] + [[arthro-]] + [[-pathy]] → disease of the joints of the spine**). The suffix ***[[-pathy]]*** is very productive in medical terminology, appearing in ***[[neuropathy]]***, ***[[myopathy]]***, ***[[nephropathy]]***, ***[[retinopathy]]***, and ***[[cardiomyopathy]]***.

> [!success]+ 🔀 ALIASES / ALTERNATE TERMS
> 
> - **Spondylopathic** _(adjective form — "spondylopathic changes," "spondylopathic disease")_
> - **Vertebral disease / disease of the vertebrae** _(lay and clinical term for a disorder of the spinal bones)_
> - **[[spondylopathy]], unspecified** _(partial form — the nonspecific diagnosis when the type is not documented; [[M48.9]])_
> - **Other specified spondylopathies** _(clinical descriptor — M48.8X-; used when a named spondylopathy has no more specific code)_
> - **Spondyloarthropathy / spondyloarthritis** _(related entity — group of inflammatory diseases of the spine and joints, including ankylosing spondylitis, psoriatic, reactive, and enteropathic arthritis)_
> - **[[Ankylosing spondylitis]]** _(inflammatory subtype — Greek *ankylos* "crooked or curved" + *spondylos* + *[[-itis]]*; M45.-)_
> - **Inflammatory spondylopathy** _(etiologic subtype — infection or autoimmune; M46.-)_
> - **Infective spondylopathy** _(etiologic subtype — vertebral [[osteomyelitis]], [[discitis]]; M46.2-, M46.4-, M46.5-)_
> - **Traumatic / fatigue spondylopathy** _(etiologic subtype — stress or fatigue fracture of a vertebra; M48.4-)_
> - **Collapsed vertebra** _(etiologic subtype — vertebral body collapse from osteoporosis, tumor, or disease; M48.5-, M49.8-)_
> - **[[Spondylosis]]** _(degenerative subtype — arthrosis of the spine, facet degeneration; M47.-)_
> - **[[Spinal stenosis]] / DISH** _(structural subtypes — canal narrowing M48.0-, ankylosing [[hyperostosis]] of Forestier M48.1-)_

> [!danger]+ 🔗 RELATED TERMS
> 
> - **[[Spondylitis]]** — shares the *[[spondyl-]]* root; inflammation of one or more vertebrae
> - **[[Spondylosis]]** — degenerative arthritis of the spine with facet and disc wear (M47.-)
> - **[[Spondylolysis]]** — a defect of the pars interarticularis (M43.0-)
> - **[[spondylolisthesis]]** — slippage of one vertebra on another (M43.1-)
> - **[[Spondyloarthropathy]]** — group of inflammatory diseases affecting the spine, SI joints, and other sites, often HLA-B27-linked
> - **[[Ankylosing spondylitis]]** — chronic inflammatory disease causing sacroiliitis, stiffening, and bony fusion of the spine (M45.-)
> - **[[Sacroiliitis]]** — inflammation of the sacroiliac joints, a hallmark of ankylosing spondylitis and a coded entity in its own right (M46.1)
> - **[[Discitis]]** — inflammation or infection of an intervertebral disc (M46.4-)
> - **Vertebral [[osteomyelitis]]** — bone infection of a vertebra, usually bacterial (M46.2-)
> - **[[Spinal stenosis]]** — narrowing of the spinal canal or foramina (M48.0-)
> - **Diffuse idiopathic skeletal [[hyperostosis]]** — also called DISH or Forestier disease; flowing ossification along the spine (M48.1-)
> - **[[Dorsopathy]]** — the broader ICD-10 group of back disorders (M40-M54), which includes deformities, spondylopathies, and disc disorders
> - **[[Syndesmophyte]]** — thin bony bridge between vertebrae, seen in ankylosing spondylitis
> - **HLA-B27** — genetic marker associated with ankylosing spondylitis and related spondyloarthropathies
> - **X-ray / MRI** — key diagnostic tools; MRI detects early [[inflammation]] and infection, and plain films show structural change and fusion

> > ⚠️ **Coding Note:** "**[[spondylopathy]]**" on its own is nonspecific, so the unspecified diagnosis **[[M48.9]]** is a last resort and should prompt a query for the actual type: inflammatory (**M45-M46**), degenerative (**M47**), **[[stenosis]]** or other (**M48**), or secondary to another disease (**M49**). Site is required for most codes in this block, and the site character in the code must match the documented level. Check the combination codes first: **[[spondylosis]]** codes distinguish **[[myelopathy]]** (**M47.1-**), **[[radiculopathy]]** (**M47.2-**), and neither (**M47.81-, M47.89-**), so choose the most specific of those. **Facet [[arthropathy]]** and "**[[osteoarthritis]] of the spine**" are indexed to **[[spondylosis]]** (**M47.-**), not to **[[M48.9]]**, so I recommend verifying each in the Alphabetic Index. **M49.-** is an "**in diseases classified elsewhere**" category, so the underlying disease is coded first and this code is secondary, and it can never be a principal diagnosis. Common undercodes are "**back stiffness**" and "**SI joint pain**" in a patient with "**HLA-B27 positive**," "**inflammatory back pain**," or "**[[psoriasis]]**," which should prompt a query for **[[ankylosing spondylitis]]** or **axial [[spondyloarthritis]]** (**M45.-**). **Vertebral [[osteomyelitis]]** or **[[discitis]]** with fever and elevated **ESR** or **CRP** should prompt a query for organism (**use an additional B95-B98 code**) and spinal cord involvement. For **CPT**, SI joint injections (**[[27096]]**) and facet injections (**[[64493]]**) need imaging guidance included in the code, laterality (**[[-RT]], [[-LT]], or [[-50]]**) where the code is not laterality-specific, and **[[-59]]** or **[[-XS]]** for separate levels or sessions. Many payers want documented failed conservative therapy and imaging findings before injections or fusion, and biologic therapy for **[[ankylosing spondylitis]]** usually needs prior authorization linked to the exact **M45.- code**. For **inpatient surgery**, watch for **[[-22]], [[-62]], [[-80]] or [[-82]], and [[-78]]** as in other spine operations.
