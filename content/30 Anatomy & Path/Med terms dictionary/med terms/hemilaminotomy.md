>[!Note] DEFINITION of [[hemilaminotomy]]
>**[[hemilaminotomy]]** is **a spinal [[decompression]] surgery in which a small opening is cut into the lamina** (**the flat bony arch covering the back of the spinal canal**) on one side of the vertebra, usually with a **high-speed burr or Kerrison punch**, and only enough bone is removed to reach the compressed nerve root or disc. It differs from a **[[hemilaminectomy]]**, which removes most or all of the half-lamina on one side, and from a full **[[laminectomy]]**, which removes both laminae and often the spinous process. The "**[[-otomy]]**" suffix means cutting into, so a **[[hemilaminotomy]]** creates a window, while an "**[[-ectomy]]**" removes the structure. The mechanism of benefit is **[[decompression]]**: **enlarging the space around nerve roots, the cauda equina, or the spinal cord that are compressed by a herniated disc, a bone spur, a thickened ligamentum flavum, or [[stenosis]]**. It is always therapeutic and pathological in intent (**lumbar disc [[herniation]], lateral recess stenosis, foraminal stenosis**). The diagnoses you'll code most are disc disorders with **[[radiculopathy]]** (**[[M51.16]], [[M51.17]], M50.12-**), **lumbar spinal stenosis** (**[[M48.061]], [[M48.062]]**), and disc displacement (**[[M51.26]], [[M51.27]]**). It is often confused with **[[hemilaminectomy]]** and **[[laminectomy]]**, but **CPT [[63020]]-[[63035]]** report them as the same **[[laminotomy]]** (**[[hemilaminectomy]]**) family, so the code does not depend on which word the surgeon used. A variant, **unilateral [[laminotomy]] for bilateral [[decompression]]** (**ULBD, "over the top"**), uses a one-sided approach to decompress both sides of the canal.

>[!info]+ ETYMOLOGY of [[hemilaminotomy]]
>#greek #latin
>
>|**Component**|**Origin**|**Meaning**|
>|:---:|---|---|
>|**[[hemi-]]**|Greek _hēmi-_ (HEH-mee)|"**half**," "**one side**" — quantitative prefix|
>|**[[lamin-]]** (lamina)|Latin _lāmina_ (LAH-mih-nah)|"**thin plate**," "**thin layer**," "**flat bony arch of a vertebra**"|
>|**[[-otomy]]** / **[[-tomy]]**|Greek _tomē_ (toh-MAY), from _temnein_ (TEM-nine, "**to cut**")|Surgical suffix — "**cutting into**," "**incision**," "**making an opening**" (the connecting vowel _[[-o-]]_ links the Latin stem to the Greek suffix)|
>
> The word is a modern medical compound formed in English from Latin and Greek parts as *[[hemilaminotomy]]* (**noun**), built on *[[laminotomy]]*. The first English use of the full compound is not verified here. Literally it means **"half-lamina cutting."** The Greek root *temnein* ("**to cut**") connects [[hemilaminotomy]] to the entire **[[-tomy]] family**: ***laminotomy*** (**[[lamin-]] + [[-otomy]] → cutting into the lamina**), ***[[craniotomy]]*** (**[[crani-]] + -otomy → cutting into the skull**), and *[[tracheotomy]]* (**trache- + -otomy → cutting into the windpipe**). The Greek prefix *[[hemi-]]* is productive in medical terminology, appearing in ***[[hemiplegia]]***, ***[[hemicolectomy]]***, ***[[hemithyroidectomy]]***, ***[[hemianopia]]***, and ***[[hemisphere]]***.

> [!success]+ 🔀 ALIASES / ALTERNATE TERMS
> 
> - **Hemilaminotomies** _(plural form; the same procedure at more than one level or side)_
> - **[[Laminotomy]]** _(parent term; in CPT, [[63020]]-[[63035]] describe "laminotomy (hemilaminectomy)" as one procedure)_
> - **[[Hemilaminectomy]]** _(commonly interchangeable; strictly, removes more bone on one side than a hemilaminotomy window)_
> - **Unilateral [[laminotomy]]** _(descriptive synonym used in operative reports and the literature)_
> - **Partial laminectomy** _(clinical synonym; removal of only part of the lamina; do not code as a full laminectomy)_
> - **ULBD** _(technique subtype — unilateral laminotomy for bilateral decompression; a one-sided approach to treat both sides in lumbar stenosis)_
> - **Microscopic tubular laminotomy** _(technique subtype — minimally invasive approach through a tubular retractor, performed with a microscope)_
> - **Microdiscectomy** _(related procedure usually done through a hemilaminotomy window; coded with the same laminotomy codes when a herniated disc is excised)_
> - **Lumbar hemilaminotomy** _(anatomic subtype — most common; CPT [[63030]], [[63035]])_
> - **Cervical hemilaminotomy** _(anatomic subtype — CPT [[63020]], [[63035]])_
> - **Thoracic hemilaminotomy** _(anatomic subtype — CPT [[63046]] or [[63055]] depending on the approach)_
> - **Reexploration hemilaminotomy** _(revision subtype — reexploration for recurrent HNP or scar; CPT [[63040]] cervical, [[63042]] lumbar)_

> [!danger]+ 🔗 RELATED TERMS
> 
> - **[[hemilaminectomy]]** — the sibling procedure that removes more of the half-lamina on one side; used interchangeably in many reports
> - **[[Laminotomy]]** — shares the *[[lamin-]]* root; a window in the lamina on one or both sides
> - **[[Laminectomy]]** — the broader operation that removes both laminae and usually the spinous process for wide bilateral [[decompression]]
> - **Lamina** — the thin bony plate of the vertebral arch that forms the posterior wall of the spinal canal
> - **[[Laminoplasty]]** — cervical procedure that hinges and reshapes the lamina rather than removing it; CPT [[63050]]-[[63051]]
> - **[[Foraminotomy]]** — widening of the neural foramen to free the exiting nerve root; usually done with a [[hemilaminotomy]]
> - **[[Facetectomy]]** — removal of part of the facet joint, usually partial or medial, to decompress the lateral recess
> - **[[Discectomy]]** — removal of herniated disc material through the laminotomy window; related but a separate component
> - **[[Spinal stenosis]]** — narrowing of the spinal canal or foramina compressing neural elements; a leading indication (M48.0-)
> - **[[Radiculopathy]]** — nerve root dysfunction from compression or irritation; the main indication for a [[hemilaminotomy]] (M51.1-, M50.1-)
> - **[[Cauda equina syndrome]]** — [[compression]] of the lumbosacral nerve roots, a surgical emergency ([[G83.4]])
> - **Post-laminectomy syndrome** — persistent pain after spine surgery ([[M96.1]])
> - **[[Arthrodesis]]** — spinal fusion; NCCI bundles [[63030]], [[63042]], and [[63047]] into [[22633]] at the same interspace
> - **MRI / CT myelogram** — key imaging tools for planning and confirming the compressive lesion

> > ⚠️ **Coding Note:** The **CPT code** follows the work done, not the word in the operative report, so "**[[hemilaminotomy]]**," "**[[hemilaminectomy]]**," "**[[laminotomy]]**," and "**partial laminectomy**" with **nerve root decompression** all point to **[[63020]]**-**[[63035]]**. CPT counts these by interspace, while **[[63045]]**-**[[63048]]** count by vertebral segment, so a one-sided window at one level is not a **full laminectomy**. A **ULBD** that decompresses both sides through a one-sided approach is still reported by the work documented, so query the surgeon about whether it is a **[[laminotomy]]** (**[[63030]]**) or a **[[laminectomy]]** with **[[facetectomy]]** and **[[foraminotomy]]** (**[[63047]]**). Do not report **[[63030]]** and **[[63047]]** at the same level. **[[63035]]** and **[[63048]]** are add-on codes and are never reported alone. When the **laminotomy** is done bilaterally, CPT directs modifier **[[-50]]** for the **laminotomy** codes. NCCI bundles **[[63030]]**, **[[63042]]**, and **[[63047]]** into **[[22633]]** at the same interspace, so a **[[fusion]]** at the same level will absorb the **[[decompression]]**. For the diagnosis, link **M51.1-** (**[[radiculopathy]]**) or **M51.0-** (**[[myelopathy]]**) instead of **M51.2-** when the neural deficit is documented, and use **[[M48.062]]** when **[[claudication|Neurogenic claudication]]** is documented. For inpatient profee, watch for **[[-22]]** (increased procedural services), **[[-62]]** (co-surgeons), **[[-80]]** or **[[-82]]** for assistant surgeons, and **[[-78]]** for an unplanned return to the OR (**for example, for [[hematoma]] or CSF leak**). Documentation triggers for a query are "**partial [[facetectomy]]**," "**[[foraminotomy]]**," "**[[microdiscectomy]]**," "**over the top decompression**," and "**tubular retractor**," because the levels, laterality, and whether the disc was excised change the code.
