>[!summary] DEFINITION of [[tracheobronchomalacia]]
>**[[tracheobronchomalacia]]** is a clinical condition defined b**y the loss of structural integrity in the cartilaginous rings of the trachea and bronchi, resulting in abnormal airway wall compliance**. Unlike healthy airways that maintain patency during respiratory cycles, **TBM** patients experience significant luminal narrowing during forced expiration or coughing, which can lead to **air trapping** and **chronic obstructive symptoms.** It is distinct from fixed **[[stenosis]]**, as the collapse is dynamic and pressure-dependent. Pathophysiologically, it may be congenital (**often associated with syndromic presentations**) or acquired due to **chronic [[inflammation]]**, **mechanical [[ventilation]]**, or **external [[compression]]**. Clinical subtypes include **primary TBM, secondary TBM**, and **excessive dynamic airway collapse (EDAC)**, which are often coded under **[[J98.09]]**.

It is a compound construction combining the anatomical sites with the pathological state of softening. The root connects to related terms: *[[osteomalacia]]* (**softening of bone**) and *[[tracheomalacia]]* (**softening of the trachea alone**).

> [!danger]+ 🔗 RELATED TERMS to [[tracheobronchomalacia]]
> 
> - **Tracheal [[Stenosis]]** — the opposite of [[tracheobronchomalacia]]; fixed narrowing of the airway rather than dynamic collapse.
> - **[[Bronchiectasis]]** — shares the airway involvement; permanent dilation of bronchi.
> - **[[COPD]]** — closely related entity; often overlaps with TBM symptoms, coded as [[J44.9]].
> - **[[Asthma]]** — overlap description; can mimic TBM symptoms, coded as [[J45.909]].
> - **Airway Compliance** — physiological mechanism; the measure of airway wall stiffness.
> - **Expiratory collapse** — description of the dynamic event.
> - **Cartilage [[atrophy]]** — cellular mechanism; loss of structural support.
> - **[[Polychondritis]]** — disease entity; systemic cartilage [[inflammation]], coded as [[M94.1]].
> - **Mounier-Kuhn Syndrome** — disease entity; [[tracheobronchomegaly]], coded as [[Q32.1]].
> - **[[Cystic Fibrosis]]** — disease entity; chronic airway damage, coded as [[E84.9]].
> - **[[Bronchoscopy]]** — key diagnostic tool for visual confirmation of collapse.
