>[!Note] DEFINITION of [[periostitis]]
>**[[periostitis]]** is the **inflammation of the periosteum**, a dense layer of **[[vascular]] connective tissue** that envelops the bones. The condition is generally chronic and is marked by tenderness, swelling of the bone, and an aching pain. Acute **[[periostitis]]** is due to **infection** and is characterized by **diffuse suppuration, severe pain**, and constitutional symptoms, and it usually results in **[[necrosis]]**. **[[periostitis]]** is distinct from **[[osteomyelitis]]**, which is **[[inflammation]]** of the bone marrow and adjacent bone; **[[periostitis]]** is limited to the outer fibrous covering. It can be physiological, such as the benign periosteal reaction seen in growing children, or pathological, as seen in infectious, traumatic, or neoplastic conditions. Clinically, it is commonly encountered as shin splints (**medial tibial stress syndrome**) and as **orbital [[periostitis]]** (H05.03-). The key difference from **[[osteitis]]** is that periostitis involves only the **[[periosteum]]**, whereas **[[osteitis]]** involves the bone tissue itself.

>[!info]+ ETYMOLOGY of [[periostitis]]
>#greek
>
>|Component|Origin|Meaning|
>|---|---|---|
>|**[[peri-]]**|Greek _περί_ (perí)|"**around**," "**about**" — a prefix denoting surrounding or enveloping|
>|**[[oste-]]**|Greek _ὀστέον_ (osteon)|"**bone**" — the combining form for bone|
>|**[[-itis]]**|Greek _-ῖτις_ (-itis)|"**pertaining to**" — a suffix forming nouns denoting inflammation|
>
> The word entered English in the **1835-1845 period** as *periostitis* (**noun**), borrowed from New Latin, from *periosteum* + *[[-itis]]* — literally **"inflammation around the bone."** The root *osteon* ("**bone**") connects PERIOSTITIS to the entire **[[-OSTE-]] ROOT FAMILY**: ***[[osteomyelitis]]*** (**osteon** + **myelos** + **[[-itis]]** → [[inflammation]] of bone and marrow), ***[[osteoporosis]]*** (**osteon** + **poros** + **[[-osis]]** → porous bone), and ***[[osteophyte]]*** (**osteon** + **phyton** → bone growth). The prefix **[[peri-]]** is highly productive in medical terminology, appearing in ***[[pericarditis]]*** (inflammation around the heart), ***[[peritonitis]]*** (inflammation of the peritoneum), ***[[perineum]]*** (area around the anus), ***[[periodontitis]]*** (inflammation around the tooth), and ***[[peripheral]]*** (pertaining to the outer boundary).

>[!success]+ ### 🔀 ALIASES / ALTERNATE TERMS
>
> - **[[Periosteitis]]** _(alternative spelling; identical meaning)_
> - **Periostitic** _(adjective form — "periostitic changes," "periostitic bone reaction")_
> - **[[Periostalgia]]** _(clinical synonym denoting pain in the periosteum)_
> - **Shin splints** _(lay term for medial tibial stress syndrome, a common form of periostitis)_[reference:9]
> - **Medial tibial stress syndrome** _(clinical descriptor for exercise-induced periostitis of the tibia)_
> - **Orbital periostitis** _(anatomic subtype — H05.03-; inflammation of periosteum of the orbit)_
> - **Dental periostitis** _(anatomic subtype — inflammation of the periosteum of the jaw or maxilla, usually bacterial)_
> - **Simple periostitis** _(etiological subtype — aseptic, often post-traumatic)_
> - **Fibrous periostitis** _(etiological subtype — chronic with thickening of the periosteum by connective tissue)_
> - **Purulent periostitis** _(etiological subtype — suppurative form due to infection)_
> - **Ossifying periostitis** _(etiological subtype — with new bone formation)_
> - **Syphilitic periostitis** _(etiological subtype — due to syphilis infection; A51.4+)_
> - **Tuberculous periostitis** _(etiological subtype — due to tuberculosis)_

>[!danger]+ ### 🔗 RELATED TERMS
>
> - **[[Osteomyelitis]]** — [[inflammation]] of bone and bone marrow; often co-occurs with or follows periostitis; distinguished by involvement of the medullary cavity
> - **[[osteitis]]** — inflammation of bone tissue itself; periostitis is limited to the periosteum, while osteitis involves the bone proper
> - **[[Periosteum]]** — the dense fibrous membrane whose inflammation defines periostitis; provides nutrient supply and osteogenic potential
> - **[[Osteophyte]]** — bony outgrowth; can result from chronic ossifying periostitis
> - **Shin splints** — common clinical presentation of periostitis; exercise-induced traction periostitis of the tibia
> - **[[Periodontitis]]** — inflammation of the tissues around the tooth; shares the [[peri-]] root but affects dental supporting structures
> - **[[Pericarditis]]** — inflammation of the pericardium; shares the [[peri-]] prefix denoting surrounding structure
> - **Bone scan** — diagnostic procedure; radionuclide imaging can detect increased uptake in periostitis
> - **MRI** — diagnostic procedure; can show periosteal reaction and soft tissue changes
> - **X-ray** — diagnostic procedure; can show periosteal reaction, bone thickening, or new bone formation

> > ⚠️ **Coding Note:** For **orbital periostitis** (H05.03-), laterality must be specified — **right, left, bilateral, or unspecified**. For infectious **[[periostitis]]**, the underlying infection should be coded first, with the periostitis code as a secondary manifestation (**e.g., A51.4+ with [[M90.10]]* for secondary syphilitic periostitis**). A commonly missed code on inpatient profee claims is **[[M86.9]]** for **[[periostitis]]** without **[[osteomyelitis]]** — documentation triggers such as "**periosteal reaction**," "**bone pain with tenderness**," or "**shin splints**" should prompt a query. For **Medicare** and most payers, bone scans (**78300-78306**) and MRI (**73200, 73700**) may require prior authorization; confirm medical necessity with documented symptoms and failed conservative therapy. When coding yaws-related **[[periostitis]]**, use **[[A66.6]]** which includes yaws **[[periostitis]]** (**hypertrophic**).
