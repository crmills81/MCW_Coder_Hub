>[!Note] DEFINITION of [[-tics]]
>**[[-tics]]** (and its variant **[[-ics]]**) is a Greek-derived compound substantive suffix in medical and scientific nomenclature that designates a **systematic science, clinical discipline, practical art, or body of treatment principles**. It transforms verbal or adjectival roots into nouns representing organized fields of medical study, clinical management, or technical application (e.g., *genetics* representing the science of heredity, *therapeutics* denoting the systematic treatment of disease, *prosthetics* referring to the surgical and biomechanical specialty of artificial limb replacement, and *bariatrics* describing the medical and surgical management of obesity). Unlike **[[-logy]]**, which primarily emphasizes the theoretical discourse, doctrine, or academic study of a subject, **[[-tics]]** frequently implies practical, procedural, clinical, or technological execution and systematic application. In 2026 medical coding and health systems navigation, clinical disciplines ending in `-tics` delineate specialized physician service categories, specialized provider credentials, diagnostic genetic panels, DMEPOS orthotic/prosthetic billing schedules, and dedicated multidisciplinary care management programs.

>[!info]+ ETYMOLOGY of [[-tics]]
>#greek
>
>|Component|Origin|Meaning|
>|---|---|---|
>|**[[-ic]]** / **[[-tic]]**|[Greek] _-ikos_ (-ικός) / _-tikos_ (-τικός)|"**pertaining to**," "**characteristic of**," or "**relating to**" — adjective-forming suffix indicating belonging or function.|
>|**-s** / **-a**|[Greek/Latin] _-ta_ (-τα) / _-tika_ (-τικά) via Latin _-tica_|"**matters concerning**," "**things relating to**," or "**doctrines of**" — neuter plural inflection substantivizing the adjective into an organized field or body of knowledge.|
>
> The suffix developed from classical Greek neuter plural adjectives (such as *ta therapevtiká*, literally "matters pertaining to healing/curing," and *ta genetiká*, "matters pertaining to origins/generation"), which passed into Medieval Latin as *therapeutica* and *genetica*. During the **16th through 19th centuries**, Renaissance and Enlightenment scholars rendered these into French as *-ique* and English as *-ics* or *-tics*, establishing the standard suffix for formal sciences (*physics*, *mathematics*, *optics*) and medical disciplines (*obstetrics*, *therapeutics*, *genetics*). The element is closely related to **[[-ic]]** (the adjectival form, e.g., *therapeutic*, *genetic*, *orthotic*), **[[-ist]]** / **[[-ician]]** (the specialist practitioner, e.g., *geneticist*, *obstetrician*, *orthotist*), and **[[-logy]]** (the theoretical scientific study).

> [!danger]+ 🔗 RELATED TERMS
> 
> - **[[-logy]]** — the study or academic science of a subject (e.g., [[cardiology]], [[neurology]]); while `-logy` denotes theoretical inquiry, `[[-tics]]` emphasizes practical application, methodology, and systematic intervention.
> - **[[-ic]]** — the underlying adjectival suffix meaning "pertaining to" (e.g., *orthotic*, *bariatric*, *prophylactic*).
> - **[[-ist]]** — the person or clinician who practices a discipline ending in `-tics` (e.g., *geneticist*, *orthotist*, *prosthetist*).
> - **[[therapeutics]]** — the branch of medicine and pharmacology concerned with the remedial treatment and healing of diseases (incorporates pharmacotherapy, psychotherapy, physical modalities).
> - **[[diagnostics]]** — the science and methods of identifying the nature, cause, and extent of a disease through clinical evaluation, laboratory assays, and imaging.
> - **[[genetics]]** — the biological and medical science of heredity, gene variation, and molecular pathology in living organisms (evaluated via CPT [[81161]]–[[81479]]).
> - **[[pharmacogenetics]]** — the clinical study of how genetic variation influences individual patient responses to pharmaceutical medications.
> - **[[prosthetics]]** — the specialized branch of surgery, biomechanics, and rehabilitation dealing with the design, fabrication, and fitting of artificial limbs or organs (DMEPOS L-codes).
> - **[[orthotics]]** — the medical discipline dedicated to designing and fitting external mechanical appliances (braces, splints) to support or correct skeletal deformities.
> - **[[bariatrics]]** — the field of medicine and surgery dealing with the causes, prevention, and treatment of obesity and related metabolic disorders (e.g., CPT [[43644]], [[43775]]).
> - **[[geriatrics]]** — the clinical specialty dedicated to the comprehensive healthcare, polypharmacy management, and functional preservation of elderly patients.
> - **[[obstetrics]]** — the surgical and medical specialty concerned with pregnancy, prenatal care, labor, delivery, and postpartum management (CPT [[59400]], [[59510]]).
> - **[[theranostics]]** — emerging medical specialty combining targeted diagnostic imaging with radionuclide therapy (e.g., PSMA PET paired with Lutathera or Pluvicto).
> - **[[prophylactics]]** — measures, interventions, or medications designed to prevent the development or spread of disease.

> > ⚠️ **Coding Note:** In 2026 medical coding, specialty disciplines ending in `[[-tics]]` carry strict coverage, documentation, and modifier requirements:
> > 1. **Genetics & Molecular Diagnostics:** Molecular pathology claims (CPT [[81162]], [[81225]]) require valid personal or family history ICD-10-CM codes ([[Z15.01]]–[[Z15.09]], [[Z80.3]]) and documented pre-test counseling ([[96040]]) to meet Medicare Local Coverage Determinations (LCDs) and commercial prior authorization rules.
> > 2. **Prosthetics & Orthotics:** Time-based physical medicine codes (CPT [[97760]], [[97761]], [[97763]]) cannot be billed on the same date of service as DMEPOS supply delivery (HCPCS L-codes) if the fitting is bundled into the device allowance by payer policy. Ensure separate documentation of distinct functional training.
> > 3. **Bariatrics:** Prior authorization for bariatric surgery (CPT [[43644]], [[43775]]) mandates dual documentation of obesity diagnosis ([[E66.01]]) alongside an exact BMI code ([[Z68.35]]–[[Z68.45]]) and proof of documented comorbid conditions (e.g., obstructive sleep apnea, type 2 diabetes, severe osteoarthritis).
> > 4. **Geriatrics / Chronic Care:** For complex geriatric care coordination (CPT [[99483]], [[99490]]), documentation must verify that time thresholds were met and that a comprehensive multidimensional assessment (cognition, functional status, fall risk, and medication reconciliation) was performed.
