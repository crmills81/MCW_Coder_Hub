>[!info]+ ETYMOLOGY of [[amnio-]]
>#greek
>
>|Component|Origin|Meaning|
>|---|---|---|
>|**[[amnio-]]**|Greek _ἀμνίον_ (amnion), from _amnion_ (amnion)|"**amnion**," "**membrane**," "**membrane surrounding the fetus**"|
>
> The word entered English in the **1640s** as *amnion* (**noun**), borrowed from Late Latin ***amnion***, from Greek ***amnion*** — literally "**membrane**." The root *amnion* ("**membrane**") connects [[amnio-]] to the entire **[[embryonic membranes]]**: ***[[amniocentesis]]*** (**amnio- + kentein $\rightarrow$ puncture of the amnion**), ***[[amniotomy]]*** (**amnio- + tome $\rightarrow$ incision into the amnion**), and ***[[amniotic]]*** (**pertaining to the amnion**). The **combining form** [[amnio-]] is highly productive in obstetrics and embryology: e.g., ***[[amniochoreosis]]***, ***[[amniopathy]]***, and ***[[amniochorion]]***.
