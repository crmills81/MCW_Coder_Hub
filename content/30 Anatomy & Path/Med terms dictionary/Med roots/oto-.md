>[!Note] DEFINITION of [[oto-]]
>[[Oto‑]] comes into play as a combining form (*often written **[[ot/o-]]** when broken out in med‑term class*) to build terms for **ear anatomy, pathology, and procedures**. In practice you’ll see it across specialties: [[otology]] ([[ear]] subspecialty of [[ENT]]), [[otitis media]]/externa (*ear infections*), [[ototoxic]] (*ear‑damaging drugs*), and [[otoplasty]] (*plastic surgery of the external ear*).
>
>|Term|Breakdown|Meaning|
|---|---|---|
|[[Otology]]|[[oto‑]] “*ear*” + [[‑logy]] “study of”|Medical study and care of the ear.[rxlist+1](https://www.rxlist.com/oto-/definition.htm)|
|[[Otitis]] (e.g., otitis media)|[[oto‑]] “*ear*” + [[‑itis]] “*inflammation*”|Inflammation/infection of the ear.[wikipedia+1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_medical_roots_and_affixes)|
|[[Otoscope]]|[[ot/o]] “*ear*” + [[‑scope]] “*instrument for viewing*”|Instrument used to examine the ear canal and [[tympanic]] membrane.[mastermedicalterms](https://mastermedicalterms.com/snax_item/ot-o/)​|
|[[Otoplasty]]|[[oto‑]] “*ear*” + [[‑plasty]] “*surgical repair*”|Plastic surgery to reshape or repair the external ear.[rxlist+1](https://www.rxlist.com/oto-/definition.htm)|
|[[Otosclerosis]]|[[ot/o]] “*ear*” + [[scler/o-]] “*hardening*” + [[‑osis]] “condition”|Abnormal bone hardening of the middle ear causing conductive hearing loss.[studocu+1](https://www.studocu.com/en-au/messages/question/11259173/otosclerosis-prefix-meaning-root-meaning-combining-vowel-combining-form-suffix-meaning)|
_____
>[!info]+ ETYMOLOGY of [[oto-]]
> #greek “[[Oto‑]]” derives from Ancient Greek _οὖς_ (_ous_), genitive _ὠτός_ (_ōtós_), meaning “**ear**,” with the combining stem _ōt-_ that gives us **[[ot‑]]/[[oto‑]]** in scientific and medical vocabulary. It parallels the Latin‑derived **[[aur‑]]/[[auri‑**]] (as in aural) which also means “ear,” but [[oto‑]] is the standard Greek form in most medical terminology lists.
