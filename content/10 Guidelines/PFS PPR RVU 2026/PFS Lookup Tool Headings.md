### Practice Expense (PE) RVUs

Practice Expense RVUs cover the overhead costs of running a practice or clinic, such as rent, equipment, clinical supplies, and non-physician staff wages. CMS pays different amounts depending on the setting where the service takes place.

* **Fully Implemented Non-FAC PE RVU:** The final, intended Practice Expense Relative Value Unit for a service performed in a non-facility setting (like an independent physician's office) once any multi-year CMS phase-in adjustments are entirely complete.
* **Transitioned Non-FAC PE RVU:** The current, active intermediate Practice Expense value for a non-facility setting. When CMS introduces significant valuation changes to a code, they often phase those changes in over several years to prevent sudden payment shocks. If no phase-in is actively happening for a code, this number simply matches the Fully Implemented value.
* **Fully Implemented Facility PE RVU:** The final, intended Practice Expense RVU for a service performed in a facility setting (like an inpatient hospital, ambulatory surgical center, or skilled nursing facility) after all transition periods have ended.
* **Transitioned Facility PE RVU:** The current, blended Practice Expense value for a facility setting during an active multi-year phase-in period.

### Malpractice (MP) RVUs

* **MP RVU (Malpractice RVU):** The specific portion of the total RVU designated to reflect the relative cost of professional liability (malpractice) insurance associated with providing that exact procedure. Higher-risk procedures (like major surgeries) carry significantly higher MP RVUs than low-risk procedures (like office visits).

### OPPS Cap Indicators (Informational)

Under the Deficit Reduction Act, CMS caps the payment for the technical component of certain diagnostic imaging services (like X-rays and MRIs). The law states that the MPFS payment cannot exceed what a hospital outpatient department would be paid for the exact same service under the Outpatient Prospective Payment System (OPPS). These fields exist in the lookup tool to show the math behind that cap.

* **Used for OPPS PMT AMT (PE Used for OPPS Payment Amount):** The specific Practice Expense RVU value utilized strictly for calculating the OPPS imaging cap comparison. It is informational only.
* **MP used for OPPS PMT AMT:** The specific Malpractice RVU value applied alongside the PE RVU to calculate the final OPPS payment limit. This shows exactly how much of the OPPS capped amount is attributed to malpractice costs.
