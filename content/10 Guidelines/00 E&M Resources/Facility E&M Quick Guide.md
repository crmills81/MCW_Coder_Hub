# 🏥 Facility E/M Quick Guide

## 🎯 Purpose
Quick reference for facility E/M leveling (not provider E/M).

---

# 🧩 Key Differences (Facility vs. Professional)
- Facility E/M is based on **resource utilization**
- NOT based on MDM
- Driven by:
  - Nursing intensity
  - Monitoring
  - Interventions
  - Equipment use
  - Time in department

---

# 🛠 Common Facility E/M Drivers
- IV meds
- Cardiac monitoring
- Frequent vitals
- Complex nursing care
- Multiple interventions

---

# 🧠 Documentation Tips
- Capture **nursing workload**
- Document **monitoring frequency**
- Note **interventions** clearly
- Link resource use to **patient condition**
