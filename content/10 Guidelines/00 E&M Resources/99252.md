# CPT Code 99252 Documentation & Billing Guide

**Last Updated:** February 2026  
**Status:** 2025 Medicare Fee Schedule Compliant  

---
### **CPT 99252  - Inpatient Consultation (Level 2)**

**Description & Explanation:** Used for an inpatient consultation requiring a medically appropriate history/exam and **Straightforward** MDM.

>[!warning]
>**Important Payer Note:** CPT codes **99252 -99255 (Inpatient Consultations)** are **not recognized by Medicare** (and many private payers aligning with CMS). For Medicare patients, you must typically bill these as Initial (9922x) or Subsequent (9923x) Hospital Care, even if you are a consultant.

- **Definition of Consultation:** Requires a specific request from another physician/appropriate source to recommend care for a specific condition.
    
- **MDM:** Straightforward.
    
- **Time:** 20 minutes (total time on date of encounter).
    
- **Note:** 99251 was deleted; 99252 is now the lowest level inpatient consult code.

**Clinical Examples:**

1. **Dermatology Consult:** Patient admitted for cellulitis has a separate, unrelated benign rash on the arm. Dermatologist consulted to evaluate. Diagnosis: Seborrheic keratosis (minor, stable).
    
2. **Podiatry Consult:** Diabetic patient admitted for pneumonia. Podiatry asked to trim mycotic nails (routine).
