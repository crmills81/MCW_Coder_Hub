# CPT Code 99223 Documentation & Billing Guide

**Last Updated:** February 2026  
**Status:** 2025 Medicare Fee Schedule Compliant  

### **CPT Code(s):**

- **99223  - Initial Hospital Inpatient or Observation Care:** Initial hospital inpatient or observation care, per day, for the evaluation and management of a patient, which requires a medically appropriate history and/or examination and **high level of medical decision making**. When using total time on the date of the encounter for code selection, **75 minutes** must be met or exceeded.
    

### **CPT/HCPCS Code Details:**

- **Code:** 99223
    
- **Long Title:** Initial hospital inpatient or observation care, per day, for the evaluation and management of a patient, which requires a medically appropriate history and/or examination and high level of medical decision making. When using total time on the date of the encounter for code selection, 75 minutes must be met or exceeded.
    
- **Explanation:** This code is used for the **first** hospital inpatient or observation encounter with the patient by the admitting physician (or other qualified healthcare professional). It represents the highest level of initial care (Level 3), typically for patients with severe, life-threatening, or very complex conditions.
    
    - **Selection Criteria:** Requires a **High** level of Medical Decision Making (MDM) **OR** at least **75 minutes** of total time spent by the provider on the date of the encounter (including non-face-to-face time).
        
- **WRVU:** 3.50
    
- **Global Period:** XXX (The global concept does not apply to this code).
    
- **Assistant Payable:** No (Status Indicator 9: Concept does not apply).
    
    - _Note: While a surgical assistant is not payable, this service may be billed as a "split/shared" visit between a physician and a Non-Physician Practitioner (NPP) if requirements are met._
        

### **Bundling & NCCI Edits:**

- **Inclusives (Included Services):**
    
    - All evaluation and management (E/M) services provided by the same physician (or same specialty/same group) on the same date of service.
        
    - Minor procedures (0 or 10-day global period) performed on the same day are generally included unless a significant, separately identifiable E/M service is documented (Modifier 25).
        
    - Interpretation of diagnostic tests performed by the same provider may be bundled if the professional component is reported separately.
        
- **Mutually Exclusives (Cannot be billed together):**
    
    - Other **Initial** Hospital Care codes ([[10 Guidelines/00 E&M Resources/99221]], [[50 Medical Coding/CPT Codes/99222|99222]]) on the same date.
        
    - **Subsequent** Hospital Care codes ([[99231]]-[[99233]]) on the same date (by the same provider).
        
    - Emergency Department visits ([[99281]]-[[99285]]) on the same date are generally bundled into the initial inpatient care if the patient is admitted.
        
    - Observation care codes (99234-99236) if billed for the same admission (refer to "Same Day Admission and Discharge" rules).
        
- **Exclusives:**
    
    - Telehealth facility fees (Q3014) are generally not applicable to the provider's professional claim for this code.
        

### **HCC Information:**

- **N/A:** CPT codes generally do not map directly to Hierarchical Condition Categories (HCCs) for risk adjustment; HCCs are primarily derived from ICD-10-CM diagnosis codes.

### **Assistant Pay Information: CPT 99223**

- **Assistant at Surgery (Modifiers 80, 81, 82, AS):** **Not Payable.**
    
    - **Indicator:** 9 (Concept does not apply).
        
    - **Explanation:** This is an Evaluation and Management (E/M) service, not a surgical procedure. Therefore, it is not eligible for reimbursement for a surgical assistant.
        
- **Split/Shared Visits:** **Payable.**
    
    - This code **can** be billed as a split/shared visit when performed jointly by a Physician and a Non-Physician Practitioner (NPP) in the same group.
        
    - **Current Rule:** The provider who performs the **substantive portion** of the visit (more than half of the total time or the complete MDM) bills the service.
