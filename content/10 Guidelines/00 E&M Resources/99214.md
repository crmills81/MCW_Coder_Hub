## 99214 vs. 99213 Quick Comparison
The primary leap from [[99213]] to [[99214]] is the transition from managing single/stable problems to managing multiple chronic issues or complex acute symptoms. [247medicalbillingservices](https://www.247medicalbillingservices.com/blog/cpt-99214-vs-99213-key-differences-in-office-visit-coding-for-2025)

## <font color="#8064a2">CPT Code 99214 Definition: 30-39 Minute Established Patient Visits</font>

**CPT code 99214** is one of the most valuable codes in outpatient settings, representing established patient visits with moderate complexity medical decision-making. This E/M (Evaluation and Management) code is essential for healthcare providers who need to accurately bill for comprehensive patient care.

**Key Requirements for 99214:**

- **Established patient only** (new patients use 99204)
- **30-39 minutes total time** spent on the encounter
- **Moderate complexity medical decision-making**
- **Detailed history and comprehensive examination**
- **Office or outpatient setting**

## <font color="#8064a2">**CPT 99214 Time Requirements: 2025 Guidelines**</font>

Time documentation is **critical** for CPT 99214 billing success. The 2025 guidelines emphasize precise time tracking for all E/M services.

### Exact Time Requirements:

- **Minimum time:** 30 minutes
- **Maximum time:** 39 minutes
- **Time includes:** Face-to-face interaction, record review, counseling, care coordination
- **Documentation required:** Start/end times or total time spent

### Time-Based vs. Medical Decision Making

Providers can bill 99214 based on either:

1. **Time-based billing:** 30-39 minutes of total encounter time
2. **MDM-based billing:** Moderate complexity medical decision-making

**Pro Tip:** Use whichever method results in appropriate code selection for your specific encounter.
