# 🏥 Observation vs Inpatient Guide

## 🎯 Purpose
Clear criteria for determining observation vs inpatient status.

---

# 🧩 Observation Status
Use when:
- Condition expected to resolve in < 24–48 hours
- Diagnostic uncertainty
- Short‑term monitoring needed
- Treatment is low‑intensity

Examples:
- Chest pain rule‑out
- Mild dehydration
- Nausea/vomiting

---

# 🧩 Inpatient Status
Use when:
- High‑intensity treatment required
- Risk of deterioration
- Multi‑day stay expected
- Severe exacerbation of chronic illness

Examples:
- Sepsis
- Acute respiratory failure
- Stroke symptoms
- Uncontrolled diabetes

---

# 🧠 Documentation Tips
- State **why** inpatient care is needed  
- Link symptoms → risk → treatment intensity  
- Avoid vague terms (“monitoring,” “weakness”)
