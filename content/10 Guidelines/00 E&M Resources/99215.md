## Definitions and Requirements

The **long definition** describes an office or other outpatient visit for the evaluation and management of an established patient, requiring a medically appropriate history/examination and **High Level of Medical Decision Making (MDM)**. When using time for code selection, at least 40 minutes must be spent on the date of the encounter.[aapc+2](https://www.aapc.com/codes/cpt-codes/99215)

## Key Requirements for High MDM

Documentation must satisfy at least two of these three high-level elements:[brellium+1](https://brellium.com/resources/articles/99215-cpt-code-documentation-requirements-compliance-guide)

- **High Complexity of Problems**: One or more chronic illnesses with severe exacerbation or progression, or an acute/chronic illness/injury that poses a threat to life or bodily function.[statmedical+1](https://www.statmedical.net/understanding-cpt-code-99215-a-complete-guide-for-medical-billing-professionals)
    
- **Extensive Data**: Requires extensive review or analysis of data, such as independent interpretation of tests or discussion of management with an external provider.[brellium+1](https://brellium.com/resources/articles/99215-cpt-code-documentation-requirements-compliance-guide)
    
- **High Risk**: High risk of morbidity from treatment or testing, such as decisions for **hospitalization**, emergency major surgery, or monitoring **toxic drugs**.[statmedical+1](https://www.statmedical.net/understanding-cpt-code-99215-a-complete-guide-for-medical-billing-professionals)
    
## Mutually Exclusive and Common Modifiers

[[99215]] is subject to National Correct Coding Initiative (NCCI) edits and cannot be reported with other outpatient E/M codes for the same patient on the same day by the same provider.[cms+1](https://www.cms.gov/files/document/chapter11cptcodes90000-99999final11.pdf)

- **Mutually Exclusive Codes**: Includes other E/M levels (99211-99214), initial psychiatric evaluations (90791-90792), and certain diagnostic imaging if performed by the same practitioner.[bluecrossnc+1](https://www.bluecrossnc.com/providers/policies-guidelines-codes/commercial/reimbursement/updates/evaluation-and-management-services)
    
- **[[Modifier 25]]**: Used to report 99215 as a "significant, separately identifiable" service when performed on the same day as a minor procedure or a preventive medicine visit.[ama-assn+1](https://www.ama-assn.org/practice-management/cpt/can-physicians-bill-both-preventive-and-em-services-same-visit)
    
- **[[Modifier 24]]**: Essential when 99215 is performed during the global period of a previous surgery but is for a completely unrelated problem.[medsolercm+1](https://medsolercm.com/blog/modifier-24-guide-healthcare-providers)
    
- **Telehealth Modifiers**: Use [[Modifier 95]] (synchronous audio-video) or [[Modifier 93]] (audio-only) depending on the platform used and payer requirements.[bellmedex+1](https://bellmedex.com/what-is-cpt-code-99214-in-medical-coding/)
    
## Common Use Cases

- **Severe Exacerbation**: A patient with acute congestive heart failure (CHF) exacerbation requiring high-dose diuretic adjustment and discussion of potential hospitalization.[trytwofold+1](https://www.trytwofold.com/medical-codes/99215-cpt-code)
    
- **Life-Threatening Symptoms**: An established patient presenting with new-onset chest pain and shortness of breath requiring emergency evaluation and stabilization.[brellium+1](https://brellium.com/resources/articles/99215-cpt-code-documentation-requirements-compliance-guide)
    
- **Complex Comorbidities**: Managing a patient with multiple severe, unstable chronic conditions (e.g., [[end-stage renal disease]] and advanced COPD) where treatment decisions carry high risk.[statmedical+1](https://www.statmedical.net/understanding-cpt-code-99215-a-complete-guide-for-medical-billing-professionals)

## Key Requirements for 99215

To bill 99215, documentation must support "High" MDM by meeting at least two of the following three elements:[brellium+1](https://brellium.com/resources/articles/99215-cpt-code-documentation-requirements-compliance-guide)

- **Complexity of Problems**: One or more chronic illnesses with severe exacerbation, progression, or side effects of treatment; or one acute or chronic illness/injury that poses a threat to life or bodily function (e.g., suspected acute MI, pulmonary embolism, or suicidal ideation).[statmedical+1](https://www.statmedical.net/understanding-cpt-code-99215-a-complete-guide-for-medical-billing-professionals)
    
- **Amount/Complexity of Data**: Extensive data review, such as independent interpretation of tests, discussion of management with an external physician, or reviewing a unique combination of at least three tests or documents.[brellium+1](https://brellium.com/resources/articles/99215-cpt-code-documentation-requirements-compliance-guide)
    
- **Risk of Management**: High risk of morbidity from treatment or testing. Key examples include decisions regarding **hospitalization**, emergency major surgery, or initiating/monitoring **toxic drugs** (e.g., chemotherapy, high-dose anticoagulants).[statmedical+1](https://www.statmedical.net/understanding-cpt-code-99215-a-complete-guide-for-medical-billing-professionals)
