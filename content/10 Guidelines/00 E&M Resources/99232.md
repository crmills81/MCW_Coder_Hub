# CPT Code 99232 Documentation & Billing Guide

**Last Updated:** February 2026  
**Status:** 2025 Medicare Fee Schedule Compliant  

### **CPT 99232  - Subsequent Hospital Care (Level 2)**

**Description & Explanation:** Used for daily hospital rounds for a patient who requires **Moderate** complexity management. This is the most common subsequent hospital care code.

- **MDM:** Moderate.
    
- **Time:** 35 minutes (total time on date of encounter).
    
- **Status:** Patient usually has an exacerbation of a chronic condition, a new problem, or side effects of treatment.
    

**Code Tree:**

- Evaluation and Management
    
    - Hospital Inpatient or Observation Care
        
        - Subsequent Care
            
            - **99232: Subsequent hospital care, moderate MDM or 35 min**
                

**Technical Data:**

- **WRVU (2025):** ~1.39
    
- **Global Period:** XXX
    
- **Assistant Payable:** No
    
- **HCC Information:** N/A
    

**Bundling & Edits:**

- **Exclusive:** Only one subsequent care code per specialty per day.
    

**Clinical Examples:**

1. **Pneumonia with slow response:** Patient with pneumonia still on oxygen, spiking low-grade fevers. Antibiotics adjusted or workup expanded.
    
2. **Heart Failure Management:** Patient undergoing diuresis. Electrolytes reviewed and repleted. Meds titrated based on weight/output.
