## 🎯 CPT Code 99231 Documentation & Billing Guide

**Last Updated:** February 2026  
**Status:** 2025 Medicare Fee Schedule Compliant  

### **CPT 99231  - Subsequent Hospital Care (Level 1)**

**Description & Explanation:** Used for daily hospital rounds (follow-up) for a patient who is improving or stable. This is the lowest level of subsequent care.

- Requires a medically appropriate history/exam.
    
- **MDM:** Straightforward or Low.
    
- **Time:** 25 minutes (total time on date of encounter).
    
- **Status:** Patient is typically stable, recovering, or has a resolving problem.
    

**Code Tree:**

- Evaluation and Management
    
    - Hospital Inpatient or Observation Care
        
        - Subsequent Care
            
            - **99231: Subsequent hospital care, low MDM or 25 min**
                

**Technical Data:**

- **WRVU (2025):** ~0.80
    
- **Global Period:** XXX
    
- **Assistant Payable:** No
    
- **HCC Information:** N/A
    

**Bundling & Edits:**

- **Exclusive:** Cannot be billed on the same day as Initial Care (9922x) or Discharge (99238/9) by the same provider.
    

**Clinical Examples:**

1. **Stable Recovery:** Day 3 of admission for cellulitis. Erythema receding, patient afebrile. IV antibiotics continued.
    
2. **Awaiting Placement:** Patient medically cleared for discharge but waiting for SNF bed. Vitals stable, no new complaints.
