### **CPT 99292 - Critical Care (Add-on)**

**Description & Explanation:** The "add-on" code for critical care time beyond the first 74 minutes.

- **Usage:** Use **in addition** to **99291**.
    
- **Time:** Each additional **30 minutes** (must pass 15 mins into the next block).
    
    - 75 - 104 min = **99291** + **99292** x1
        
    - 105 - 134 min = **99291** + **99292** x2
        

**Code Tree:**

- Evaluation and Management
    
    - Critical Care Services
        
        - **99292: Critical care, each additional 30 minutes**
            

**Technical Data:**

- **WRVU (2025):** ~2.25
    
- **Global Period:** ZZZ (Add-on code; follows the global period of the primary code, usually XXX for Crit Care).
    
- **Assistant Payable:** No
    

**Clinical Examples:**

1. **Prolonged Resuscitation:** Physician spends 100 minutes total stabilizing a trauma patient with multiple bleeds and tension pneumothorax. Bill 99291 + 99292 (x1).
    
2. **Multi-System Failure:** Physician spends 4 hours (240 mins) continuously managing a post-arrest patient. Bill 99291 + 99292 (x5).
