## 🎯 CPT Code 99221 Documentation & Billing Guide

**Last Updated:** February 2026  
**Status:** 2025 Medicare Fee Schedule Compliant  

### **CPT 99221  - Initial Hospital Care (Level 1)**

**Description & Explanation:** Used for the **first** hospital inpatient or observation encounter by the admitting physician. It represents the lowest level of initial care.

- Requires a medically appropriate history/exam.
    
- **MDM:** Straightforward or Low.
    
- **Time:** 40 minutes (total time on date of encounter).
    

**Code Tree:**

- Evaluation and Management
    
    - Hospital Inpatient or Observation Care
        
        - Initial Care
            
            - **99221: Initial hospital care, low MDM or 40 min**
                

**Technical Data:**

- **WRVU (2025):** ~1.92
    
- **Global Period:** XXX
    
- **Assistant Payable:** No
    
- **HCC Information:** N/A
    

**Bundling & Edits:**

- **Included:** All E/M services by the same provider on that date.
    
- **Exclusive:** Subsequent hospital care (99231-99233) on the same date.
    
- **AI/Assistant Note:** Split/Shared visits allowed (Modifier FS).
    

**Clinical Examples:**

1. **Observation for Dehydration:** Patient admitted to observation for IV fluids due to viral gastroenteritis. Condition is stable/not life-threatening.
    
2. **Elective Surgery Admission:** Patient admitted for scheduled surgery (if the surgeon is admitting for medical management distinct from the global surgical package, though rare; usually for medical admissions).
