**Last Updated:** February 2026  
**Status:** 2025 Medicare Fee Schedule Compliant

### **CPT 99253  - Inpatient Consultation (Level 3)**

**Description & Explanation:** Used for an inpatient consultation requiring **Low** complexity MDM. This is the standard "low complexity" consult.

>[!warning]
>**Important Payer Note:** CPT codes **99252 -99255 (Inpatient Consultations)** are **not recognized by Medicare** (and many private payers aligning with CMS). For Medicare patients, you must typically bill these as Initial (9922x) or Subsequent (9923x) Hospital Care, even if you are a consultant.

- **MDM:** Low.
    
- **Time:** 35 minutes.

**Clinical Examples:**

1. **Ortho Consult (Stable):** Patient admitted for syncope found to have a chronic, stable rotator cuff tear. Orthopedics consulted for long-term plan (PT vs surgery later).
    
2. **Psych Consult (Uncomplicated):** Patient admitted for surgery, requesting restart of home anti-depressants. Psychiatrist reviews history, restarts meds, no acute safety risk.
