### **CPT 99238  - Hospital Discharge Management (< 30 min)**

**Description & Explanation:** Used to report the total duration of time spent by the physician on the date of discharge for a patient who was admitted on a **previous** date.

- **Time:** **30 minutes or less**.
    
- **Includes:** Final exam, discussion of hospital stay, instructions for continuing care to all relevant caregivers, and preparation of discharge records (prescriptions, referral forms, discharge summary).
    
- **Face-to-Face:** Does not require continuous face-to-face time; reports total time spent on the discharge date.

- **WRVU (2025):** ~2.42
    
- **Global Period:** XXX
    
- **Assistant Payable:** No
    

**Bundling & Edits:**

- **Exclusive:** Cannot be billed if the patient was admitted on the _same_ day (use 99234-99236).
    
- **Exclusive:** Only the attending physician (or physician of record) typically bills the discharge code. Consultants bill a subsequent hospital visit (99231-99233) on the discharge date if they see the patient.
    

**Clinical Examples:**

1. **Routine Discharge:** Patient recovering from pneumonia. Brief check, scripts written, instructions given. Total time 20 minutes.
    
2. **Post-Op Discharge:** Surgeon clears patient home day 2 after surgery (if not in global period, or if billing for complication).
