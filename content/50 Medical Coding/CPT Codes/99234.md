### **CPT 99234  - Observation/Inpatient Care, Same Day (Low)**

**Description & Explanation:** This code is used when a patient is admitted to inpatient or observation status and **discharged on the same calendar date**.

- **Requirement:** Requires **two** distinct encounters (one for admission, one for discharge) by the same provider/group.
    
- **Duration Rule:** Most payers (including Medicare) require the stay to be **> 8 hours** but less than 24 hours to use this code range. If < 8 hours, use Initial Care codes (99221-99223).
    
- **MDM:** Straightforward or Low.
    
- **Time:** 45 minutes.

**Clinical Examples:**

1. **Elective Procedure Monitoring:** Patient admitted for observation after a liver biopsy due to mild pain, monitored for 9 hours, then discharged stable.
    
2. **Syncope:** Patient admitted for observation following fainting. Cardiac workup (echo/telemetry) is negative. Discharged same evening.
