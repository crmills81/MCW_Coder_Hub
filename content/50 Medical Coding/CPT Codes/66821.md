#  🧬 CPT Code 66821: YAG capsulotomy
>
## 📋 Core Data

- **Code:** **66821**
    
- **Short Description:** **YAG capsulotomy**
    
- **Long Description:** Discission of secondary membranous cataract (opacified posterior lens capsule and/or anterior hyaloid); laser surgery (eg, **YAG laser**) (one or more stages)
    
- **Service Type:** Surgery / [[Ophthalmology]] / Anterior Segment
    
- **Anatomy:** [[Posterior Lens Capsule]] / [[Lens]]
    

---

## 💰 Valuation & Fee Schedule (2025 Estimates)

- **wRVU (Work RVU):** ~3.53
    
- **Global Period:** `090` (90-Day Global Period)
    
    - _Note:_ This is a "major" surgical procedure in terms of global days, meaning routine post-op care is included for 90 days.
        
- **Assistant Payable:** **No** (Statutory restriction; modifiers 80/81/82 are not allowed).
    
- **Bilateral Status:** Unilateral (Report with modifier 50, RT, or LT).

## 🏥 Clinical Context

### Definition

A non-invasive laser procedure used to treat **Posterior Capsule Opacification (PCO)**. PCO occurs when the back (posterior) part of the lens capsule, which holds the artificial intraocular lens (IOL) in place, becomes cloudy after cataract surgery. The YAG laser creates an opening in the cloudy capsule to restore clear vision.

### Equipment Used

- **Nd:YAG Laser** (Neodymium-doped Yttrium Aluminum Garnet)
    
- Slit lamp bio-microscope with laser attachment
    
- Capsulotomy lens (contact lens placed on eye)
    

### Clinical Indications

- **Posterior Capsule Opacification (PCO):** Often called a "secondary cataract."
    
- **Visual complaints:** Glare, halos, or decreased visual acuity that impairs Activities of Daily Living (ADLs).
    
- **Capsular Block Syndrome:** Fluid accumulation behind the **IOL**.
    
- **Contracture:** Capsular phimosis affecting the visual axis.

## 📝 Coding & Billing Guidelines

### 1. Timing & Global Periods (Crucial)

- **The "90-Day" Trap:** Because cataract surgery ([[66984]]/[[66982]]) has a 90-day global period, if a [[YAG capsulotomy]] is required within that window (which is rare but possible), you **must** append **Modifier 79** (Unrelated procedure or service by the same physician during the postoperative period).
    
    - _Why:_ PCO is considered a complication or sequela, but the laser treatment is a distinct, unrelated operative session from the cataract extraction. Without Modifier 79, it will be denied as part of the global package of the cataract surgery.
        

### 2. Modifiers

- **Laterality:** Requires **[[-RT]]**, **[[-LT]]**, or **[[-50]]** (Bilateral).
    
    - _Note:_ Some payers (e.g., Medicare) pay 150% for bilateral (100% first eye, 50% second eye).
        
- **[[-79]]:** If performed within the global period of the cataract surgery.
    
- **[[-KX]]:** May be required by some MACs if the procedure is performed very shortly after cataract surgery (e.g., <90 days) to attest medical necessity requirements are met despite the timing.
    

### 3. Bundling & NCCI

- **Inclusives:**
    
    - Local anesthesia.
        
    - Post-operative inflammation management (within the 90 days).
        
    - One or more stages (if the procedure requires a second session to complete the opening within the global period, you cannot bill 66821 again).
        
- **Exclusives:**
    
    - Do not bill with cataract surgery ([[66984]], [[66982]]) on the same eye, same day.
        
    - Do not bill with other laser procedures on the same structure if considered mutually exclusive components.
        

### 4. Diagnosis Linking

- **Primary Diagnosis:**
    
    - [[H26.491]] Secondary membranous cataract, right eye
        
    - [[H26.492]] Secondary membranous cataract, left eye
        
    - [[H26.493]] Secondary membranous cataract, bilateral
        
- **Secondary Diagnosis (Supportive):**
    
    - [[Z96.1]] Presence of intraocular lens ([[Pseudophakia]]) - _Highly recommended to include this._

## ⚠️ Common Denials / Pitfalls

1. **Missing Modifier 79:** The #1 denial reason. If the patient had cataract surgery 60 days ago, you generally need Modifier 79.
    
2. **Medical Necessity (Acuity):** Many MACs (Medicare Administrative Contractors) have LCDs stating the patient must have vision worse than 20/30 or 20/40 _attributed to the capsule_ to qualify.
    
3. **Medical Necessity (Symptoms):** "Blurry vision" alone may not suffice; documentation must show impairment of ADLs (driving, reading, etc.).
    
4. **Repeat Procedures:** The code definition says "one or more stages." If you didn't open the capsule enough on Tuesday and bring them back Friday to finish, you cannot bill 66821 again.
