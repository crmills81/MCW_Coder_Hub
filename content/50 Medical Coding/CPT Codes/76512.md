# **CPT Code Analysis: 76512**

**CPT/HCPCS Code(s):**

- 76512 - Ophthalmic ultrasound, diagnostic; B-scan with or without superimposed non-quantitative A-scan: This is a diagnostic procedure used to visualize the internal structures of the eye and orbit using high-frequency sound waves.
    
    - **WRVU:** **0.58** (Professional Component) / **2.28** (Global/Total) for 2025.
        
    - **Global Period:** **XXX** (The global surgery concept does not apply to diagnostic tests).
        
    - **Bundling & NCCI Edits:**
        
        - **Inclusives:** Superimposed non-quantitative A-scans are bundled into the B-scan.
            
        - **Mutually Exclusive:** **76512** is bundled with **76513** (Ophthalmic ultrasound, diagnostic; anterior segment ultrasound biomicroscopy); they should not be reported together for the same eye.
            
    - **Explanation:** Used primarily for posterior segment evaluation when the view is obscured (e.g., dense cataract or vitreous hemorrhage) or to evaluate specific lesions.
        

---

**ICD-10-CM Code(s):** The following are the top 3 common diagnosis codes that support medical necessity for this procedure:

1. **H33.2- - Serous retinal detachment:** Used when clinical examination cannot confirm the presence or extent of a detachment due to opaque media.
    
2. **H43.1- - Vitreous hemorrhage:** Often the primary reason for a B-scan to rule out underlying retinal tears or detachments when the retina is not visible.
    
3. **C69.2- - Malignant neoplasm of retina:** Used for the detection, measurement, and monitoring of intraocular tumors or masses.
    

---

### **Clinical Use Case Distinction**

- **76512 (B-Scan):** Appropriate for **posterior/orbital** anatomy, such as the retina, choroid, vitreous chamber, optic nerve head, and orbital soft tissues.
    
- **76513 (UBM):** Appropriate for **anterior segment** anatomy (e.g., iris, ciliary body, anterior chamber angle) typically imaged with high-frequency "immersion" techniques.
