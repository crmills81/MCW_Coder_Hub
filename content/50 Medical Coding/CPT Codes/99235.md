### **CPT 99235  - Observation/Inpatient Care, Same Day (Moderate)**

**Description & Explanation:** Same as 99234 (Admit and Discharge on the same calendar date), but for **Moderate** complexity cases.

- **MDM:** Moderate.
    
- **Time:** 70 minutes.
    
- **Duration Rule:** Stay must typically be > 8 hours.
    

**Code Tree:**

- Evaluation and Management
    
    - Hospital Inpatient or Observation Care
        
        - Same Day Admission and Discharge
            
            - **99235: Admit/Discharge same date, moderate MDM or 70 min**
                

**Technical Data:**

- **WRVU (2025):** ~4.72
    
- **Global Period:** XXX
    
- **Assistant Payable:** No
    

**Clinical Examples:**

1. **Chest Pain Rule Out:** Patient admitted at 8 AM for chest pain. Serial troponins and stress test performed. All negative. Discharged at 8 PM.
    
2. **Asthma Exacerbation:** Patient admitted for aggressive nebulizer treatment and steroids. Clears significantly after 10 hours and discharged home.
