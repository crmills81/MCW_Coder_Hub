# 🧬CPT Code 21320 — Closed Treatment of Nasal Bone Fracture
---
## 📘 Description
>
**CPT [[21320]]** describes the **closed treatment of a nasal bone fracture** performed **with manipulation and stabilization** to realign the nasal bones following trauma.  
No skin incisions are made. The provider **manually reduces** the fracture, often using nasal elevators or instruments inserted through the nostril, then stabilizes it with an **external splint** or **internal packing** to maintain alignment.  
>
This procedure restores nasal form and function and is generally performed with local or general anesthesia.  
>
---
## 🩺 Clinical Context
This code is used following nasal trauma such as:
- Sports injuries  
- Accidental impacts  
- Falls or motor vehicle collisions  
The physician ensures alignment symmetry and patency of the nasal airway after reduction.  
If more complex repair (e.g., open fracture or nasal septum involvement) is required, other codes (e.g., [[21315]], [[21340]]) may be more appropriate.

---
## ⚙️ Procedure Steps

1. Evaluate fracture via physical exam and imaging.  
2. Apply local or general anesthesia.  
3. Manually realign displaced nasal bones using elevators or internal instruments.  
4. Stabilize bones with:
   - Nasal splint  
   - Intranasal packing or external dressing  
5. Advise follow-up for alignment check and breathing assessment.
---
## 💡 Coding Guidelines
- **Includes:**  
  - Manipulation and stabilization  
  - Application of nasal splints or dressings  
  - Follow-up care within the global period
- **Excludes:**  
  - Open treatment of nasal fracture — [[21330]]  
  - Closed treatment without manipulation — [[21310]]  
  - Septoplasty or other nasal reconstructive procedures — [[30520]]
- **Use of Modifiers:**  
  Apply modifiers per case complexity or re-operation circumstances (see YAML for full list).  
- **Global Period:** **<font color="#ff0000">90 days</font>** (major surgery).

## 💬 Coding Examples
>
### Example 1 - Straightforward Closed Reduction  
A patient presents with a displaced nasal bone fracture after a sports injury. The provider manually realigns the bones and applies an external splint.  
**Code:** [[21320]]
### Example 2 - Repeat Procedure Due to Re-displacement  
The same physician repeats the closed reduction two weeks later because the nasal bones have shifted again.  
**Code:** [[21320]][[-76]] (Repeat Procedure by Same Physician)
### Example 3 - Bilateral Nasal Fracture  
Reduction and stabilization required on both nasal sides.  
**Code:** [[21320]][[-50]] (Bilateral Procedure)

## 🧠 Clinical Pearls
- If **septal deviation** is also corrected, report both [[21320]] and [[30520]] only if distinct and documented.  
- Avoid reporting with [[21310]] for the same encounter.  
- Post-service care involving removal of nasal splints is **included** in global service.  
- Avoid unbundling unless clearly supported by operative note.

## 🪜 Summary
[[21320]] is the **primary CPT code** for **closed reduction of nasal fractures with manipulation and stabilization**, reflecting moderate surgical complexity and typically outpatient management.  
It includes both the **manual reduction** of nasal bones and **application of stabilizing devices** such as splints or packing for healing and symmetry restoration.
