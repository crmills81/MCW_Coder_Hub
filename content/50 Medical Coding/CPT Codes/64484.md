# 💉 CPT 64484 — Injection(s), Anesthetic Agent(s) and/or Steroid; Transforaminal Epidural, With Imaging Guidance (Fluoroscopy or CT), Lumbar or Sacral, Each Additional Level

<br>

> [!info] Quick Reference
> **wRVU**: 0.98<sup>1</sup> | **Global Period**: **<font color="#ff0000">ZZZ</font>** | **Assistant Payable**: No (indicator 1)<sup>1</sup> | **Bilateral Indicator:** 1<sup>1</sup>
> **Rule**: CPT **[[64484]]** is an add-on code that is reported only with **[[64483]]** on the same session and side. Its **ZZZ** global means it takes the **global period** of the primary procedure, and its multiple-procedure **indicator of 0** means standard multiple-procedure **reductions** do not apply. **Imaging guidance** is included, so it is not reported separately.<sup>1,2</sup>

---

## 📋 Clinical Description

CPT **[[64484]]** describes **a transforaminal epidural injection of anesthetic agent(s) and/or steroid at each additional [[lumbar]] or sacral level beyond the first**. The needle goes through the neural foramen under **[[fluoroscopy]]** or **CT**, so the medication reaches the **ventral epidural space** around the exiting **nerve root**. The first level is reported with **[[64483]]**, and each added level uses **[[64484]]**.<sup>3</sup>

Compare the siblings. **[[64479]]** and **[[64480]]** are the **[[cervical]]/thoracic equivalents**, with **[[64480]]** as the **add-on** for each additional level. **[[62323]]** is the **interlaminar** (**midline**) approach for **lumbar** or **sacral epidural [[injection]]** with imaging and is a different technique from **transforaminal**. Do **not** report **[[64484]]** with the **interlaminar code** for the same region and session without checking **NCCI** and the **MAC article.**<sup>2</sup>

**This procedure may be performed in the following clinical contexts:**

- **Lumbar radiculopathy from disc herniation** — A second adjacent foramen is injected when imaging and symptoms suggest more than one affected **nerve root**.
- **Foraminal stenosis** — A multilevel **injection** treats radicular pain from narrowing at two adjacent lumbar or **lumbosacral** levels.
- **Diagnostic selective root block** — The additional level is injected to help identify the **pain generator** before surgery.
- **Failed back surgery syndrome** — **Radicular pain** after lumbar surgery is treated at a second level when documentation supports it.

## 🔬 Anatomical & Procedural Considerations


| **Variant** | **Mechanism** | **Key Notes** |
| ----------- | --------------- | --------------- |
| **Lumbar level (L1-L5)** | The needle is advanced under imaging to the neural foramen, usually in the "safe triangle" below the pedicle. Contrast confirms epidural spread before injectate. | Reported as the additional level after [[64483]]. Two-level total per session is the MAC limit.<sup>2</sup> |
| **Sacral level (S1)** | The S1 foramen is accessed through the posterior sacral foramen under fluoroscopy. | Still uses [[64483]]/[[64484]] because the descriptor covers lumbar or sacral. T12-L1 is the exception and is reported with [[64479]].<sup>2</sup> |
| **Bilateral additional level** | Both foramina at the same level are injected. | Report on one line with [[-50]], one unit. Do not report two lines or two units.<sup>2</sup> |

> [!tip] Clinical Pearl
> CPT **[[64484]]** can never stand alone. If the **first level** is not billable or was not performed, the **add-on** has no primary code and will reject. The **MAC articles** also limit the session to two total levels, one spinal region only, so a third level is not payable to **Medicare**.<sup>2</sup>

---

## ✅ Procedure Includes


- **Needle placement** and i**njection of anesthetic and/or steroid** at the additional foramen.
- **Fluoroscopic or CT guidance**, which is bundled in the code descriptor.
- **Contrast injection** to confirm epidural spread, when used.
- **Local anesthesia** and routine pre- and post-procedure monitoring.
- **Image storage** and documentation of level and side.

## ❌ Excludes / Do Not Report Together


| **Code** | **Description** | **Relationship** |
| :-----------------: | ------------- | -------------- |
| **[[64483]]** | Transforaminal epidural, lumbar or sacral, single level | Required primary code. [[64484]] is reported only with it, one unit per additional level, up to the MAC limit. |
| **[[64480]]** | Transforaminal epidural, cervical or thoracic, each additional level | Different spinal region. Only one spinal region may be treated per session under the MAC articles, so do not combine them. |
| **[[62323]]** | Interlaminar epidural, lumbar or sacral, with imaging | Different approach to the same region. Reporting both in one session needs payer review and documentation of separate indications. |
| **[[77003]]** | Fluoroscopic guidance for spine injection | Imaging is included in [[64484]], so [[77003]] is not separately reportable. |

> [!warning] Bundling Alert
> CPT **[[64484]]** has a **ZZZ** global, so it follows the primary procedure and carries no **global period** of its own. **Modifier [[-51]]** is not appended because **add-on codes** are exempt from multiple-procedure reduction. **Audit risk centers** on the third level, billing without **[[6448]]**, reporting **[[77003]]** with it, and missing level-by-level documentation of imaging and contrast.<sup>1,2</sup>

## 🏥 MS‑DRG Considerations

CPT **[[64484]]** is **a professional add-on service and does not drive MS-DRG assignment.** For **inpatient facility reporting**, the injection is captured with **ICD-10-PCS** and normally does not change the **DRG**. **NCD:** I found no **NCD** for transforaminal **ESIs**. **LCD/MAC billing articles:** the **CMS Medicare Coverage Database articles** state that **[[64484]]** is reported with **[[6448]]**, that only one spinal region is treated per session, and that only **two** total levels per session are allowed.<sup>2</sup> **PFS comparison:** **PFS** status **A**, **ZZZ** global, **bilateral indicator 1** and **assistant indicator 1** match the billing-article rules on one-line **[[-50]]** reporting; the **PFS** does not carry the two-level cap, so verify the **MUE** and the **LCD** limit separately.<sup>1,2</sup>

## 📝 Coding Examples

### Example 1
> **Clinical Scenario:**
> A patient with bilateral L4 and L5 radiculopathy gets bilateral fluoroscopic transforaminal ESIs at L4-L5 and L5-S1 in one session. Contrast is documented at both levels and both sides.

| **Field** | **Code** | **Rationale** |
|---|---|---|
| CPT | [[64483]]-[[-50]] | First level, bilateral, one line. |
| CPT 2 | [[64484]]-[[-50]] | Additional level, bilateral, one line and one unit. |
| PDx | [[M54.16]] | Lumbar radiculopathy documented. |

> [!note]
> Compliance note: both levels must be documented, and the level count stays within the LCD two-level limit.

### Example 2
> **Clinical Scenario:**
> A patient with a right L5-S1 disc herniation and right radicular pain has a right TFESI at L5-S1 and S1 in the same session.

| **Field** | **Code** | **Rationale** |
|---|---|---|
| CPT 1 | [[64483]]-[[-RT]] | First level, right side. |
| CPT 2 | [[64484]]-[[-RT]] | Additional level, right side. |
| PDx | [[M51.17]] | Disc disorder with radiculopathy, lumbosacral region. |

> [!warning]
> Compliance warning: do not add [[77003]] for the [[fluoroscopy]], because imaging is included.

### Example 3
> Clinical Scenario:
> A provider injects three left lumbar levels (L3, L4 and L5) in one session for stenosis with radiculopathy.

| **Field** | **Code** | **Rationale** |
|---|---|---|
| CPT | [[64483]]-[[-LT]] | First level only. |
| PDx | [[M48.062]] | Stenosis with neurogenic claudication. |

> [!note] Global period reminder, if applicable
> The MAC articles allow only two levels per session, so the third level is not payable to Medicare. [[64484]] is added once for the second level, and ZZZ means no separate global period.<sup>2</sup>

## ⚠️ Common Coding Pitfalls

- **Pitfall 1**: Billing **[[64484]]** without **[[6448]]**. It is an **add-on and rejects** when reported alone. Always link it to the primary code on the same claim.
- **Pitfall 2**: Reporting multiple units of **[[64484]]** beyond the **LCD** limit. The **MAC articles** allow two total levels per session, so extra levels are not payable. **Cap units** to the policy and document medical necessity.
- **Pitfall 3**: Adding **[[77003]]** for the **[[fluoroscopy]]**. Imaging guidance is part of the **descriptor**. Report only **[[6448]]** and **[[64484]]**.
- **Pitfall 4**: Billing **bilateral** as two lines. **Use** one line with **[[-50]]** and one unit. Two lines can trigger **denials** or **payment** errors.
- **Pitfall 5**: Appending **[[-51]]**. **Add-on codes** are exempt from the multiple procedure **[[reduction]]**, so do not append it.
- **Pitfall 6**: Mixing regions. **Cervical/thoracic** and **[[lumbar]]/sacral** are not billed in the same session under the **MAC articles**. Report one spinal region per session.
