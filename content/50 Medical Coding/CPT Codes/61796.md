# 🧠 CPT 61796 — Stereotactic Radiosurgery (Particle Beam, Gamma Ray, or Linear Accelerator); 1 Simple Cranial Lesion

<br>

> [!info] Quick Reference  
> **wRVU**: 13.58 (2026; verify 2027)<sup>4</sup> | **Global Period**: **<font color="#ff0000">090</font>** | **Assistant Payable**: Verify in PFS | **Bilateral Indicator:** Verify in PFS  
> **Rule**: **CPT [[61796]]** is a physician-only code (**PC/TC indicator 0**) that represents the neurosurgeon's work in **SRS**, so there is no **[[-26]]** or **[[-TC]]** split.<sup>8</sup> The co-surgeon indicator is **0**, which means modifier **[[-62]]** is not payable even when the **neurosurgeon** and **radiation oncologist** work side by side, and each reports their own codes instead.<sup>3</sup> It is reported once per lesion per **course of treatment, not per session or fraction**, and it carries a **90-day global** even though there is no incision.<sup>2</sup>

---

## 📋 Clinical Description
**CPT [[61796]]** describes **stereotactic radiosurgery (SRS) of a single simple cranial lesion using a particle beam, gamma ray** (**e.g., Gamma Knife**), or **linear accelerator source**, where externally generated **ionizing radiation** is focused to destroy the target.<sup>1</sup> A simple **lesion** is generally one that is less than **3.5 cm** in maximum dimension and does not meet the complex **lesion** definition.<sup>1</sup><sup>11</sup> The code covers one **lesion** with one or more **isocenters**, whether delivered in a single fraction or over several sessions, and it includes the full course of treatment for that **lesion**.<sup>2</sup>

The closest siblings are **[[61798]]**, which is the primary code for 1 complex cranial lesion (**3.5 cm or larger, or near critical structures such as the brainstem or optic apparatus, or treating a lesion such as an AVM or schwannoma**), and the add-on **[[61797]]**, which is reported for each additional simple cranial lesion.<sup>2</sup><sup>11</sup> When a frame is placed for the **SRS**, the add-on **[[61800]]** is reported with **[[61796]]** or **[[61798]]**.<sup>11</sup> The radiation oncologist's management is reported separately with **[[77432]]**, and the facility reports delivery with **[[77371]]** or **[[77372]]**.<sup>3</sup>

**This procedure may be performed in the following clinical contexts:**  

- **Solitary brain metastasis** — a single metastatic deposit under **3.5 cm** (**often from lung, breast, melanoma, or renal primaries**) is treated with **SRS** in place of or after **[[resection]]**. The record must document lesion size, number, and the patient's performance status for **Medicare**.<sup>6</sup>  
- **Vestibular schwannoma or other cranial nerve [[tumor]]** — a small **acoustic neuroma** is treated with gamma ray or **LINAC SRS** for tumor control. Check the **[[lesion]]** criteria carefully, because some payers and the **CPT** definitions treat certain **schwannomas** as complex, which would move the service to **[[61798]]**.<sup>11</sup>  
- **[[Meningioma]]** — a small skull base or convexity meningioma that is surgically risky or residual after resection is treated with SRS.  
- **Medically refractory [[trigeminal neuralgia]]** — the **trigeminal nerve root** entry zone is targeted with **[[radiosurgery]]** when medical management has failed. Palmetto limits **[[G50.0]]** coverage to patients who failed documented trials of at least **2 or 3 medications** at maximally tolerated doses.<sup>6</sup>

## 🔬 Anatomical & Procedural Considerations

| **Variant**     | **Mechanism**       | **Key Notes**       |
| ----------- | --------------- | --------------- |
| **Gamma ray (cobalt-60, e.g., Gamma Knife)** | Many cobalt-60 sources converge on the target, usually with a rigid head frame or mask fixation and MRI-based planning. Dose is delivered in a single session to one or more isocenters. | The neurosurgeon reports [[61796]] (plus [[61800]] if a frame is applied), and the facility reports technical delivery with [[77371]]. The radiation oncologist reports [[77432]] for management.<sup>3</sup> |
| **Linear accelerator (LINAC, including robotic systems)** | A gantry-mounted or robotic linear accelerator delivers shaped photon beams from many angles under image guidance, commonly frameless with a thermoplastic mask. | Facility delivery is reported with [[77372]] or, for robotic systems in OPPS, [[G0339]] and [[G0340]]. Fractionated delivery over up to 5 sessions still counts as one course and one unit of [[61796]] per lesion.<sup>2</sup> |
| **Particle beam (proton)** | Protons deposit most of their dose at the Bragg peak, limiting exit dose to surrounding brain tissue. | Particle beam SRS is less common and payer coverage can be narrower. Medicare coverage still depends on the MAC LCD indications and documentation.<sup>5</sup> |

> [!tip] Clinical Pearl  
> Count lesions, not sessions. One primary SRS code (**[[61796]] or [[61798]]**) is reported per course of treatment, with add-on codes for each additional lesion up to a total of 5 lesions.<sup>2</sup> When both simple and complex lesions are treated, report the complex lesion first as the primary code (**[[61798]]**) and use add-ons for the rest.<sup>1</sup> If a new lesion is treated during the **90-day global**, report **[[61796]]** again with modifier **[[-58]]**.<sup>2</sup>

## ✅ Procedure Includes
- **Neurosurgeon participation** in target identification and contouring of the lesion on planning images. This physician work is bundled into **[[61796]]**.  
- **Review and approval of the treatment plan, dose prescription, and isodose distributions** with the radiation oncologist and physicist.  
- **Physician presence and participation** during delivery of the radiosurgery to the single simple lesion.  
- **All sessions for that lesion** within a single course of treatment, including fractionated delivery up to 5 sessions.<sup>2</sup>  
- **Position stabilization, image guidance, and setup verification** as part of the overall **SRS** episode, consistent with how Palmetto describes **SRS** components.<sup>5</sup>  
- **Routine postoperative follow-up** related to the procedure during the **90-day global period**.

> [!warning] Bundling Alert  
> **CPT [[61796]]** carries a **90-day global**, so related follow-up visits are bundled and a **decision-for-surgery E/M** the day before or day of **SRS** needs modifier **[[-57]]**.<sup>4</sup> Because the **co-surgeon indicator** is **0**, do not append **[[-62]]**; the neurosurgeon reports **[[61796]]** (and **[[61800]]** if a frame is applied) while the radiation oncologist reports **[[77432]]** and planning codes.<sup>3</sup> **Audit risk** is concentrated on reporting **[[61796]]** per session or fraction, reporting both **[[61796]]** and **[[61798]]** as primaries, missing documentation of **lesion** size and count, and missing performance status (**KPS/ECOG**) required by the **Palmetto billing articl**e.<sup>2</sup><sup>6</sup> Codes are also subject to **NCCI edits**.<sup>6</sup>

## 🏥 MS‑DRG Considerations
**CPT [[61796]]** is a professional service reported on the **CMS-1500** and does not drive an **MS-DRG**; when **SRS** is delivered during an inpatient stay, the facility reports **ICD-10-PCS D02 codes** and is paid under the **MS-DRG** for the stay, while outpatient technical delivery is paid under **OPPS** (**77371, 77372, G0339, or G0340**).<sup>3</sup> **NCD**: **Medicare** has no **NCD** for **SRS**, so coverage is set at the **MAC** level.<sup>9</sup> LCD (**North Carolina and the rest of JJ/JM**): **Palmetto GBA LCD L39553** (**Radiation Therapies**) and billing article **A59350** cover **SRS** for indications such as primary **CNS** malignancies (**generally under 5 cm as boost or salvage**), **metastatic** and **benign CNS tumors**, and **refractory trigeminal neuralgia**. They require documentation of the primary diagnosis, imaging, number, location, and size of tumors, disease stage, and **KPS** or **ECOG** performance status. 

**ICD-10-CM support** comes from **Group 2**, revision effective 10/01/2026.<sup>5</sup><sup>6</sup> Wellpoint Federal (**formerly NGS**) jurisdictions use **LCD L35076** and **article A56874**, which list **[[61796]]** in **Group 1** for cranial SRS.<sup>7</sup> **PFS** comparison: The PFS lists **[[61796]]** as status A (**active, separately payable**), global **090**, **PC/TC 0, and co-surgeon 0**. This agrees with the **LCDs** treating **[[61796]]** as the surgeon's physician-only **SRS** code, but status **A** only means the code is payable. It does not mean the service is covered; coverage still depends on meeting the **LCD** indications and documentation.<sup>3</sup><sup>4</sup><sup>8</sup>

## 📝 Coding Examples

### Example 1
> **Clinical Scenario:**  
> A 64-year-old with non-small cell lung cancer of the right upper lobe has a single 1.8 cm right frontal brain metastasis. The neurosurgeon and radiation oncologist plan frameless LINAC SRS in a single session. The neurosurgeon contours the lesion, approves the plan, and is present for delivery. KPS of 80 is documented.

| **Field** | **Code** | **Rationale** |
|---|---|---|
| CPT | [[61796]] | Single simple cranial lesion under 3.5 cm treated with SRS. No frame was applied, so 61800 is not reported. |
| PDx | [[C79.31]] | The brain metastasis is the target of treatment and is in Palmetto Group 2.<sup>6</sup> |
| SDx | [[C34.11]] | The documented primary lung site is reported as an additional diagnosis. |

> [!note] 
> The radiation oncologist reports [[77432]] separately, and the facility reports [[77372]]. Performance status and lesion size must be documented for Palmetto.<sup>6</sup>

### Example 2
> **Clinical Scenario:**  
> A patient with metastatic breast cancer has three brain metastases, each under 2 cm, treated with Gamma Knife in one session. A stereotactic head frame is applied by the neurosurgeon. None of the lesions meet the complex definition.

| **Field** | **Code** | **Rationale** |
|---|---|---|
| CPT 1 | [[61796]] | Primary code for the first simple lesion. |
| CPT 2 | [[61797]] x 2 | Add-on for each of the 2 additional simple lesions.<sup>2</sup> |
| CPT 3 | [[61800]] | Add-on for application of the stereotactic headframe.<sup>11</sup> |
| PDx | [[C79.31]] | Brain metastases are the treated condition. |
| SDx | [[C50.912]] | Breast primary; code the specific quadrant if documented. |

> [!warning] 
> Do not report 3 units of [[61796]]. One primary code plus add-ons is reported per course, to a maximum of 5 total lesions.<sup>2</sup>

### Example 3
> Clinical Scenario:  
> Six weeks after SRS for a single brain metastasis, follow-up MRI shows a new, separate 1.2 cm metastasis. The same neurosurgeon treats the new lesion with LINAC SRS.

| **Field** | **Code** | **Rationale** |
|---|---|---|
| CPT | [[61796]]-[[-58]] | A new lesion treated during the 90-day global is reported again with -58 as a staged or related, more extensive procedure.<sup>2</sup> |
| PDx | [[C79.31]] | Same metastatic process as the original treatment. |

> [!note] Global period reminder
> The new procedure starts a new 90-day global period. Routine follow-up visits for the original SRS during the first global period are not separately billable.

## ⚠️ Common Coding Pitfalls

- **Pitfall 1**: Billing [[61796]] per session or fraction. The code covers the full course for one lesion, even if delivered over several sessions, so additional units are not reported for the same lesion.<sup>2</sup>  
- **Pitfall 2**: Reporting multiple units of [[61796]] for multiple lesions. Report one primary code with [[61797]] (simple) or [[61799]] (complex) for each additional lesion, up to 5 total.<sup>2</sup>  
- **Pitfall 3**: Reporting both [[61796]] and [[61798]] as primary codes. Only one primary SRS code is reported per course, and the complex lesion is reported first when both types are treated.<sup>1</sup>  
- **Pitfall 4**: Appending [[-62]] or [[-26]]. The co-surgeon indicator is 0 and the **PC/TC indicator is 0**, so the neurosurgeon reports 61796 and the radiation oncologist reports [[77432]] and planning codes on their own claims.<sup>3</sup><sup>8</sup>  
- **Pitfall 5**: Missing the frame add-on or using the wrong frame code. Report +[[61800]] when the treating neurosurgeon applies the headframe as part of SRS, and reserve [[20660]] for frame-only participation.<sup>3</sup>  
- **Pitfall 6**: Thin medical necessity documentation. Palmetto requires [[lesion]] number, location, and size, disease stage, imaging, and KPS or ECOG; [[trigeminal neuralgia]] requires documented failure of multiple medications.<sup>6</sup>
