### **CPT 99254  - Inpatient Consultation (Level 4)**

**Description & Explanation:** Used for an inpatient consultation requiring **Moderate** complexity MDM. This is a very common code for specialists seeing patients with active exacerbations or new diagnostic problems.

>[!warning]
>**Important Payer Note:** CPT codes **99252 -99255 (Inpatient Consultations)** are **not recognized by Medicare** (and many private payers aligning with CMS). For Medicare patients, you must typically bill these as Initial (9922x) or Subsequent (9923x) Hospital Care, even if you are a consultant.

- **MDM:** Moderate.
    
- **Time:** 55 minutes.

1. **Cardiology Consult (Afib):** Patient admitted for sepsis goes into RVR (Afib). Cardiology consulted. Meds ordered (Beta blockers), echo reviewed, rate control strategy discussed.
    
2. **Nephrology Consult (AKI):** Patient with creatinine bump from 1.0 to 2.5. Nephrology consults to manage fluids and review meds.
