## 🚧 Bundling & NCCI Edits
*(What is INCLUDED in this code?)*

- **Inclusives:** Standard A-scans and B-scans performed on the same structures are generally bundled
- **Mutually Exclusive:** - **[[76513]]** is bundled with **[[76512]]** (B-scan) under NCCI edits. If both are performed, only the more comprehensive service is typically paid unless a distinct clinical reason/different eye is documented (using [[Modifier -59]] or laterality modifiers).
- **Explanation:** Used for high-resolution imaging of structures _behind_ the iris or for precise measurement of the anterior chamber angle when optical methods (like OCT) are limited by opaque media.

# **CPT Code Analysis: 76513**

**ICD-10-CM Code(s):**

Common diagnosis codes supporting medical necessity for an anterior segment ultrasound (UBM) include:

- **[[H40.033]] - Anatomical narrow angle, bilateral:** To visualize the angle structures and confirm closure or plateau iris.
    
- **[[H21.503]] - Unspecified adhesions of iris, bilateral:** To evaluate iris cysts or tumors that cannot be visualized via slit lamp.
    
- **[[H40.60X4]] - [[Glaucoma]] secondary to drugs, unspecified eye, indeterminate stage:** Often used to decide on surgical intervention like peripheral [[iridotomy]].
    

---

**CPT/HCPCS Code(s):**

- **[[76513]] - Ophthalmic ultrasound, diagnostic; anterior segment ultrasound biomicroscopy (UBM), unilateral or bilateral:** This procedure uses high-frequency sound waves (*usually 35-50 MHz*) to produce high-resolution images of the anterior segment of the eye, including the iris, ciliary body, and anterior chamber angle.

### **Clinical Distinction: 76513 vs. 76512**

|**Feature**|**[[76513]] (Anterior Segment/UBM)**|**[[76512]] (Diagnostic [[B-Scan]])**|
|---|---|---|
|**Primary Target**|Anterior chamber, iris, ciliary body.|Retina, [[choroid]], [[vitreous]], optic nerve.|
|**Typical Use Case**|[[Angle-closure glaucoma]], iris tumors.|[[retinal detachment]], [[vitreous hemorrhage]].|
|**Resolution**|Very high (microscopic detail).|**Moderate** (gross anatomical detail).|
