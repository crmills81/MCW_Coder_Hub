# 🧬 CPT 15731 - Muscle, Myocutaneous, or Fasciocutaneous Flap; Head and Neck

## Code Description

**CPT [[15731]]** - **Muscle**, **myocutaneous**, or **fasciocutaneous flap**; head and neck

**This code represents a complex reconstructive surgical procedure involving the transfer of muscle**, [[myocutaneous]] (**muscle and skin**), or [[fasciocutaneous]] (**fascia and skin**) tissue from one area to the head and neck region. The flap remains attached to its original blood supply (**pedicled flap**) or may be completely detached and reconnected microsurgically (**free flap**). This procedure is commonly used for reconstruction following tumor [[resection]], trauma, or congenital defects in the head and neck area.

**Key Components:**
- **Muscle Flap** - Transfer of muscle tissue only
- **Myocutaneous Flap** - Transfer of muscle and overlying skin
- **Fasciocutaneous Flap** - Transfer of fascia and overlying skin
- **Head and Neck Region** - Includes scalp, face, oral cavity, pharynx, larynx, and neck

**Critical Distinction:**
- **[[15731]]** - Head and neck flaps
- **[[15732]]** - Trunk flaps
- **[[15733]]** - Upper extremity flaps
- **[[15734]]** - Lower extremity flaps
- **[[15750]]** - Free muscle, myocutaneous, or fasciocutaneous flap (microvascular)

## Includes

- **Muscle Flap Transfer** - Movement of muscle tissue to head and neck region
- **Myocutaneous Flap** - Combined muscle and skin transfer
- **Fasciocutaneous Flap** - Combined fascia and skin transfer
- **Pedicled Flap** - Flap remains attached to original blood supply
- **Head and Neck Region** - Scalp, face, oral cavity, pharynx, larynx, neck
- **Flap Elevation** - [[dissection]] and mobilization of flap tissue
- **Flap Inset** - Suturing and securing flap to recipient site
- **Donor Site Closure** - Repair of area where flap was harvested
- **Microvascular Anastomosis** - If free flap technique used (may require [[15750]])
- **Nerve Coaptation** - If sensory or motor nerve repair performed
- **Immediate Post-op Care** - Recovery room monitoring

## Documentation Requirements

### Operative Report Must Include:

1. **Preoperative Diagnosis**
2. **Postoperative Diagnosis**
3. **Procedure Performed** (Specify type of flap)
4. **Flap Type** - **CRITICAL** (Muscle, myocutaneous, or fasciocutaneous)
5. **Flap Location** - **CRITICAL** (Must be head and neck region)
6. **Donor Site** - Where flap was harvested from
7. **Recipient Site** - Where flap was placed
8. **Vascular Supply** - Pedicle identification and preservation
9. **Microvascular Anastomosis** - If performed (may require [[15750]])
10. **Flap Dimensions** - Size of flap transferred
11. **Donor Site Closure** - Method of closure
12. **Drains** - Placement and type
13. **Complications** - Bleeding, nerve injury, etc.
14. **Estimated Blood Loss**
15. **Operative Time**
16. **Specimen Sent to Pathology** (if cancer resection)

### Key Phrases to Document:

- "Muscle/myocutaneous/fasciocutaneous flap elevated"
- "Flap transferred to head and neck region"
- "Vascular pedicle identified and preserved"
- "Microvascular anastomosis performed" (if applicable)
- "Donor site closed primarily"
- "Flap inset completed"
- "Hemostasis achieved"
- "Drains placed"
- "Patient tolerated procedure well"

## Medical Necessity

### Indications for [[15731]]:

- **Head and Neck Cancer** - Following tumor resection (oral cavity, pharynx, larynx)
- **Trauma** - Complex facial defects from injury
- **Burns** - Contracture release and coverage
- **Osteoradionecrosis** - Coverage of exposed bone
- **Congenital Defects** - Cleft palate, craniofacial anomalies
- **Infection** - Osteomyelitis requiring debridement and coverage
- **Radiation Necrosis** - Soft tissue coverage after radiation damage
- **Salivary Gland Defects** - Following parotidectomy or submandibular gland excision

### Contraindications:

- **Medically Unstable** - Cannot tolerate major surgery/anesthesia
- **Inadequate Donor Site** - No suitable tissue available
- **Vascular Disease** - Compromised blood supply to flap
- **Active Infection** - Uncontrolled infection at recipient or donor site
- **Metastatic Disease** - Where surgery provides no survival benefit
- **Patient Refusal** - Informed consent not obtained

## Compliance Considerations

1. **Flap Type Documentation** - The most critical audit point. Must clearly document muscle, myocutaneous, or fasciocutaneous flap.
2. **Anatomical Region** - Must be head and neck. Trunk, upper extremity, or lower extremity flaps use different codes ([[15732]], [[15733]], [[15734]]).
3. **Free vs. Pedicled** - If microvascular anastomosis performed, [[15750]] may be more appropriate than [[15731]].
4. **Staged Procedures** - Use modifier [[-58]] for planned staged procedures (**e.g., flap divisio**n) within global period.
5. **Assistant Surgeon** - Document medical necessity for assistant (**complexity, exposure, hemostasis, microsurgical anastomosis**).
6. **External Causes** - If trauma documented, assign codes per ICD-10-CM External Cause Index 2025.<sup>1</sup>
7. **Cancer Staging** - Ensure pathology report aligns with preoperative diagnosis for medical necessity.
8. **Concurrent Procedures** - Verify what is bundled vs. separately billable (debridement, nerve repair, bone graft).

## Clinical Pearls

1. **Flap Viability** - Monitor flap color, temperature, capillary refill, and Doppler signals postoperatively.
2. **Donor Site Morbidity** - Common donor sites include pectoralis major, radial forearm, anterolateral thigh, rectus abdominis.
3. **Microvascular Anastomosis** - If free flap technique used, [[15750]] may be more appropriate than [[15731]].
4. **Airway Management** - Head and neck flaps may compromise airway; tracheostomy may be needed.
5. **Nutrition** - Feeding tube **(gastrostomy or jejunostomy**) often placed for postoperative nutrition.
6. **Radiation History** - Prior radiation increases complication risk; document in operative report.
7. **External Causes** - For trauma cases, document circumstances for correct ICD-10 coding per External Cause Index 2025.<sup>1</sup>
8. **Flap Failure** - Most serious complication; may require return to OR (modifier [[-78]]).
9. **Nerve Coaptation** - Sensory or motor nerve repair may be separately billable.
10. **Multidisciplinary** - Often involves plastic surgery, ENT, oral surgery, and oncology teams.

## Audit Risk Areas

1. **Flap Type Documentation** - Most common audit trigger. Ensure operative report clearly states muscle, myocutaneous, or fasciocutaneous.
2. **Anatomical Region** - Head and neck must be documented. Other regions use different codes.
3. **Free vs. Pedicled** - If microvascular anastomosis performed, [[15750]] may be required instead of [[15731]].
4. **Medical Necessity** - Document defect size, location, and why flap was necessary vs. simpler closure.
5. **Assistant Surgeon** - Document why assistant was medically necessary.
6. **External Cause Codes** - If trauma documented, assign codes per ICD-10-CM External Cause Index 2025.<sup>1</sup>
7. **Concurrent Procedures** - Verify what is bundled vs. separately billable.
8. **Staged Procedures** - Use appropriate modifiers ([[-58]], [[-78]]) for staged or return procedures.
