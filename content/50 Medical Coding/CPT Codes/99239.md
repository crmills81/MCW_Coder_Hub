### **CPT 99239  - Hospital Discharge Management (> 30 min)**

**Description & Explanation:** Same as 99238, but for discharges requiring **more than 30 minutes** of total time on the discharge date.

- **Time:** **More than 30 minutes**.
    
- **Documentation Tip:** You **must** document the specific time spent (e.g., "Total discharge time 45 minutes") to bill this code. "Greater than 30 minutes" is acceptable but specific minutes are preferred for audit defense.
    

**Code Tree:**

- **WRVU (2025):** ~3.42

**Clinical Examples:**

1. **Complex Discharge to SNF:** Patient transferring to Skilled Nursing Facility. Physician calls receiving doctor, completes detailed transfer forms, coordinates with social work, and counsels family. Total time 45 minutes.
    
2. **Polypharmacy Reconciliation:** Patient with heart failure. Extensive medication reconciliation required, coordination with cardiologist, and detailed education to patient regarding fluid restrictions. Total time 35 minutes.
