## CPT 99213 Quick Reference Guide (2025/2026)

**Code:** [[99213]] 
**Type:** Evaluation and Management (E/M) 
**Patient Status:** Established Patient

### **Official Definitions**

**Long Definition:**

> Office or other outpatient visit for the evaluation and management of an established patient, which requires a medically appropriate history and/or examination and **low level of medical decision making**. When using time for code selection, **20-29 minutes** of total time is spent on the date of the encounter.

**Short Definition:** Established patient office visit, low MDM, 20-29 min.

---
### **Key Requirements (Billing Criteria)**

To bill 99213, you must meet **ONE** of the following two criteria (MDM _or_ Time):

#### **1. Medical Decision Making (MDM)**

Must meet **2 out of 3** elements of **Low Complexity**:

- **Problems:** Low number and complexity (e.g., 2 minor self-limited problems; 1 stable chronic illness; 1 acute uncomplicated illness/injury).

- **Data:** Limited amount of data to be reviewed and analyzed (e.g., ordering/reviewing 2 unique tests or documents).

- **Risk:** Low risk of morbidity from additional diagnostic testing or treatment (e.g., Over-the-counter drugs, physical therapy, minor surgery with no risk factors).

#### **2. Time (Total Time)**

- **Range:** 20-29 minutes.

- **Includes:** Face-to-face time **AND** non-face-to-face time spent by the provider on the **same day** (e.g., reviewing records, documenting, ordering meds, communicating with other professionals).

- **Excludes:** Time spent by clinical staff.

---

### **Common Use Cases**

- **Stable Chronic Conditions:** Routine follow-up for well-controlled hypertension, diabetes, or hyperlipidemia.

- **Minor Acute Injuries:** Evaluation of a minor ankle sprain or uncomplicated lower back strain.

- **Medication Management:** Refilling prescriptions with no or minor changes.

- **Infections:** Uncomplicated URI, otitis media, or simple UTI.

### **Differentiation (99213 vs. 99214)**

- **99213 (Low):** Patient is stable; 1 chronic condition; OTC meds; "Run of the mill" visit.
    
- **[[99214]] (Moderate):** Patient is exacerbating; 2+ chronic conditions; Prescription drug management (new RX or dosage change); Systemic symptoms.
    

### **Documentation Tips**

- **History & Exam:** "Medically appropriate" (no longer scored by bullet points/elements).
    
- **Time:** Must document exact total minutes if billing by time (e.g., "I spent 25 minutes total time...").
    
- **MDM:** Explicitly state the status of the problem (e.g., "Stable," "Worsening") to support the complexity level.

## Key Requirements

Coding is based on either the total time spent by the provider on the day of the encounter or the Level of Medical Decision Making (MDM).[](https://rhinomds.com/cpt-99213-coding-guide/)

- **Medical Decision Making**: Requires a "Low" level of MDM, which is met by satisfying two of the three elements: Low complexity of problems (e.g., one stable chronic illness), low amount/complexity of data, or low risk of complications.[](https://auctusgroupconsulting.com/99213-vs-99214/)
    
- **History and Exam**: Must be "medically appropriate" as determined by the provider; however, the extent of history and examination no longer dictates the code level.[](https://blog.hsmedsolutions.com/cpt-code-99213/)
    
- **Use Cases**: Routine follow-ups for controlled chronic conditions (e.g., hypertension, Type 2 diabetes), simple acute illnesses (e.g., [[sinusitis]], uncomplicated UTI), or minor injuries.[](https://www.pcgsoftware.com/cpt-code-99213-knowledge-usage-and-studies)
    

## Exclusions and Bundling

Under the National Correct Coding Initiative (NCCI), 99213 is often subject to bundling edits with other procedures performed on the same day.[](https://www.cms.gov/files/document/01-chapter1-ncci-medicare-policy-manual-2026-final.pdf)

- **Mutually Exclusive Codes**: It cannot be billed with other E/M codes (like [[99214]] or [[99203]]) for the same patient on the same day by the same provider. It is also bundled into "major" surgical procedures (those with 10- or 90-day global periods) unless the visit is for a completely unrelated problem.[](https://www.cgsmedicare.com/medicare_dynamic/j15/partb/ptpb/ptp.aspx)
    
- **Assistant at Surgery**: The "Assistant Surgeon" concept (Modifiers 80, 81, 82, or AS) is not applicable to 99213. Assistant modifiers are restricted to surgical procedure codes where an assistant is medically necessary; E/M codes are considered professional services inherent to the attending provider.[](https://www.premera.com/paymentpolicies/cmi_051730.pdf)
    
- **Modifier 25**: This is required if 99213 is performed on the same day as a "minor" procedure (e.g., an injection or simple repair) to indicate that the E/M service was a "significant, separately identifiable" service.[](https://www.cgsmedicare.com/partb/pubs/news/2012/0912/cope19880.html)
    

## Long Definition

"Office or other outpatient visit for the evaluation and management of an established patient, which requires a medically appropriate history and/or examination and low level of medical decision making. When using total time on the date of the encounter for code selection, 20 minutes must be met or exceeded".
