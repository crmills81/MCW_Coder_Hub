### **CPT 99236  - Observation/Inpatient Care, Same Day (High)**

**Description & Explanation:** Same as 99234/5 (Admit and Discharge on the same calendar date), but for **High** complexity cases.

- **MDM:** High.
    
- **Time:** 85 minutes.
    
- **Duration Rule:** Stay must typically be > 8 hours.
    

**Code Tree:**

- Evaluation and Management
    
    - Hospital Inpatient or Observation Care
        
        - Same Day Admission and Discharge
            
            - **99236: Admit/Discharge same date, high MDM or 85 min**
                

**Technical Data:**

- **WRVU (2025):** ~6.17
    
- **Global Period:** XXX
    
- **Assistant Payable:** No
    

**Clinical Examples:**

1. **Anaphylaxis:** Patient admitted with severe anaphylaxis requiring epinephrine drip and close monitoring. Stabilizes rapidly over 12 hours and is discharged with EpiPen and strict precautions.
    
2. **Suicide Attempt (Medical Clearance):** Patient admitted for monitoring after overdose (high risk). Medically cleared after 10 hours and transferred to inpatient psychiatric facility (discharge from medical service).
