### **CPT 99255  - Inpatient Consultation (Level 5)**

**Description & Explanation:** Used for an inpatient consultation requiring **High** complexity MDM. Reserved for severe, life-threatening, or highly complex diagnostic problems.

>[!warning]
>**Important Payer Note:** CPT codes **99252 -99255 (Inpatient Consultations)** are **not recognized by Medicare** (and many private payers aligning with CMS). For Medicare patients, you must typically bill these as Initial (9922x) or Subsequent (9923x) Hospital Care, even if you are a consultant.

- **MDM:** High.
    
- **Time:** 80 minutes.

**Clinical Examples:**

1. **Infectious Disease (Endocarditis):** Patient with new murmur and fevers. ID consulted for suspected endocarditis. Multiple blood cultures, echo review, long-term IV antibiotic planning, coordination for possible valve surgery.
    
2. **Neurology (Status Epilepticus):** Patient with intractable seizures in ICU. Neurology manages multiple anti-epileptic drips and EEG monitoring.
