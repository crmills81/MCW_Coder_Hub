# Modifier 57: Decision for [[surgery]]

**Modifier 57**, **Decision for Surgery**, is a powerful tool in your coding arsenal, especially for the high-stakes surgeries you'll encounter in [[Urology]], [[Ophthalmology]], and [[Otolaryngology]] at the Medical College of Wisconsin.

Unlike Modifiers [[-54]] and [[-55]], which split the "**work**" of a surgery, Modifier 57 identifies the **Evaluation and Management (E/M)** service where the surgeon first determined that a major operation was necessary.

---

## Deep Dive: Modifier 57

### The "90-Day Rule" (Major Surgery Only)

The most critical thing to remember is that Modifier 57 is **exclusively for major surgeries** (those with a 90-day global period).

- If you use it on a minor procedure (0 or 10-day global), the claim will likely be denied. For minor procedures, you should look at **Modifier [[-25]]** instead.
    

### Timing is Everything

To use Modifier 57, the E/M visit must occur on:

1. **The day of** the surgery, or
    
2. **The day before** the surgery.
    

### Common Use Cases in Your Specialties

- **Urology:** A patient presents to the ER with acute renal failure due to an obstruction. After a consult (E/M), the urologist decides to perform an emergency nephrostomy that same day.
    
- **Ophthalmology:** A patient has a sudden, traumatic retinal detachment. During the exam, the surgeon decides to operate immediately to save the patient's vision.
    
- **Otolaryngology:** An E/M visit for a patient with severe, acute airway obstruction where the **ENT** decides a tracheostomy is required within 24 hours.
    

### Documentation Requirements

Auditors look for the "Initial Decision." Your documentation must clearly state that the surgery was **not** planned prior to this specific encounter. If the patient was already scheduled for surgery and this was just a "pre-op clearance" visit, Modifier 57 is **not** appropriate.

|**Feature**|**Modifier 57**|**Modifier 25**|
|---|---|---|
|**Applicable To** |Major Surgery (90-day global)|Minor Surgery (0-10 day global) |
|**Service Type** | E/M only | E/M only|
|**Core Message** |"I just decided we need surgery." | "I did an extra, separate exam today." |
|**Common Scenarios** | Emergency or Urgent Major Surgery | Office-based minor procedures|

>[!important] 
>Pro-fee Coding Tip: At MCW, you may see "global" codes used frequently. Remember that the global surgical package _includes_ the pre-op visit the day before or the day of surgery. Modifier 57 is the "key" that unlocks payment for that E/M visit so it isn't bundled into the surgery fee.
