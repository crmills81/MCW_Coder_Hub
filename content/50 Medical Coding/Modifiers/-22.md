# CPT Modifier 22 - Increased Procedural Services

## Definition
- Indicates the work required to provide a service was substantially greater than typically required for the reported CPT code.

## When It Applies
- Significantly increased:
  - Intensity, time, physical/mental effort.
  - Technical difficulty of the procedure. [
- Examples:
  - Extreme obesity complicating surgery. 
  - Significant comorbidities or intra‑op complications increasing complexity (not otherwise coded).
  - Extensive adhesions or trauma complicating exposure and dissection.
  - Complex revision or removal of prior operative work that is unusually difficult.

## When NOT to Use
- Routine variations in difficulty or time that fall within the normal range for the CPT code. 
- Situations better described with additional CPT codes, add‑on codes, or unlisted procedure codes. 
- Cases supported only by vague statements like "difficult case" or "longer than usual" without specific comparative detail.

## Documentation Requirements
- In the official operative/procedure note (not just a cover letter), clearly document:
  - Why the case was unusual (anatomy, pathology, adhesions, body habitus, complication, etc.).
  - How much more work than a typical case (e.g., % more time, additional steps, extra exposure/closures). 
  - Objective data when available (extra blood loss, additional staff/time, need for complex reconstruction). 

### Suggested Documentation Phrase (Example)
> "This procedure required approximately 60% more operative time than typical due to dense intra‑abdominal adhesions from multiple prior surgeries, requiring extensive lysis before safe dissection could begin, significantly increasing technical difficulty and risk."

## Billing Notes
- Append modifier 22 only to the primary procedure code.
- Expect manual review; additional payment is not guaranteed and depends on payer policy and documentation quality.

## Related Modifiers
- 52 - Reduced services (used when part of the service is not performed; opposite direction from 22). 
- 53 - Discontinued procedure (procedure stopped before completion).
- 23 - Unusual anesthesia (for anesthesia complexity rather than surgical work). 

## Quick Example
- Code: 44140 - Colectomy, partial; with anastomosis.
- Billed: 44140‑22.
- Rationale: Extreme obesity and dense adhesions requiring extensive lysis and prolonged exposure/closure, clearly detailed in the operative note.
