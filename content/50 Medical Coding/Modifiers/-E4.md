# 👁️ Modifier -E4 — Lower Right Eyelid

## 📋 Modifier Description

> **E4 — Lower right eyelid**

**Modifier [[-E4]]** is a **HCPCS Level II anatomical modifier** used to identify that a procedure was performed specifically on the **lower eyelid of the right eye**. It is appended to **CPT** or **HCPCS** procedure codes to ensure precise anatomical identification of eyelid procedures when payer reporting requirements mandate eyelid-level specificity.

The lower right eyelid is the **mirror counterpart** to the lower left eyelid ([[-E2]]) and carries an identical pathology profile. **Involutional ectropion**, **entropion**, **lower lid laxity**, **cicatricial eyelid changes**, **lower lid malignancies** (particularly [[basal cell carcinoma]], which is highly prevalent on the lower lids), **lower lid chalazia**, and **lower lid reconstruction** following Mohs surgery are among the most common drivers of procedures coded with [[-E4]].

In clinical practice, lower eyelid procedures are frequently performed **bilaterally** (**using [[-E2]] and [[-E4]] together**), making correct E modifier pairing essential for accurate claim submission.

> [!note]
> > 📌 [[-E4]] and [[-E2]] together represent the **bilateral lower eyelid** combination — the most common pairing used in bilateral [[ectropion]] and [[entropion]] repair procedures. [[E4]] is the lower right counterpart to [[-E2]] (**lower left**).


---

## 🔬 When to Use Modifier [[-E4]]

Modifier [[E4]] should be appended when:

- The procedure is performed **exclusively** on the **lower right eyelid**
- Multiple eyelids are treated in the same session and the **lower right lid** must be distinguished
- **Medicare** or a **commercial payer** requires eyelid-specific anatomical identification
- The operative note specifically documents the **lower right eyelid** as the operative site

## 📋 Documentation Requirements

To support modifier [[-E4]], the operative or procedure note must clearly document:

- The **right eye** as the operative eye
- The **lower eyelid** as the operative lid
- The specific procedure performed at this location
- For multi-lid cases, each lid's procedure must be individually and explicitly described

> ✅ **Example Documentation Language:**
> *"The patient was positioned supine and the right eye was prepped and draped. Attention was directed to the lower right eyelid where significant involutional ectropion was noted with a markedly positive distraction and snap-back test. A lateral tarsal strip procedure was performed with tightening of the lower lid retractors. Final lid position and contour were satisfactory at the conclusion of the case."*

## 💡 Coding Examples

### Example 1 — Ectropion Repair, Lower Right Eyelid

**Clinical Scenario:** A 78-year-old female presents with progressive involutional ectropion of the lower right eyelid with epiphora and conjunctival exposure. A lateral tarsal strip procedure is performed.

**Correct Coding:**
- [[67917]] - [[-E4]] — Repair of ectropion, extensive (tarsal strip), lower right eyelid
- [[H02.104]] — Unspecified ectropion of right lower eyelid

---

### Example 2 — Bilateral Lower Lid Ectropion Repair (Same Session)

**Clinical Scenario:** A 71-year-old male has bilateral involutional lower lid ectropion with symptomatic epiphora and corneal exposure bilaterally. Bilateral tarsal strip procedures performed in the same operative session.

**Correct Coding:**
- [[67917]] - [[-E4]] — Ectropion repair, extensive, lower right eyelid
- [[67917]] - [[-E2]] - [[-51]] — Ectropion repair, extensive, lower left eyelid
- [[H02.104]] — Ectropion, right lower eyelid
- [[H02.105]] — Ectropion, left lower eyelid

> ✅ [[-E4]] for lower right + [[-E2]] for lower left. Modifier [[-51]] on the second procedure line.

---

### Example 3 — Basal Cell Carcinoma Excision and Full-Thickness Reconstruction, Lower Right Eyelid

**Clinical Scenario:** A 65-year-old male undergoes excision of a basal cell carcinoma involving greater than one-fourth of the lower right eyelid margin with full-thickness lid reconstruction using a lid-sharing technique.

**Correct Coding:**
- [[67966]] - [[-E4]] — Full-thickness excision and repair, lower right eyelid, over one-fourth of lid margin
- [[C44.112]] — Basal cell carcinoma of skin of right lower eyelid, including canthus

> ⚠️ If total lower lid reconstruction is required (**entire lower lid**), use [[67973]] (**lid-sharing technique**) or [[67975]] (other technique) instead of [[67966]].

---

### Example 4 — All Four Eyelids Addressed (Complete Four-Modifier Scenario)

**Clinical Scenario:** A patient with chronic blepharitis and recurrent chalazia on all four eyelids undergoes four-lid chalazion excisions in the same OR session.

**Correct Coding:**
- [[67800]] - [[-E1]] — Chalazion, upper left eyelid
- [[67800]] - [[-E2]] - [[-51]] — Chalazion, lower left eyelid
- [[67800]] - [[-E3]] - [[-51]] — Chalazion, upper right eyelid
- [[67800]] - [[-E4]] - [[-51]] — Chalazion, lower right eyelid

> ✅ All four E modifiers used. Each lid on its own billing line. Modifier [[-51]] on lines 2-4.

---

### Example 5 — Lower Right Lid Entropion + Upper Right Lid Ptosis (Same Eye, Same Session)

**Clinical Scenario:** A patient with right-sided facial nerve palsy has both lower right lid ectropion and upper right lid ptosis requiring surgical correction in the same session.

**Correct Coding:**
- [[67917]] - [-E4]] — Ectropion repair, extensive, lower right eyelid
- [[67904]] - [[-E3]] - [[-51]] — Ptosis repair, external levator advancement, upper right eyelid
- [[H02.104]] — Ectropion, right lower eyelid
- [[H02.401]] — Ptosis, right upper eyelid
- [[G51.0]] — Bell's palsy (or appropriate facial nerve palsy code as additional diagnosis)

> ✅ Two different lids on the same eye, each with its own E modifier. [[-51]] on the secondary procedure.

## ⚠️ Common Pitfalls

- **Confusing [[-E4]] (lower right) with [[-E2]] (lower left):** Both are lower eyelid modifiers — the critical distinction is **laterality**. Always confirm right vs. left from the operative report. Transposing -E2 and -E4 is a common and auditable error.
- **Using [[-RT]] instead of [[-E4]] for lower eyelid procedures:** [[-RT]] alone does not identify the lower (vs. upper) eyelid. [[-E4]] is required for lower right eyelid specificity.
- **Omitting the E modifier on reconstructive eyelid procedures:** Complex reconstructions ([[67961]], [[67966]], [[67973]], [[67975]]) still require E modifiers when they are eyelid-specific claims.
- **Mismatching diagnosis and procedure laterality:** If [[-E4]] is used on the procedure line but the diagnosis code identifies the left lower eyelid ([[H02.105]]) instead of the right ([[H02.104]]), the claim will fail consistency edits. Always confirm laterality alignment between diagnosis and procedure modifier.
- **Billing reconstruction codes without adequate documentation:** [[67973]] and [[67975]] (total lower eyelid reconstruction) require documentation confirming the entire lower lid was reconstructed — not merely greater than one-fourth of the lid margin (which is [[67966]]).
