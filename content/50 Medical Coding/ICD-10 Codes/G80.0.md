# ⚕️ICD-10 CM G80.0 - Spastic Quadriplegic Cerebral Palsy

## Code Description
**ICD-10 CM G80.0** represents **Spastic Quadriplegic Cerebral Palsy**. This is a specific subtype of [[cerebral palsy]] characterized by spasticity (**stiff muscles**) and weakness affecting all four limbs ([[quadriplegia]]), as well as the trunk and face. It is often the most severe form of **cerebral palsy** due to the extensive involvement of the motor system.<sup>CMS ICD-10</sup>

### Clinical Overview
**Spastic quadriplegic cerebral palsy** results from damage to the motor centers of the developing brain, typically occurring before, during, or shortly after birth. Patients often experience significant **motor impairments, difficulty with swallowing and speech**, and a high prevalence of comorbidities such as [[epilepsy]], intellectual disabilities, and orthopedic complications.<sup>NINDS</sup>

## Coding Hierarchy (Code Tree)
This code exists within the following hierarchy in the ICD-10-CM structure:

- **Chapter VI:** Diseases of the nervous system (G00-G99)
  - **Block:** Cerebral palsy and other paralytic syndromes (G80-G83)
    - **Category:** Cerebral palsy (G80)
      - **Subcategory:** Spastic quadriplegic cerebral palsy ([[G80.0]])

## Official Coding Guidelines & Notes

### Includes
This code includes conditions specified as:
- Cerebral palsy (**spastic**) quadriplegic
- Quadriplegic cerebral palsy<sup>WHO ICD-10</sup>

### Excludes1
*Note: Excludes1 means "not coded here". If the patient has this condition, do not use [[G80.0]].*
- **Congenital malformations of the nervous system** ([[Q04.-]])<sup>CMS ICD-10</sup>

### Excludes2
*Note: Excludes2 means "not included here". The patient may have both conditions, but code separately.*
- **Acquired cerebral palsy** ([[G83.1]])
- **Spastic diplegic cerebral palsy** ([[G80.1]])
- **Spastic hemiplegic cerebral palsy** ([[G80.2]])<sup>CMS ICD-10</sup>

### Coding Tips
- **Intellectual Disability:** If the patient has associated intellectual disabilities, code the intellectual disability first or concurrently using codes from category F70-F79.<sup>Coding Clinic</sup>
- **Epilepsy:** If the patient has associated epilepsy, assign the appropriate code from category G40 or G41.
- **Vision/Hearing:** Code any associated vision (H54.-) or hearing (H90.-) impairments.
- **Specificity:** Ensure documentation specifically supports "[[quadriplegic]]" and "[[spastic]]." If the type is unspecified, use [[G80.9]].

## Reimbursement & Risk Adjustment

### HCC (Hierarchical Condition Category)
- **Status:** **Yes**
- **Model:** CMS-HCC V28 (2024 Model)
- **Category:** Cerebral Palsy maps to specific HCC categories (often HCC 54 or similar depending on the specific model year) which significantly impact Risk Adjustment Factor (RAF) scores.<sup>CMS Risk Adjustment</sup>
- **Impact:** Accurate documentation of [[G80.0]] is critical for **Medicare Advantage plans** and ACA exchanges to reflect the high cost and complexity of care associated with this chronic neurological condition.

### wRVU (Work Relative Value Unit)
- **Value:** **N/A**
- **Reason:** [[G80.0]] is an ICD-10-CM diagnosis code. wRVU values are assigned to **CPT/HCPC**S procedure codes (e.g., [[99213]]), not diagnosis codes.<sup>AMA CPT</sup>

### Assistant Surgeon Payable
- **Status:** **N/A**
- **Reason:** Assistant surgeon modifiers (e.g., [[-80]], [[-81]], [[-82]]) apply to surgical CPT codes. Diagnosis codes do not have assistant payable status.<sup>CMS MPFS</sup>

### MS-DRG (Medicare Severity Diagnosis Related Group)
- **Applicability:** **Yes**
- **Impact:** [[G80.0]] influences DRG assignment when used as a principal or secondary diagnosis in inpatient settings.
- **MDC:** Typically falls under **MDC 01** (Diseases and Disorders of the Nervous System).
- **CC/MCC:** Cerebral Palsy is often considered a Complication or Comorbidity (CC). If associated with major complications (e.g., severe malnutrition, pneumonia), it may contribute to MCC status depending on the specific DRG logic.<sup>CMS MS-DRG</sup>

## Coding Examples

### Example 1: Initial Pediatric Diagnosis
**Scenario:** A 2-year-old child is evaluated by neurology following developmental delays. MRI shows periventricular leukomalacia. The physician diagnoses spastic quadriplegic cerebral palsy.
- **Primary Diagnosis:** [[G80.0]] (Spastic quadriplegic cerebral palsy)
- **Secondary Diagnosis:** [[P91.2]] (Cerebral ischemia of newborn) - *if applicable based on history*
- **Secondary Diagnosis:** [[F70]] (Mild intellectual disabilities) - *if documented*
- **Note:** Link the CP to any known birth complications if known.<sup>Coding Clinic</sup>

### Example 2: Adult Management with Comorbidities
**Scenario:** An adult patient with known CP presents for management of severe spasticity and contractures.
- **Primary Diagnosis:** [[G80.0]] (Spastic quadriplegic cerebral palsy)
- **Secondary Diagnosis:** [[M24.50]] (Contracture, unspecified joint)
- **Secondary Diagnosis:** [[G40.909]] (Epilepsy, unspecified)
- **Procedure Code:** [[95992]] (Evaluation of spinal cord stimulation system) - *if applicable*
- **Note:** Capture HCC status annually for risk adjustment.<sup>CMS Risk Adjustment</sup>

### Example 3: Inpatient Admission for Pneumonia
**Scenario:** Patient with [[G80.0]] is admitted with aspiration [[pneumonia]].
- **Principal Diagnosis:** [[J69.0]] ([[Pneumonitis]] due to inhalation of food and vomit)
- **Secondary Diagnosis:** [[G80.0]] (Spastic quadriplegic cerebral palsy)
- **Secondary Diagnosis:** [[R13.10]] ([[Dysphagia]], unspecified)
- **Note:** The CP is a significant comorbidity affecting the DRG weight.<sup>CMS MS-DRG</sup>
