# 🧬 ICD-10 CM M48.062 — Spinal Stenosis, Lumbar Region With Neurogenic Claudication

> [!success] Billable Code Confirmed  
> **ICD-10 CM [[M48.062]]** is a fully billable and valid **6-character diagnosis code** for **FY2027** that specifies **spinal stenosis** in the lumbar region accompanied by **neurogenic claudication**.<sup>1</sup> The sixth character "**2**" provides the highest level of clinical specificity required by **HIPAA** and **CMS standards**, confirming both the precise anatomical site and the specific functional manifestation.<sup>1</sup>

> [!warning] Non-Billable Parent Codes  
> Category **M48** (**Other spondylopathies**) and subcategory **M48.0** (**Spinal stenosis**) are non-billable parent codes that lack regional anatomical specificity.<sup>1</sup> Furthermore, subcategory **M48.06** (**Spinal stenosis, lumbar region**) is a **non-billable 5-character parent code** that cannot be reported on claims because it requires a 6th character to indicate the presence or absence of **neurogenic claudication**.<sup>1</sup>

> [!important] Clinical Context  
> The essential clinical driver for selecting **[[M48.062]]** is the definitive documentation of **neurogenic claudication**, where narrowing of the **lumbar spinal canal** compromises **lumbosacral nerve roots**, producing posture-dependent lower extremity pain, heaviness, or **[[paresthesias]]** during **[[ambulation]]** or standing.<sup>3</sup> This distinguishes the condition from **asymptomatic [[stenosis]]** or **lumbar [[stenosis]] without claudication** (**[[M48.061]]**).<sup>1</sup>

> [!danger] Code Classification  
> **[[M48.062]]** is a primary diagnosis code that classifies a **degenerative musculoskeletal condition** under **Chapter 13 of ICD-10-CM**.<sup>1</sup> It is used to support medical necessity for **conservative therapies, epidural steroid injections, and surgical decompressive** procedures rather than representing a procedural or manifestation-only code.<sup>1</sup>

## 🔍 Code Description  
**Lumbar spinal [[stenosis]] with neurogenic [[claudication]]**, designated by **[[M48.062]]**, describes **degenerative or acquired narrowing of the lumbar spinal canal, lateral recesses, or neural foramina** resulting in **mechanical compression and [[ischemia]] of the cauda equina or exiting nerve roots**.<sup>1</sup> This **compression** produces **neurogenic [[claudication]], characterized by radiating leg pain, numbness, and motor weakness that intensifies with walking or spinal extension and resolves with seated spinal flexion**.<sup>3</sup> When initial conservative therapies fail, interventional physiatrists utilize diagnostic and therapeutic fluoroscopically guided epidural injections, reported under **[[62323]]**, or **transforaminal epidural injections** under **[[64483]]**, to alleviate **nerve root inflammation**.<sup>2</sup>

In inpatient and surgical scenarios, accurate assignment of **[[M48.062]]** establishes medical necessity under **commercial** and **Medicare policies** for **invasive spinal decompression**.<sup>1</sup> Operative interventions associated with this condition commonly include **open lumbar [[laminectomy]], [[facetectomy]], and [[foraminotomy]]** at a single vertebral segment represented by **[[63047]]**, or **[[hemilaminotomy]] decompression** reported with **[[63030]]**. When significant mechanical instability, **facet [[arthropathy]]**, or **degenerative [[spondylolisthesis]]** accompanies the **[[stenosis]]**, surgeons frequently combine **[[decompression]]** with **posterior lumbar arthrodesis** coded as **[[22612]]** to stabilize the lumbar motion segment.<sup>2</sup>

> [!example] Specificity Impact on Payer Coverage
> 
> Assigning **[[M48.062]]** instead of the **non-billable parent code M48.06** or the non-claudication sibling **[[M48.061]]** is required by **Medicare Administrative Contractors** and **commercial payers** to approve prior authorizations for **decompressive spine surgery** or interventional epidural injections.<sup>1</sup> **Payer** policies strictly deny surgical **decompression** coverage under **[[M48.061]]** unless documented, reproducible **neurogenic claudication** is demonstrated.<sup>3</sup>
> 
>   

> [!tip]
> 
> Review clinic progress notes for positional symptom relief, such as pain relief upon sitting or bending forward ("**shopping cart sign**"), which clinically confirms **neurogenic claudication** over simple mechanical low back pain.<sup>3</sup>

## ❌ Excludes

### Excludes 1

- **[[G55]]** — **Nerve root and plexus compressions in diseases classified elsewhere**. Under **ICD-10-CM tabular coding conventions**, **nerve root compressions** due to **spondylopathies** (**M46.-, M48.-**) carry an **Excludes1 instruction** at **[[G55]]**, directing the coder to report the underlying **[[spondylopathy]]** code directly rather than coding the manifestation separately.<sup>1</sup>
    
      
    
- **[[M48.02]]** — **Spinal stenosis, cervical region**. Cervical spinal stenosis involves the cervical canal and spinal cord pathology, which is anatomically distinct from lumbar stenosis and cannot be represented by a lumbar code.<sup>1</sup>
    
      
    
- **[[M48.061]]** — **Spinal [[stenosis]], lumbar region without neurogenic claudication**. This code is mutually exclusive with **[[M48.062]]** because a patient encounter either exhibits neurogenic claudication symptoms or does not.<sup>1</sup>
    
      
    

> [!danger]
> 
> The most frequent **Excludes1 error** is dual-coding both **[[M48.061]]** and **[[M48.062]]** on the same date of service, or appending **[[G55]]** alongside **lumbar stenosis**. **ICD-10-CM conventions** dictate that **[[M48.062]]** comprehensively captures both the structural **lumbar stenosis** and the associated **nerve root compression** leading to **[[claudication]]**, making additional secondary **nerve root compression codes** contradictory.<sup>1</sup>
> 
>   

---

### Excludes 2

- **[[M54.41]]** — **Lumbago with sciatica, right side.** When a patient exhibits underlying lumbar canal [[stenosis]] along with an acute, distinct right-sided lumbar [[radiculopathy]] or [[sciatica]], an Excludes2 note allows both conditions to be reported concurrently if documented as separate clinical conditions.<sup>1</sup>
    
      
    
- **[[M54.42]]** — **Lumbago with sciatica, left side**. Both codes may be reported on the same claim when documented left-sided sciatic nerve impingement coexists with generalized lumbar central canal [[stenosis]].<sup>1</sup>
    
      
    
- **[[M99.63]]** — **Osseous and subluxation stenosis of intervertebral foramina of lumbar region.** If a biomechanical or post-traumatic somatic foraminal [[subluxation]] is identified and treated alongside degenerative canal stenosis, both codes can be assigned under Excludes2 guidelines if supported by documentation.<sup>1</sup>

## 📋 Clinical Overview

### Neurogenic Claudication vs. Non-Claudication Stenosis and Sciatica

**Differentiating neurogenic claudication** associated with **lumbar spinal stenosis** from **non-claudication** presentations and isolated **lumbar radiculopat**hy is essential for **correct diagnostic coding** and **procedural authorization**. While all three conditions cause lower extremity discomfort, their underlying pathophysiology, exacerbating factors, and postural relief mechanisms differ significantly. The table below delineates the distinguishing clinical, diagnostic, and functional characteristics of **[[M48.062]]** compared to related dorsopathies.

  

|**Feature**|**[[M48.062]]**|**Related [[M48.061]]**|**Related [[M54.41]]**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Symptom Manifestation**|Bilateral or unilateral leg pain, heaviness, numbness, or cramping triggered by ambulation or standing, rapidly relieved by sitting or lumbar spinal flexion.<sup>3</sup>|Back pain or local spinal stiffness without walking-induced lower extremity cramping, heaviness, or neurological claudication.<sup>1</sup>|Sharp, lancinating pain following a specific dermatome down the right lower extremity, often aggravated by sitting and coughing.<sup>1</sup>|
|**Pathophysiology**|Central or lateral canal narrowing leading to microvascular ischemia and compression of lumbosacral cauda equina nerve roots during lordotic extension.<sup>3</sup>|Anatomical narrowing of the central lumbar canal without significant functional neural ischemia or dynamic ambulation deficits.<sup>1</sup>|Direct impingement or chemical inflammation of an isolated lumbar nerve root (typically L4, L5, or S1) on the right side.<sup>1</sup>|
|**Clinical Exam Findings**|Normal resting peripheral pulses; positive "shopping cart sign" or stooped posture during gait; preserved motor function at rest.<sup>3</sup>|Normal neurological examination of the lower extremities; restricted lumbar extension and localized facet tenderness.<sup>1</sup>|Positive straight leg raise test on the right side; localized dermatomal sensory loss or diminished deep tendon reflexes.<sup>3</sup>|

> [!important]
> 
> Documentation stating "**pain with walking**" or "**leg weakness with standing relieved by sitting**" constitutes a major CDI trigger for **[[M48.062]]**. Coders should verify that the provider explicitly correlates these symptoms to **lumbar [[stenosis]]** rather than defaulting to unspecified **lower back pain** or **vascular disease**.<sup>3</sup>
> 
>   

---

### Manifestations & Symptom Burden

- **Postural Pseudo-Claudication:** Heaviness, numbness, or burning pain in the buttocks, thighs, and calves that worsens when standing erect or walking and resolves when sitting or leaning forward.<sup>3</sup>
    
      
    
- **Motor Weakness & Gait Imbalance:** Transient weakness in the lower extremities during prolonged ambulation, predisposing elderly patients to fall risks and loss of functional independence.<sup>3</sup>
    
      
    
- **Bilateral Radicular Sensory Disturbances:** [[Paresthesias]], [[dysesthesias]], or tingling distributed diffusely across the L4, L5, and S1 dermatomes rather than a solitary isolated root distribution.<sup>3</sup>
    
      
    
- **Loss of Lumbar Extension:** Severe exacerbation of back and leg symptoms during spinal extension or backward leaning due to anatomical canal narrowing in [[lordosis]].<sup>3</sup>
    
      
    

> [!tip]
> 
> If **acute bowel or bladder dysfunction, saddle anesthesia, or progressive bilateral foot drop** develops, immediately evaluate the chart for **cauda equina syndrome**, which represents a surgical emergency requiring distinct diagnostic coding.<sup>3</sup>
> 
>

When **[[M48.062]]** is sequenced as the principal diagnosis for a medical inpatient admission, it groups into the **Medical Back Problems category** under **MS-DRG 551 or 552** depending on the presence of a qualifying **Major Complication or Comorbidity (MCC).**<sup>1</sup> If the inpatient admission is primarily for **surgical [[decompression]]** (**such as a [[laminectomy]]**), the surgical procedure takes precedence, routing the stay to the **Back and Neck Procedures family** (**MS-DRGs 518–520**) or **Spinal Fusion family** (**MS-DRGs 453–460**), substantially increasing **hospital resource allocation** and **reimbursement**.<sup>1</sup> Secondary diagnoses must be audited carefully to capture comorbid conditions that achieve **CC** or **MCC status**.<sup>1</sup>

  

- **LCD Coverage Requirements:** **Medicare Administrative Contractor (MAC)** **Local Coverage Determinations** **(e.g., LCDs for Epidural Steroid Injections and Lumbar Decompression**) explicitly mandate documentation of **[[M48.062]]** alongside failure of at least 4 to 6 weeks of conservative, non-operative management (**e.g., physical therapy, pharmacotherapy**) before interventional injections or elective surgical **[[decompression]]** are deemed medically necessary.<sup>1</sup>
    
      
    
- **Imaging Correlation:** LCD guidelines require that advanced imaging (MRI or CT) findings correlating with the clinical diagnosis of **lumbar stenosis** and **neurogenic claudication** be documented within the previous 12 to 24 months.<sup>1</sup>

### NCCI Bundling Considerations

Under **National Correct Coding Initiative (NCCI) edits**, fluoroscopic imaging guidance is bundled directly into lumbar epidural injection code **[[62323]]** and transforaminal injection code **[[64483]]**, precluding separate reporting of **[[fluoroscopy]]** codes.<sup>2</sup> In addition, lumbar **[[laminectomy]]** code **[[63047]]** includes **[[facetectomy]]**, **[[foraminotomy]]**, and **nerve root decompression** at that segment; separate **[[foraminotomy]]** or disc excision codes at the same level are **column 2 edits** and bundled unless performed at a completely distinct anatomical level with modifier **[[-59]]**.<sup>2</sup>

## ⚠️ Coding Pitfalls and Tips

- **Pitfall 1**: Submitting the **5-character parent code M48.06** on outpatient or inpatient claims; **Tips**: **M48.06** is a non-billable code that triggers automated clearinghouse rejections, requiring selection of **6-character** code **[[M48.062]]** when **claudication** is present.
    
      
    
- **Pitfall 2**: Confusing **neurogenic claudication** with **vascular claudication**; **Tips**: Ensure documentation confirms a spinal etiology with positional relief (**sitting or [[flexion]]**) rather than **peripheral arterial disease**, which codes to the **I70 category**.<sup>3</sup>
    
      
    
- **Pitfall 3**: Assigning **non-claudication code** **[[M48.061]]** when clinical notes clearly document **ambulatory** leg pain or the "**shopping cart sign**"; **Tips**: Query the provider to align the formal diagnosis with the clinical symptoms described in the **HPI**.<sup>3</sup>
    
      
    
- **Pitfall 4**: Appending bilateral modifier **[[-50]]** to **lumbar laminectomy code** **[[63047]]**; **Tips**: Code **[[63047]]** inherently includes unilateral or bilateral **[[decompression]]** in its **CPT definition**, making modifier **[[-50]]** redundant and inappropriate.
    
      
    
- **Pitfall 5**: Unbundling fluoroscopic guidance code **[[77003]]** when reporting **lumbar interlaminar epidural injection** **[[62323]]**; **Tips**: CPT **[[62323]]** includes **fluoroscopic** or **CT guidance** in its base description; **NCCI edits** strictly bundle separate guidance codes.
    
      
    
- **Pitfall 6**: Failing to document failed conservative management prior to elective surgical **[[decompression]]**; **Tips**: **Medicare LCD guidelines** mandate documentation of at least 4 to 6 weeks of failed conservative therapy (**physical therapy, NSAIDs**) before approving elective spinal surgery.<sup>1</sup>
