# 🧬 ICD-10 CM G55 — Nerve Root and Plexus Compressions in Diseases Classified Elsewhere

> [!success] Billable Code Confirmed  
> **ICD-10 CM [[G55]]** is a complete **3-character code with no children**, so it is valid for claim submission with no further characters.<sup>1,3</sup> It is a **manifestation code** and may only be reported as a secondary diagnosis after the underlying disease.<sup>2,3</sup>

> [!warning] Non-Billable Parent Codes  
> **G50-G59** is a **block range**, not a code, and is never reported. **G54** (**Nerve root and plexus disorders**) is a non-billable header that requires a 4th character such as **[[G54.0]]** or **[[G54.4]]**.

> [!important] Clinical Context  
> Use **[[G55]]** only when the **[[compression]]** is documented as due to a disease classified elsewhere, most reliably a **[[neoplasm]]** (**C00-D49**). **Compression** due to **disc disorders**, **[[spondylosis]]**, **spondylopathies**, **dorsopathies** or **[[ankylosing spondylitis]]** is an **Excludes1** situation and belongs in the **M-codes** instead.<sup>3</sup>

> [!danger] Code Classification  
> **ICD-10 CM [[G55]]** is a diagnosis code (**manifestation**) with a mandatory "**code first underlying disease**" instruction.<sup>3</sup> It is **never first-listed or principal**, and it is not a procedure code.

---

## 🔍 Code Description  
**ICD-10 CM [[G55]]** **captures compression of a nerve root or a nerve plexus** (**[[cervical]], brachial, [[lumbosacral]]**) that is **a manifestation of another disease**. A common example is a **[[tumor]] mass** pressing on a **root** or **plexus**, where the **[[tumor]]** is coded first, for example **[[C41.2]]** (**malignant neoplasm of vertebral column**) or **[[C79.51]]** (**secondary malignant neoplasm of bone**). The code title carries "**in diseases classified elsewhere**", which marks it as part of the **etiology/manifestation** convention.<sup>2,3</sup>

Because the **Excludes1 note** removes the most common causes of **[[radiculopathy]]** (**disc and [[spondylosis]]**), **[[G55]]** is used much less often than **[[G54.4]]** or **[[M51.16]]**. When the provider documents a specific **[[compression]]** due to a non-excluded disease, **[[G55]]** tells the reader the **nerve injury** is secondary to that disease and not a primary nerve disorder.

> [!example] Manifestation Versus Primary Code  
> Choosing **[[G55]]** over **[[G54.4]]** or **[[G54.0]]** depends on documentation of an underlying disease that causes the **[[compression]]**. Without that link, the root or plexus disorder is a primary **G54** code.

> [!tip]  
> If the documented cause is a **disc [[herniation]]** or **[[spondylosis]]** with **[[radiculopathy]]**, stop and use the **M-code.** Examples are **[[M51.16]]** and **[[M47.22]]**.

## ✅ Includes  
- **Nerve root [[compression]] due to a neoplasm**, with the neoplasm code **[[C79.51]]** or **[[C41.2]]** sequenced first.
- **Plexus compression** (**brachial or [[lumbosacral]]**) due to another underlying disease that is not on the Excludes1 list.
- **Compressive [[radiculopathy]]** documented as "**in**" or "**due to**" a disease classified elsewhere.

---

## ❌ Excludes  

### Excludes 1  
- **M45.-** ([[ankylosing spondylitis]]): The M-code already captures the nerve root compression, so [[G55]] is never added.
- **M53.- / M54.-** (dorsopathies): Radiculopathy in these conditions is classified in Chapter 13.
- **M50.1- / M51.1-** (disc disorders with radiculopathy): These are the correct codes for disc-related root compression, such as **[[M51.16]]**.
- **M46.- / M48.-** (spondylopathies): Compression is classified to the spondylopathy code.
- **M47.0- / M47.2-** ([[spondylosis]]): Use the spondylosis with radiculopathy code, such as **[[M47.22]]**.

> [!danger]  
> The most common **error** is pairing **[[G55]]** with a **disc** or **[[spondylosis]] [[radiculopathy]] code** because the note says "**nerve compression**". **Excludes1** means the two are never reported together, and the **M-code** alone is correct.

### Excludes 2  
No Excludes2 note exists at **[[G55]]**.

## 📋 Clinical Overview  

### Manifestation Versus Primary Nerve Root Disorder  
**ICD-10 CM [[G55]]** sits next to **primary root** and **plexus disorders** in the **G50-G59 block**, so selection depends on whether an underlying disease is documented and whether it is excluded. The table below shows how **[[G55]]** compares with two common alternatives.

| **Feature** | **[[G55]]** | **Related [[G54.4]]** | **Related [[M51.16]]** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Type of code** | Manifestation code, secondary only. | Primary root disorder, may be first-listed. | Disc disorder with lumbar radiculopathy, may be first-listed. |
| **Typical cause** | Neoplasm or other non-excluded disease. | Lumbosacral root disorder with no classified cause. | Herniated or displaced lumbar disc. |
| **Sequencing** | Code first the underlying disease. | No code-first rule. | No code-first rule. |

> [!important]  
> **CDI trigger**: a note that says "**tumor compressing nerve root**" or "**[[plexopathy]] due to mass**" needs the cause linked in the documentation. Query when the cause is unclear, and exclude **disc** and **[[spondylosis]]** causes.

### Manifestations & Symptom Burden  
- **Radicular pain:** Sharp or burning pain along the nerve distribution, often worse with movement.
- **Sensory loss:** Numbness or **[[paresthesia]]** in the affected dermatome.
- **Motor weakness:** Weakness or **[[atrophy]]** of muscles supplied by the compressed root or plexus.
- **Reflex change:** Diminished or absent deep tendon reflexes at the involved level.

> [!tip]  
> Do **not** add a separate code for **pain** or **numbness** when it is integral to the **[[compression]]**. Report additional deficits only when they are not routinely part of the condition.

**Nerve [[decompression]]** is generally bundled into a more extensive **[[laminectomy]]** or **[[decompression]]** at the same site and session. Check current **NCCI PTP edits** before reporting more than one decompression code.

## 💊 Coding Scenarios and Examples  

### Example 1
> **Clinical Scenario:**  
> A patient with metastatic bone disease to the lumbar spine has radicular pain and weakness. The provider documents "lumbar nerve root compression due to [[metastasis]]". The root is decompressed by laminectomy.

| **Field** | **Code** | **Rationale** |
|---|---|---|
| CPT | [[63047]] | Laminectomy with decompression of a lumbar nerve root. |
| PDx | [[C79.51]] | The underlying disease is coded first. |
| Dx 2 | [[G55]] | Manifestation of the metastasis, sequenced after it. |

> [!tip]  
> Sequence the cancer first, then [[G55]]. Query if the provider does not link the compression to the metastasis.

### Example 2
> **Clinical Scenario:**  
> A patient with a vertebral column tumor has cervical root compression and undergoes decompression. The right side is treated.

| **Field** | **Code** | **Rationale** |
|---|---|---|
| CPT | [[64722]] | Decompression of an unspecified nerve. |
| CPT 2 | [[64713]] | Reported only if the brachial plexus is separately decompressed and documented. |
| PDx | [[C41.2]] | The primary neoplasm is coded first. |

> [!tip]  
> [[G55]] follows the neoplasm code. Check NCCI edits before reporting two decompression codes.

### Example 3
> **Clinical Scenario:**  
> A patient with a lumbar disc herniation has radiculopathy and the provider writes "nerve root compression".

| **Field** | **Code** | **Rationale** |
|---|---|---|
| CPT | [[63030]] | Lumbar laminotomy with decompression. |
| PDx | [[M51.16]] | Disc disorder with radiculopathy captures the compression. |

> [!tip]  
> Do not add [[G55]]. Excludes1 applies to disc disorders with radiculopathy.

## ⚠️ Coding Pitfalls and Tips  

- **Pitfall 1**: Reporting **[[G55]]** as the first-listed diagnosis; **Tips**: It is a **manifestation** code, so list the underlying disease first.
- **Pitfall 2**: Pairing **[[G55]]** with **M51.1-** or **M47.2-**; **Tips**: **Excludes1** applies, so use only the **M-code**.
- **Pitfall 3**: Assuming **[[G55]]** for any "**nerve compression**" statement; **Tips**: Look for a documented underlying disease before using it.
- **Pitfall 4**: Using **[[G55]]** for **[[carpal tunnel syndrome]]**; **Tips**: **Carpal tunnel** is classified to **G56.0-**, such as **[[G56.01]]**.
- **Pitfall 5**: Expecting **[[G55]]** to drive the **MS-DRG**; **Tips**: The principal diagnosis assigns the **DRG**, so verify any **CC/MCC status** in **Appendix C**.
- **Pitfall 6**: Reporting a code from the **Excludes1 list** because the title looks similar; **Tips**: Confirm the root cause before choosing between **[[G55]], G54.- and M-codes**.
