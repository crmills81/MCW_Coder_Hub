# 🧬 ICD-10 CM M51.26 — Other Intervertebral Disc Displacement, Lumbar Region

> [!success] Billable Code Confirmed
> **ICD-10 CM [[M51.26]]** is a **5-character** code. The category is **M51**, the subcategory is **M51.2** (**other displacement**), and the **6th** character "**6**" is the lumbar region. It has no child codes, so it is valid and billable for **FY2027** (effective 10/1/2026).<sup>1,2</sup>

> [!warning] Non-Billable Parent Codes
> **M51** (**Thoracic, thoracolumbar, and lumbosacral intervertebral disc disorders**) is a category header that needs 4 or more additional characters. **M51.2** (**Other thoracic, thoracolumbar and lumbosacral intervertebral disc displacement**) is a subcategory header that still needs the site character.<sup>1,2</sup>

> [!important] Clinical Context
> Use **[[M51.26]]** when the provider documents lumbar disc **[[displacement]]** (**[[herniation]], [[protrusion]], bulge**) with no **[[radiculopathy]]** and no **[[myelopathy]]**. If nerve root symptoms are documented, the choice moves to the **M51.16- series** (**disc disorder with [[radiculopathy]], lumbar region**).<sup>1,3</sup>

> [!danger] Code Classification
> This is a **diagnosis code (ICD-10-CM)** and is not a procedure code. It supports **medical necessity for imaging, injections and spine surgery**, but it never describes the service itself.

---

## 🔍 Code Description

**ICD-10 CM [[M51.26]]** reports **[[displacement]] of a lumbar intervertebral disc (L1-L5 levels) when the provider documents [[herniation]], [[protrusion]] or bulge**. The **disc** material has moved beyond its normal margin, but the record shows no **[[radiculopathy]]** and no **[[myelopathy]]**. **[[Lumbago]]** due to **disc displacement** indexes here as well.<sup>1,2</sup>

The code is a **laterality-neutral**, single-region code. **Displacement** at the **L5-S1 junction** goes to **[[M51.27]]** (**lumbosacral region**), and an **L1-L5 level** plus **L5-S1 level** need separate codes. Documentation of **nerve root involvement** changes the code to **[[M51.16]]** or **[[M54.16]]**, depending on how the provider links the **[[radiculopathy]]**.<sup>1</sup>

> [!example] Radiculopathy Drives the Code
> **Payers** and **MAC ESI** policies usually look for **radicular pain** or **[[stenosis]]** symptoms, so a plain **[[M51.26]]** can fall short of the covered-diagnosis list. A query for documented **[[radiculopathy]]** changes the code to **M51.16-** and can change coverage.<sup>3,4</sup>

> [!tip]
> The **6th** character "**6**" means **[[lumbar]]**. Match the documented disc level to the region (**L1-L5 is lumbar, L5-S1 is lumbosacral**) before choosing **[[M51.26]]** or **[[M51.27]]**.

## ✅ Includes

- **Lumbar disc [[herniation]]**, **[[protrusion]]** or **bulge** with no **[[radiculopathy]]** or **[[myelopathy]]** documented.
- L**umbago due to displacement of a lumbar intervertebral disc**, which indexes to the **M51.2- subcategory**.
- **Disc displacement** at the **L1-L5 levels**. **Disc displacement** at **L5-S1** goes to **[[M51.27]]**.

## ❌ Excludes

### Excludes 1
> **Detail Rule:** No **Excludes1 note** is listed at **[[M51.26]]** or at subcategory **M51.2 in the tabular list**.

> [!danger]
> The **common error** is treating **radiculopathy** as a free add-on. When the provider links **[[radiculopathy]]** to the disc, use **[[M51.16]]** rather than **[[M51.26]]** plus a **[[radiculopathy]]** code. Check the **tabular list** before pairing **[[M51.26]]** with **[[M54.16]]**, **[[M51.06]]** or **[[M51.36A]]**-type **disc [[degeneration]]** codes.

### Excludes 2
- **M50.-** (**cervical and cervicothoracic disc disorders**): can be reported with **[[M51.26]]** if the patient has cervical disc disease at a separate level, because the **Excludes2 note** allows both.
- **[[M53.3]]** (**[[sacral]] and [[sacrococcygeal]] disorders, not elsewhere classified**): can be reported with **[[M51.26]]** when the sacral disorder is a separate documented condition.

## 📋 Clinical Overview

### Displacement vs. Radiculopathy vs. Degeneration
**Lumbar disc codes** are chosen by what the provider documents about the disc and the nerve. **[[Displacement]]** without neurologic findings is **[[M51.26]]**. **[[Displacement]]** with **[[radiculopathy]]** is **M51.16-**, and **degeneration** alone goes to the **M51.36- serie**s.<sup>1</sup>

| **Feature** | **[[M51.26]]** | **Related [[M51.16]]** | **Related [[M51.06]]** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Neurologic involvement** | None documented; local lumbar or low back pain only. | Radiculopathy documented as linked to the disc. | Myelopathy documented as linked to the disc. |
| **Typical imaging** | MRI shows herniation, protrusion or bulge. | MRI disc finding plus a concordant nerve root. | MRI with cord or conus compromise. |
| **Typical management** | Conservative care, PT, possible injection if criteria are met. | ESI, surgical decompression if conservative care fails. | Often urgent surgical evaluation. |

> [!important]
> **CDI trigger**: a record of "**herniated lumbar disc**" plus leg pain, **numbness** or **weakness** should prompt a query on whether the **[[radiculopathy]]** is due to the disc. Without that link **[[M51.26]]** may be assigned when **[[M51.16]]** is more accurate.

### Manifestations & Symptom Burden
- **Low back pain (lumbago)** that is worse with **[[flexion]]**, sitting or lifting.
- **Paraspinal muscle [[spasm]]** with reduced lumbar range of motion.
- Pain that stays in the back and does not follow a **dermatomal pattern**, which is the clinical reason **[[radiculopathy]]** is not documented.
- Functional limits in sitting tolerance, lifting and prolonged standing.

> [!tip]
> **Symptoms** alone do not make the diagnosis. **Code** the **disc [[displacement]]** as documented by the provider, and add **[[radiculopathy]]** or **[[myelopathy]]** only when the provider documents them.

| **Field** | **Value** |
|:---:|---|
| **MDC** | MDC 08 — Musculoskeletal System and Connective Tissue |
| **Medical DRG (with MCC)** | **DRG 551** — Medical Back Problems with MCC |
| **Medical DRG (without MCC)** | **DRG 552** — Medical Back Problems without MCC |
| **Surgical DRG (with MCC)** | **DRG 518** — Back and Neck Procedures Except Spinal Fusion with MCC or Disc Device/Neurostimulator |
| **Surgical DRG (with CC)** | **DRG 519** — Back and Neck Procedures Except Spinal Fusion with CC |
| **Surgical DRG (without CC/MCC)** | **DRG 520** — Back and Neck Procedures Except Spinal Fusion without CC/MCC |
| **[[M51.26]] CC/MCC status** | Neither CC nor MCC |

Sequence **[[M51.26]]** as the principal diagnosis only when the disc **[[displacement]]** is the condition that occasioned the admission. Secondary conditions such as **[[sepsis]]**, **post-op complications** or specific **neurologic deficits** supply the **CC/MCC**. **[[Fusion]]** cases group to separate **[[fusion]] DRGs**.

- **NCD:** no **NCD** is specific to **[[M51.26]]**. **NCD 150.10** (**Lumbar Artificial Disc Replacement**) is the nearest **NCD** for CPT **[[22857]]** and has age and indication limits.
- **LCD:** the **ESI LCDs** (**for example Novitas L36920**) require **radicular pain** or **[[stenosis]]**, imaging, baseline pain scoring and fluoroscopic or CT guidance. Check whether your **MAC's billing article lists [[M51.26]]**.<sup>3,4</sup>
- **Verify:** the covered-diagnosis list in each **MAC's billing article**, because they differ by **contractor**.

## 💊 Coding Scenarios and Examples

### Example 1
> **Clinical Scenario:**
> A 48-year-old has lumbar MRI for low back pain. The MRI shows an L4-L5 disc [[protrusion]], and the provider documents no radiating symptoms and no neurologic deficit.

| **Field** | **Code** | **Rationale** |
|---|---|---|
| CPT | [[72148]] | MRI of the lumbar spine without contrast. |
| PDx | [[M51.26]] | Documented lumbar disc displacement with no radiculopathy or myelopathy. |

> [!tip]
> Code the provider's confirmed diagnosis rather than the imaging report alone. If the clinician has not documented the finding in the encounter note, query before coding.

### Example 2
> **Clinical Scenario:**
> A patient with L4-L5 disc herniation has failed 6 weeks of conservative care. The provider documents pain and performs a fluoroscopically guided lumbar interlaminar ESI on the same day as a separate E/M.

| **Field** | **Code** | **Rationale** |
|---|---|---|
| CPT | [[62323]] | Lumbar interlaminar ESI with imaging guidance. |
| CPT 2 | [[99213]]-[[-25]] | Separate, identifiable E/M on the same day. |
| PDx | [[M51.26]] | Lumbar disc displacement; confirm the MAC list accepts it. |

> [!tip]
> If leg pain is documented and linked to the disc, use [[M51.16]] because it is more likely to be a covered ESI diagnosis.

### Example 3
> **Clinical Scenario:**
> A patient is admitted for an open L5 [[hemilaminotomy]] with [[discectomy]] for a documented L4-L5 herniated disc. The provider documents the [[herniation]] without [[radiculopathy]].

| **Field** | **Code** | **Rationale** |
|---|---|---|
| CPT | [[63030]] | Hemilaminotomy with excision of herniated disc, one lumbar interspace. |
| PDx | [[M51.26]] | Lumbar disc displacement is the reason for surgery. |

> [!tip]
> Most surgical patients have documented radicular findings, so confirm with the surgeon before settling on [[M51.26]]. Inpatient facility coding would add 0SB20ZZ.

## ⚠️ Coding Pitfalls and Tips

- **Pitfall 1**: Reporting **[[M51.26]]** when the provider documents **[[sciatica]]** or **[[radiculopathy]]** linked to the disc; **Tips**: Use **[[M51.16]]** when the disc is linked to the **[[radiculopathy]]**.
- **Pitfall 2**: Using **[[M51.26]]** for an L5-S1 disc; **Tips**: L5-S1 is the **[[lumbosacral]] region**, so use **[[M51.27]]**.
- **Pitfall 3**: Coding **disc [[degeneration]]** as **[[displacement]]**; **Tips**: **[[Degeneration]]** alone goes to the **M51.36- series**, and **[[displacement]]** plus **[[degeneration]]** at the same level are reported per **Tabular guidance**.
- **Pitfall 4**: Assuming **[[M51.26]]** supports an **ESI**; **Tips**: Many **MAC ESI** policies want **radicular pain** or **[[stenosis]]**, so check the billing article.
- **Pitfall 5**: Coding from the **MRI report** alone; **Tips**: Outpatient and **pro-fee coding** requires the treating provider's diagnostic statement.
- **Pitfall 6**: Expecting **RAF** or **DRG severity** from **[[M51.26]]**; **Tips**: It is not an **HCC**, **CC** or **MCC**, so capture other documented conditions for **severity**.
