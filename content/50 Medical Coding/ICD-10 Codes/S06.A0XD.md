# 🧬 ICD-10 CM S06.A0XD — Traumatic Brain Compression Without Herniation, Subsequent Encounter

> [!success] Billable Code Confirmed  
> **ICD-10 CM [[S06.A0XD]]** is a **7-character code**: **S06.A0** (**traumatic brain [[compression]] without [[herniation]]**), **X** (**placeholder**), and **D** (**subsequent encounter**). It is complete and valid for **FY2026** claims.<sup>1</sup>

> [!warning] Non-Billable Parent Codes  
> **S06.A0** (**Traumatic brain [[compression]] without [[herniation]]**) and **S06.A** (**Traumatic brain [[compression]] and [[herniation]]**) are headers and cannot be reported alone. **S06** (**Intracranial injury**) is also non-billable.<sup>2</sup>

> [!important] Clinical Context  
> Use **[[S06.A0XD]]** when **brain compression** is documented without **[[herniation]]** and the encounter is for routine healing care after active treatment. **[[compression]]** with **[[herniation]]** is **[[S06.A1XD]]**.<sup>2</sup>

> [!danger] Code Classification  
> This is an **ICD-10-CM injury diagnosis code** (**Chapter 19**). It follows a **Code** first note, so the underlying **traumatic brain injury** must be sequenced ahead of it.<sup>2</sup>

---

## 🔍 Code Description  
**Traumatic brain compression** happens when **brain tissue, CSF and vessels are pushed out of their usual position inside the skull**. This is usually from **swelling, [[hematoma]] or other mass effect after head trauma**.<sup>4</sup> The code was added with subcategory **S06.A**, which splits **[[compression]]** without **[[herniation]]** (**[[S06.A0XD]]**) from **[[compression]]** with **[[herniation]]** (**[[S06.A1XD]]**).<sup>4</sup>

**S06.A** requires you to code first the underlying **traumatic brain injury**, such as diffuse (**S06.2-**), focal (**S06.3-**), subdural (**S06.5-**) or **subarachnoid** (**S06.6-**) injury.<sup>2</sup> The **7th character D** marks routine healing care after active treatment, such as **[[neurosurgery]]** follow-up. **Active** treatment uses **[[S06.A0XA]]**, and residual deficits use **[[S06.A0XS]]**.

> [!example] Compression vs Herniation  
> The split between **S06.A0** and **S06.A1** comes from the provider's documentation of herniation. Choose the code from what is documented, **not from imaging terms alone**.

> [!tip]  
> **Sequence** the underlying **TBI** first, then **[[S06.A0XD]]**. Both codes should carry the same **7th character** when they describe the same phase of care.

## ❌ Excludes  

### Excludes 1  
- **S09.90 (Head injury NOS):** an **Excludes1** at **S06**, so do not report **head injury NOS** with a specific intracranial injury.<sup>2</sup>

> [!danger]  
> The most common **error** is reporting **[[S06.A0XD]]** alone with no underlying **TBI**. The **Code first note** makes the **TBI mandatory**, and an unspecified head injury code is not a substitute.

### Excludes 2  
- No **Excludes2 note** is listed at **S06.A0**. Use the Code also and Use additional notes at **S06** instead: **S01.-** (**open wound of head**), **S02.-** (**skull fracture**) and **[[F06.70]]** or **[[F06.71]]** (**mild [[neurocognitive]] disorder due to known physiological condition**).<sup>2</sup>

## 📋 Clinical Overview  

### Compression Without vs With Herniation  
**Both codes** follow trauma with mass effect on the **brain**. What separates them is whether brain tissue has shifted across a **dural or skull boundary**, which carries a **much worse prognosis**.

| **Feature** | **[[S06.A0XD]]** | **Related [[S06.A1XD]]** | **Related [[S06.A0XA]]** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Herniation** | Not documented. | Documented. | Not documented. |
| **Phase of care** | Subsequent, routine healing. | Subsequent, routine healing. | Initial, active treatment. |
| **Typical follow-up** | Repeat CT/MRI, neuro checks, rehab. | Intensive neuro follow-up, rehab. | Hospital treatment and monitoring. |

> [!important]  
> **CDI trigger**: "**mass effect**" or "**midline shift**" on imaging with no diagnosis in the note. Query for **traumatic brain compression** and whether **[[herniation]]** is present.

### Manifestations & Symptom Burden  
- **Headache and dizziness:** common after intracranial injury and reported during follow-up.
- **Cognitive changes:** memory and attention problems that may support **[[F06.70]]** or **[[F06.71]]**.
- **Motor or sensory deficits:** coded when documented as a result of the injury.
- **Fatigue and mood changes:** coded only when the provider links them to the injury.

> [!tip]  
> **Symptoms** integral to the brain injury are **not coded separately**. **Report** a documented **neurocognitive disorder** with the use additional note at **S06**.<sup>2</sup>

| **DRG without CC/MCC** | **950** — Aftercare without CC/MCC |
| **Dx role** | Groups to 949/950<sup>3</sup> |

Under the **Code first note**, the underlying **TBI** drives the principal diagnosis and the **DRG** on an inpatient claim. As a secondary diagnosis, confirm **CC/MCC** status in your grouper before counting on severity. In contrast to **[[S06.A0XD]]**, the initial-encounter code **[[S06.A0XA]]** is the one expected to carry severity.

NCD/LCD:
- No **NCD** or **LCD** with a diagnosis-specific coverage rule for **[[S06.A0XD]]** was found.
- Follow-up imaging such as **[[70450]]** and **[[70551]]** falls under the CT and MRI **NCDs** (**220.1 and 220.2**) and your **MAC's LCDs**, so check them for covered diagnoses and device restrictions.
- Check your **MAC**'s rules for **cognitive therapy** (**[[97129]]**) and **neuropsychological testing** (**[[96132]]**) before billing.

### NCCI Bundling Considerations  
**E/M services** on the same day as a separately reportable service need modifier **[[-25]]** and documentation of a separate service. Imaging reported globally includes both the **technical** and **professional components**, so use **[[-26]]** or **[[-TC]]** only when they are split. Confirm current edits in the NCCI tables.

## 💊 Coding Scenarios and Examples  

### Example 1
> **Clinical Scenario:**  
> A patient treated for a traumatic subdural hemorrhage with brain compression returns for a neurosurgery follow-up. Repeat CT shows resolving mass effect with no herniation, and the provider documents routine healing.

| **Field** | **Code** | **Rationale** |
|---|---|---|
| CPT | [[99214]] | Established patient visit, moderate MDM. |
| CPT 2 | [[70450]] | Follow-up CT head without contrast. |
| PDx | [[S06.5X0D]] | Underlying TBI sequenced first per the Code first note. |
| Dx 2 | [[S06.A0XD]] | Brain compression without herniation, healing phase. |

> [!tip]  
> The Code first note puts the underlying TBI before [[S06.A0XD]]. CDI: document the TBI type, loss of consciousness status and healing status.

### Example 2
> **Clinical Scenario:**  
> A patient recovering from a traumatic brain injury with compression is referred for cognitive therapy. The therapist treats attention and memory deficits.

| **Field** | **Code** | **Rationale** |
|---|---|---|
| CPT | [[97129]] | Cognitive function intervention, initial 15 minutes. |
| PDx | [[S06.A0XD]] | Healing-phase brain compression, with the underlying TBI also reported. |
| Dx 2 | [[F06.70]] | Mild neurocognitive disorder, only if the provider documents it. |

> [!tip]  
> Report the underlying TBI code too, per the Code first note. CDI: document the link between the cognitive deficits and the injury.

### Example 3
> **Clinical Scenario:**  
> A patient returns for an MRI to reassess brain compression after trauma. The imaging facility bills the technical component and the radiologist bills the interpretation.

| **Field** | **Code** | **Rationale** |
|---|---|---|
| CPT | [[70551]]-[[-TC]] | Facility technical component of the MRI. |
| CPT 2 | [[70551]]-[[-26]] | Radiologist's professional interpretation. |
| PDx | [[S06.A0XD]] | Follow-up of healing brain compression, linked to the underlying TBI. |

> [!tip]  
> Check the MRI NCD and device restrictions before scanning. CDI: the order should document the injury and the reason for follow-up imaging.

## ⚠️ Coding Pitfalls and Tips  
- **Pitfall 1**: Reporting **[[S06.A0XD]]** without the underlying TBI; **Tips**: **Code** first the **S06.2-, S06.3-, S06.5- or S06.6- injury** per the note.<sup>2</sup>
- **Pitfall 2**: Using **[[S06.A0XA]]** for every post-discharge visit; **Tips**: Use **[[S06.A0XD]]** once active treatment is complete and care is routine healing.
- **Pitfall 3**: Choosing **[[compression]]** without **[[herniation]]** when **[[herniation]]** is documented; **Tips**: Use **[[S06.A1XD]]** when the provider documents S06.A0XD[[.
- **Pitfall 4**: Using **head injury NOS** (**S09.90**) with a specific intracranial code; **Tips**: **S09.90** is an **Excludes1** at **S06**, so report only the specific injury.<sup>2</sup>
- **Pitfall 5**: **Missing** the Use additional note for **neurocognitive disorder**; **Tips**: Add **[[F06.70]]** or **[[F06.71]]** when the provider documents it.
- **Pitfall 6**: Leaving off the **X** placeholder; **Tips**: **[[S06.A0XD]]** needs the **X** in the 6th position to reach **7** characters.
