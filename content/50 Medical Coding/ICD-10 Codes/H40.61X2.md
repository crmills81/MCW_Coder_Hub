# ICD-10-CM H40.61X2 – Glaucoma secondary to drugs, right eye, moderate stage

### Primary Diagnosis
- **H40.61X2 – Glaucoma secondary to drugs, right eye, moderate stage:**
  **Detailed Explanation:** This specific code describes an elevation in [[intraocular]] pressure (IOP) that has caused [[glaucomatous]] optic neuropathy in the **right eye**, directly resulting from the use of a medication (**most commonly topical, inhaled, or systemic corticosteroids, or anticholinergics**). 
  - **"Moderate Stage" Clinical Definition:** According to ICD-10-CM guidelines and the American Academy of Ophthalmology (**AAO**), "moderate stage" means there are documented optic nerve abnormalities consistent with glaucoma, AND visual field abnormalities in one hemifield that are *not* within 5 degrees of fixation. 
  - **The "X" Placeholder:** The 6th character 'X' is a required placeholder to allow the staging character ('2') to sit in the 7th position.

### Mandatory Sequencing & Related Codes (Top 6 Options)
*ICD-10-CM Guidelines strictly dictate that for "Glaucoma secondary to drugs," you must **first** code the adverse effect of the drug.*

1. **[[T38.0X5A]] – Adverse effect of glucocorticoids and synthetic analogues, initial encounter:** Often the required primary/sequenced-first code, as steroids are the most common culprit for drug-induced glaucoma.
2. **[[T49.5X5A]] – Adverse effect of ophthalmological drugs and preparations, initial encounter:** Sequenced first if eye drops (like steroid drops) caused the glaucoma.
3. **[[H40.61X1]] – Glaucoma secondary to drugs, right eye, mild stage:** Alternative if the patient has no visual field defects yet.
4. **[[H40.61X3]] – Glaucoma secondary to drugs, right eye, severe stage:** Alternative if visual field defects fall within 5 degrees of fixation.
5. **[[H40.62X2]] – Glaucoma secondary to drugs, left eye, moderate stage:** Alternative for laterality.
6. **[[H40.63X2]] – Glaucoma secondary to drugs, bilateral, moderate stage:** Alternative if both eyes are affected at a moderate stage. *(Note: If stages differ between eyes, code each eye separately).*

# CPT/HCPCS Code(s) (Commonly Associated Procedures)

When treating moderate drug-induced glaucoma, physicians typically perform advanced diagnostic imaging or surgical interventions.

### 1. Diagnostic Imaging
- **[[92133]] – Scanning computerized ophthalmic diagnostic imaging (SCODI), posterior segment, with interpretation and report, unilateral or bilateral; optic nerve:**
  - **Explanation:** Uses OCT technology to measure the retinal nerve fiber layer (RNFL) thickness to monitor glaucoma progression.
  - **wRVU:** 0.88
  - **Global Period:** XXX (Global concept does not apply)
  - **Assistant Payable:** No

### 2. Visual Field Testing
- **[[92083]] – Visual field examination, unilateral or bilateral, with interpretation and report; extended examination:**
  - **Explanation:** Essential for proving the "moderate stage" requirement of visual field defects not within 5 degrees of fixation.
  - **wRVU:** 1.05
  - **Global Period:** XXX
  - **Assistant Payable:** No

### 3. Surgical Intervention (First-Line)
- **[[65855]] – Trabeculoplasty by laser surgery, 1 or more sessions (defined treatment series):**
  - **Explanation:** SLT or ALT laser applied to the trabecular meshwork of the right eye to increase fluid outflow and lower IOP when stopping the offending drug isn't enough.
  - **wRVU:** 3.86
  - **Global Period:** 010 (10-day minor surgery global period)
  - **Assistant Payable:** No (Not traditionally payable for laser procedures)

# Exclusives/Inclusives (Bundling & NCCI Edits)

### Diagnostic Edits (92133 / 92083)
- **Mutually Exclusive:** **92133** (SCODI optic nerve) cannot be billed on the same day as **[[92134]]** (SCODI retina) for the same eye. You must choose the code that matches the primary pathology. Since H40.61X2 is glaucoma, [[92133]] is the correct choice.
- **Mutually Exclusive:** **92133** is generally bundled into extended ophthalmoscopy (**[[92222]]/[[92223]]**) in many payer policies unless performed for distinct, separate clinical reasons (which requires modifier [[-59]] or [[-XU]], though rarely applicable for the same disease process).

### Surgical Edits (65855)
- **Inclusives:** Topical anesthesia, positioning of the laser lens, and the immediate post-operative IOP check are included in the laser surgery (65855) and cannot be billed separately.
- **E/M Bundling:** Since [[65855]] has a 010 global period, an Evaluation and Management (E/M) or Eye Code (920xx) billed on the same day must have a **Modifier [[-25]]**, and the documentation must prove the E/M was a *significant, separately identifiable* service from the pre-operative workup of the laser.

# Detailed Clinical Context & Documentation Tips
- **The "Code First" Rule:** If you bill H40.61X2 without a corresponding 'T' code (e.g., [[T38.0X5A]]) indicating *which* drug caused the adverse effect, your claim will likely be denied by payers using strict ICD-10-CM sequencing edits.
- **Stage Progression:** If a patient progresses from moderate (2) to severe (3) during the course of an inpatient admission or prolonged treatment period, guidelines instruct you to assign the code for the **highest stage** documented.
- **Laterality Mismatch:** Ensure the procedural modifier (e.g., CPT 65855[[-RT]] for right eye) directly matches the laterality character in the ICD-10-CM code (H40.6**1** = Right).
