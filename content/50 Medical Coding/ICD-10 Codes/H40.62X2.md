# 🧬 ICD-10-CM H40.62X2 — Glaucoma Secondary to Drugs, Left Eye, Moderate Stage

> [!success] Billable Code Confirmed — 7 Characters Complete
> **ICD-10 CM [[H40.62X2]]** is a valid, billable 7-character ICD-10-CM code for FY2025. Structure: `H40` (**glaucoma**) + `.6` (**secondary to drugs**) + `2` (**left eye**) + `X` (**structural placeholder**) + `2` **(moderate stage**). Complete and current.

> - ❌ `H40.6` — 4-character header — non-billable

## 🔍 Code Description

**ICD-10 CM [[H40.62X2]]** classifies **glaucoma secondary to drug therapy, left eye, at the moderate stage** — established drug-induced glaucomatous optic neuropathy in the left eye with a **Humphrey Visual Field 24-2 mean deviation (MD) between -6 dB and -12 dB**, outside the central 5° of fixation. The underlying mechanism, drug exposures, IOP dynamics, and T-code framework are **identical** to the right-eye counterpart [[H40.61X2]] — the sole coding distinction is the **6th character: "2" for left eye vs. "1" for right eye** and the corresponding **-LT procedure modifier** on all unilateral CPT codes.

Moderate stage is the **clinical inflection point** in drug-induced glaucoma management — the stage where physicians must weigh medical escalation against surgical intervention, where SLT eligibility peaks, and where the MIGS conversation becomes urgent. Central vision remains intact, but the field loss is significant enough that progression to [[H40.62X3]] (**severe**) carries a real risk of functional disability.

## 🔑 The Only Thing That Changed — Laterality

> [!important] Right Eye → Left Eye: The One-Character Shift

| Element | Right Eye | **Left Eye** |
|---|---|---|
| **Code** | [[H40.61X2]] | **[[H40.62X2]]** |
| **6th character** | `1` = Right | **`2` = Left** |
| **CPT modifier** | `-RT` | **`-LT`** |
| **Tonometry documentation** | IOP OD | **IOP OS** |
| **HVF laterality** | 24-2 OD | **24-2 OS** |
| **OCT laterality** | RNFL OD | **RNFL OS** |
| **Stage criteria** | Identical | **Identical** |
| **T-code** | T38.0X5X | **T38.0X5X** |

> [!tip] LT Modifier Is Non-Negotiable for Left Eye CPT Codes
> Every unilateral CPT code billed for left-eye glaucoma services requires the **-LT modifier**. Omitting it on a claim for a unilateral procedure defaults to "**unspecified side**" in most payer systems and will generate a mismatch with [[H40.62X2]] (**which explicitly specifies left eye**). Apply LT to:
> - [[92083]][[-LT]] — HVF left eye
> - [[92133]]-LT — OCT RNFL left eye
> - [[65855]]-LT — SLT left eye
> - [[66170]]-LT / [[66172]]-LT — trabeculectomy left eye
> - Any other lateralized ophthalmic procedure

## 📊 Asymmetric Bilateral Glaucoma — The Most Common Real-World Scenario

Drug-induced glaucoma (**particularly steroid-induced**) frequently presents **asymmetrically** — one eye at a different stage than the other. This is where laterality-specific coding earns its keep in the record.

### Common Asymmetric Stage Combinations

| Right Eye | Left Eye | Codes to Assign |
|---|---|---|
| Mild — [[H40.61X1]] | **Moderate — [[H40.62X2]]** ← | [[H40.61X1]] + [[H40.62X2]] + T-code |
| **Moderate — [[H40.61X2]]** | **Moderate — [[H40.62X2]]** | Consider [[H40.63X2]] (bilateral moderate) if documented bilaterally |
| Moderate — [[H40.61X2]] | **Moderate — [[H40.62X2]]** | If stages truly equal → [[H40.63X2]] preferred |
| Severe — [[H40.61X3]] | **Moderate — [[H40.62X2]]** ← | [[H40.61X3]] + [[H40.62X2]] + T-code |
| Indeterminate — [[H40.61X4]] | **Moderate — [[H40.62X2]]** ← | [[H40.61X4]] + [[H40.62X2]] + T-code |

> [!important] Bilateral Code vs. Two Laterality Codes — The Decision Rule
> **Use [[H40.63X2]]** (**bilateral, moderate**) ONLY when BOTH eyes are at the same moderate stage AND the physician documents bilateral disease of equal stage.
> **Use [[H40.61X2]] + [[H40.62X2]]** when the eyes are at different stages — bilateral code is not appropriate for asymmetric staging.
> **Use [[H40.62X2]] alone** when only the left eye has glaucoma documented and the right eye is normal or has a different condition.

---

### Scenario — Asymmetric Bilateral SIG, Right Mild / Left Moderate

> **Clinical Vignette:** A 61-year-old female with steroid-induced [[glaucoma]] secondary to chronic inhaled budesonide presents for 4-month follow-up. IOP OD: 18 mmHg on latanoprost. IOP OS: 22 mmHg on latanoprost + timolol — target not met. HVF 24-2: OD MD -3.8 dB (**mild, stable**). OS MD -8.1 dB (**moderate — progressed from -6.4 dB at last visit**). OCT RNFL OS: progressive superior thinning. Physician: "Steroid-induced glaucoma — right eye mild, stable. Left eye moderate — progression on OCT and VF. Left eye not at target IOP. Adding brimonidine OS. Discussing SLT OS at next visit."

**ICD-10-CM:**
- [[H40.61X1]] — Glaucoma secondary to drugs, **right eye, mild** *(OD MD -3.8 dB, stable)*
- **[[H40.62X2]]** — Glaucoma secondary to drugs, **left eye, moderate** *(OS MD -8.1 dB, progressed)*
- **[[T38.0X5D]]** — Adverse effect of glucocorticoids, subsequent encounter *(one T-code covers both eyes — same drug)*

**CPT:**
- [[92014]] — Comprehensive exam, established
- [[92083]] — HVF bilateral *(both eyes tested — no modifier)*
- [[92133]][[-LT]] — OCT RNFL, left eye *(progression monitoring on problem eye)*

> [!tip] One T-Code Covers Both Eyes When Same Drug Is the Cause
> When bilateral drug-induced glaucoma is caused by the same corticosteroid exposure, a **single [[T38.0X5D]]** covers both laterality codes — you do not need to list it twice. The T-code identifies the causative drug for the encounter; the two laterality-specific H40.6x codes identify each eye's disease and stage independently.
