# 🧬 ICD-10 CM S27.2XXD — Traumatic Hemopneumothorax, Subsequent Encounter

> [!success] Billable Code Confirmed  
> **ICD-10 CM [[S27.2XXD]]** is a **7-character code**: **S27.2** (**traumatic hemopneumothorax**), **XX** (placeholder characters), and **D** (subsequent encounter). It is complete and valid for **FY2026 claims**.<sup>1</sup>

> [!warning] Non-Billable Parent Codes  
> **S27.2** (**Traumatic hemopneumothorax**) is a header and needs the **7th character**. **S27** (Injury of other and unspecified intrathoracic organs) is also non-billable.<sup>1</sup>

> [!important] Clinical Context  
> The **7th character D** applies after active treatment is complete and the patient is in the healing or recovery phase, such as a **follow-up after chest tube removal**. If treatment is still active, use **[[S27.2XXA]]** instead.<sup>3</sup>

> [!danger] Code Classification  
> This is an **ICD-10-CM injury diagnosis code** (**Chapter 19**). The **7th character** shows the episode of care, not the clinical severity.

---

## 🔍 Code Description  
**Traumatic [[hemopneumothorax]]** is **blood and air in the pleural space after chest trauma**, such as **blunt injury**, **rib fractures** or **penetrating injury**. The 7th character **D** on **[[S27.2XXD]]** marks routine care during healing, including follow-up exams, repeat imaging and medication adjustments. Injury codes use the **D 7th character** instead of **Z** aftercare codes.<sup>3</sup>

Use **[[S27.2XXD]]** when there is no active setback. If the provider changes the plan because of a complication or recurrence, the encounter becomes active again and **[[S27.2XXA]]** may apply.<sup>3</sup> Residual effects after recovery use **[[S27.2XXS]]**. Always code any associated open wound with **S21.-** (**Code also at S27**).<sup>4</sup>

> [!example] Episode of Care Drives the Code  
> The same injury can be reported with **A, D or S** depending on the phase of care. Choosing **D** instead of **A** can change the **DRG** and payer edits, because **A** is for active treatment.

> [!tip]  
> Match the **7th character** to the encounter's purpose, not to the time since injury. A follow-up for healing is **D**, while a new procedure to treat a retained collection is active treatment.

## ✅ Includes  
- **[[Hemopneumothorax]] from trauma:** both blood and air are documented, which differs from **[[S27.1XXD]]** (**[[hemothorax]]** only) and **[[S27.0XXD]]** (**[[pneumothorax]]** only).
- **Healing-phase follow-up:** routine visits and imaging after chest tube removal or discharge.
- **Subsequent encounter with a recovering injury:** repeat care no matter how many visits occur, as long as it stays routine.<sup>3</sup>

## ❌ Excludes  

### Excludes 1  
- No **Excludes1 note** is listed at **S27.2**.<sup>5</sup> The **Excludes1** for spontaneous **pneumothorax** (**J93.-**) sits at **S27.0** and should not be assumed for **S27.2**.

> [!danger]  
> The most common error is assuming **Excludes1 rules** from another **S27 subcategory** apply here. Read the note at the exact category level before dropping a code.

### Excludes 2  
- **Injury of cervical esophagus and cervical trachea (S10-S19):** an **Excludes2** at category **S27**, so both can be reported when a cervical injury is also documented.<sup>4</sup>
- **S21.- (open wound of thorax):** a **Code** also note at **S27**, so add the open wound code when present.<sup>4</sup>

## 📋 Clinical Overview  

### Hemopneumothorax vs Hemothorax vs Pneumothorax  
Traumatic injury of the **pleural space** is split by what fills it: **air, blood or both**. The code must match what the provider documents, and **D, A or S** then shows the episode of care.

| **Feature** | **[[S27.2XXD]]** | **Related [[S27.1XXD]]** | **Related [[S27.0XXD]]** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pleural content** | Blood and air. | Blood only. | Air only. |
| **Typical management** | Chest tube drainage, then imaging follow-up. | Drainage of blood, possible retained clot follow-up. | Observation or tube drainage, then repeat imaging. |
| **Subsequent-encounter use** | Healing-phase care for the combined injury. | Healing-phase care for the hemothorax. | Healing-phase care for the pneumothorax. |

> [!important]  
> **CDI trigger**: documentation of "**[[hemothorax]]**" and "**[[pneumothorax]]**" separately after trauma. Query whether the provider means **traumatic hemopneumothorax** so one combined code is reported.

### Manifestations & Symptom Burden  
- **Chest pain:** pleuritic pain from the injury or healing rib fractures.
- **[[dyspnea|Dyspnea]]:** reduced lung expansion from residual fluid or air.
- **Reduced breath sounds:** found on exam during follow-up.
- **Residual findings on imaging:** small collections or pleural thickening on follow-up films.

> [!tip]  
> Symptoms integral to the **injury** are not coded separately. A complication such as **[[empyema]]** or **retained [[hemothorax]]** is coded and may restart active treatment.

| **Field** | **Value** |
|---|---|
| **MDC** | MDC 23 |
| **DRG with CC/MCC** | **949** — Aftercare with CC/MCC |
| **DRG without CC/MCC** | **950** — Aftercare without CC/MCC |
| **Dx role** | Principal diagnosis groups to 949/950<sup>2</sup> |
| **CC/MCC status as secondary** | Not a CC or MCC<sup>1</sup> |

As a principal diagnosis, **[[S27.2XXD]]** groups to the **Aftercare DRGs**, and a **CC** or **MCC** elsewhere on the claim moves it from **950** to **949**.<sup>2</sup> It adds no severity as a secondary diagnosis, unlike **[[S27.2XXA]]**, which carries the severity of an active injury. It is exempt from **POA reporting**.<sup>1</sup>

**NCD/LCD:**

- Check your **MAC's LCDs** for chest imaging (**[[71046]]**) and E/M follow-up before billing.

### NCCI Bundling Considerations  
**Imaging guidance** is included in **[[32555]]**, so do not add a separate ultrasound code. An **E/M** on the same day as a procedure needs modifier **[[-25]]** and a separately documented service. Confirm current edits in the **NCCI tables**.

## 💊 Coding Scenarios and Examples  

### Example 1
> **Clinical Scenario:**  
> A patient treated for traumatic hemopneumothorax after a fall returns to clinic two weeks after chest tube removal. The chest X-ray shows resolving findings and the provider documents routine healing.

| Field | Code | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| CPT | [[99213]]-[[-25]] | Follow-up E/M, with modifier [[-25]] only if a separate procedure was performed the same day. |
| CPT 2 | [[71046]] | Two-view chest X-ray to confirm resolution. |
| PDx | [[S27.2XXD]] | Healing-phase care after active treatment. |

> [!tip]  
> Drop modifier [[-25]] if no procedure was done. CDI: document that treatment is complete and the injury is healing.

### Example 2
> **Clinical Scenario:**  
> A patient with a recovering traumatic hemopneumothorax and healing right rib fracture is seen for a routine check. Imaging shows no new collection.

| Field | Code | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| CPT | [[99214]] | Established patient visit, moderate MDM. |
| CPT 2 | [[71045]] | Single-view chest X-ray. |
| PDx | [[S27.2XXD]] | Routine healing-phase care for the hemopneumothorax. |

> [!tip]  
> Add **[[S22.31XD]]** for the healing rib fracture. CDI: document each injury's healing status.

### Example 3
> **Clinical Scenario:**  
> At a follow-up visit, imaging shows a retained pleural collection and the provider performs an ultrasound-guided thoracentesis.

| Field | Code | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| CPT | [[32555]]-[[-25]] | Thoracentesis with imaging guidance, with modifier [[-25]] on the separate E/M. |
| PDx | [[S27.2XXA]] | New active treatment may support the initial-encounter 7th character, based on provider documentation. |

> [!tip]  
> When a setback requires active treatment, the encounter is no longer routine healing. CDI: query whether the provider is documenting a complication or recurrence.

## ⚠️ Coding Pitfalls and Tips  
- **Pitfall 1**: Using **[[S27.2XXA]]** for every visit after discharge; **Tips**: Use **[[S27.2XXD]]** once active treatment is complete and care is routine healing.
- **Pitfall 2**: Using **[[Z09]]** instead of the **7th character D** for an injury follow-up; **Tips**: Injuries use the **7th character**, not a **Z aftercare code**.
- **Pitfall 3**: Reporting separate **[[hemothorax]]** and **[[pneumothorax]]** codes together; **Tips**: Use the combined **[[hemopneumothorax]] code** when both are documented.
- **Pitfall 4**: Forgetting the open wound code when the chest was penetrated; **Tips**: Add **S21.-** per the Code also note at **S27**.
- **Pitfall 5**: Expecting severity credit from **[[S27.2XXD]]** as a secondary diagnosis; **Tips**: It is not a **CC** or **MCC**, so capture severity from other documented conditions.
- **Pitfall 6**: Dropping the placeholder **X** characters; **Tips**: Keep both **X** placeholders so the code stays at **7** characters.
