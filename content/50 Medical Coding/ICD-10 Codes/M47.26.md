# 🧬 ICD-10 CM M47.26 — Other Spondylosis with Radiculopathy, Lumbar Region

> [!success] Billable Code Confirmed  
> **ICD-10 CM [[M47.26]]** is a fully billable, specific **5-character diagnosis code** that represents other **[[spondylosis]]** with **[[radiculopathy]]** in the lumbar spine.<sup>1</sup> The prefix **M47** indicates **[[spondylosis]]**, subcategory .**2** designates associated **[[radiculopathy]]**, and the **fifth character 6** specifies the **lumbar vertebral column**. Because the code has achieved the complete level of anatomical and clinical specificity required by the **official guidelines**, no additional placeholder or seventh characters are needed.<sup>1</sup>

> [!warning] Non-Billable Parent Codes  
> **M47** (**[[Spondylosis]]**): This **3-character category code** is non-billable because it lacks vital clinical details regarding associated **neurological manifestations** and exact vertebral anatomical levels.<sup>1</sup>  
> **M47.2** (**Other spondylosis with radiculopathy**): This **4-character subcategory code** is **non-billable** because it requires a **fifth character** to define the specific spinal segment involved.<sup>1</sup>

> [!important] Clinical Context  
> **Code** selection requires documentation confirming degenerative changes in the lumbar spine (**such as facet joint [[hypertrophy]] or [[osteophytosis]]**) directly causing **nerve root irritation** or **[[compression]]**.<sup>1,3</sup> When **lumbar [[radiculopathy]]** occurs in the absence of documented **[[spondylosis]]** or stems purely from an **acute intervertebral disc displacement**, alternative codes such as **[[M54.16]]** or **[[M51.16]]** must be reported instead.<sup>1</sup>

> [!danger] Code Classification  
> **[[M47.26]]** is exclusively a clinical diagnosis code located within **Chapter 13** of **ICD-10-CM** and must never be billed as an **intervention** or **procedural code**. When diagnostic or therapeutic procedures are executed to treat this pathology, appropriate **CPT** or **ICD-10-PCS codes** must be submitted alongside **[[M47.26]]**.<sup>2</sup>

## 🔍 Code Description  

**Lumbar [[spondylosis]] with [[radiculopathy]]**, designated by **[[M47.26]]**, represents a **progressive degenerative disorder** of the **lumbar spine** where structural **[[deterioration]]** of the vertebrae and facet joints results in **[[compression]]** or **[[inflammation]]** of exiting lumbar spinal nerve roots.<sup>1,3</sup> This chronic pathology encompasses disc space narrowing, **[[osteophyte]]** formation along the **vertebral margins**, and **facet arthropathy** that compromise **neuroforaminal patency**. Patients present with chronic low back discomfort paired with **radiating pain, [[paresthesias]], or focal neurological deficits** following specific **lumbar dermatomes**, differentiating this condition from **axial degenerative back pain** reported under **[[M47.816]]**.<sup>1</sup>

From an administrative and clinical coding perspective, **[[M47.26]]** functions as a combination diagnosis code that establishes medical necessity for interventional pain therapies and surgical spine interventions.<sup>1,4</sup> **Payers** and **Medicare Administrative Contractors (MACs)** reference this diagnosis to substantiate coverage for procedures including **fluoroscopy-guided transforaminal epidural steroid injections** reported under CPT **[[64483]]** and s**urgical decompressions coded** under **CPT** **[[63030]]**. Complete clinical documentation requires imaging confirmation of **lumbar degenerative disease** alongside corresponding physical examination findings to satisfy national utilization criteria and withstand payer post-payment audits.<sup>4</sup>

> [!example] Lumbar vs. Lumbosacral Anatomic Specificity
> Selecting **[[M47.26]]** rather than non-radicular **[[M47.816]]** or unspecified site codes is essential for securing **prior authorization** for targeted **transforaminal epidural injections**. Payers routinely deny **interventional injection authorizations** when billed against axial pain codes that lack verified radicular involvement.<sup>4</sup>

> [!tip] 
> Review **electrodiagnostic** and **neuroimaging documentation** to verify radicular involvement before code assignment. If **lumbar degenerative changes** are present without radiating radicular signs or symptoms, assign **[[M47.816]]** to avoid upcoding audit risks.<sup>1</sup>

## ✅ Includes  

- **Lumbar spondylosis with radiculitis**: Describes degenerative vertebral osteophytes and facet joint arthrosis producing secondary **[[inflammation]]** of lumbar nerve roots.<sup>1</sup>
- **Degenerative facet arthrosis of lumbar region with radiculopathy**: Encompasses **[[hypertrophy]]** of lumbar zygapophyseal joints that impinges exiting lumbar nerve roots within the neural foramina.<sup>1,3</sup>
- **Lumbar spondylogenic nerve root compression**: Captures structural bony canal narrowing or osseous encroachment irritating lumbar spinal nerve roots.<sup>1</sup>

## ❌ Excludes  

### Excludes 1  

- **Radiculopathy, lumbar region** (**[[M54.16]]**): Code **[[M54.16]]** includes an explicit **Excludes1 instructional note** directing coders to **[[M47.26]]** when **[[radiculopathy]]** is accompanied by **[[spondylosis]]**.<sup>1</sup>
- Other **[[spondylosis]]** with **[[myelopathy]]**, lumbar region (**[[M47.16]]**): **Spinal cord [[compression]]** involving **[[myelopathy]]** takes clinical sequencing precedence and cannot be dual-coded with **[[radiculopathy]]** under the same **[[spondylosis]]** condition.<sup>1</sup>
- **Nerve root and plexus compressions** in diseases classified elsewhere (**[[G55]]**): **Nerve root [[compression]]** directly caused by **spinal [[spondylosis]]** must be assigned directly to **[[M47.26]]** rather than secondary manifestation category **[[G55]]**.<sup>1</sup>

> [!danger] 
> The most frequent **Excludes1 error** occurs when coders submit **[[M47.816]]** (**[[spondylosis]] without [[radiculopathy]]**) concurrently with **[[M54.16]]** (**lumbar [[radiculopathy]]**). **Official guidelines** dictate that these symptoms collapse into single combination code **[[M47.26]]**, and unbundling them triggers automated claim denials.<sup>1</sup>

### Excludes 2  

- **[[Spondylosis]] without [[myelopathy]] or [[radiculopathy]], lumbar region** (**[[M47.816]]**): Represents an **Excludes2 relationship** where **non-radicular [[spondylosis]]** may be coded if distinct, non-compressed vertebral segments are independently documented.<sup>1</sup>
- **Spinal [[stenosis]], lumbar region without neurogenic [[claudication]]** (**[[M48.061]]**) and with **neurogenic claudication** (**[[M48.062]]**): Central spinal canal **[[stenosis]]** represents a distinct diagnostic entity under **category M48** and can be reported concurrently when both conditions coexist.<sup>1</sup>
- **Intervertebral disc disorders with radiculopathy, lumbar region** (**[[M51.16]]**): **[[Radiculopathy]]** arising secondary to an **acute intervertebral disc herniation** rather than **chronic spondylotic bony** changes may be coded when both separate pathologies are clinically confirmed.<sup>1</sup>

## 📋 Clinical Overview  

### CLINICAL AND DIAGNOSTIC DIFFERENTIATION  

Accurate diagnosis of **lumbar spondylosis with radiculopathy** requires identifying degenerative spinal architectural changes that mechanically compromise or biochemically irritate exiting **lumbar nerve roots**. Clinicians must differentiate this mixed **osseous-neurological entity** from isolated **axial back pain** and **disc displacements** to select appropriate **non-operative, interventional, or surgical care**. Diagnostic imaging including **magnetic resonance imaging (MRI)** or **computed tomography (CT)** confirms **neuroforaminal [[stenosis]]** correlating with dermatomal neurological findings.<sup>3</sup>

| **Feature** | **[[M47.26]]** | **Related [[M47.816]]** | **Related [[M51.16]]** |
|:---:|---|---|---|
| **Etiology** | Chronic facet arthropathy and osteophytosis causing secondary lumbar nerve root compression.<sup>1,3</sup> | Degenerative vertebral joint arthrosis without mechanical encroachment on neural structures.<sup>1</sup> | Primary lumbar disc herniation, extrusion, or rupture producing direct neural impingement.<sup>1</sup> |
| **Symptom Distribution** | Axial lumbar aching accompanied by dermatomal pain radiating below the knee (L1–L5).<sup>3</sup> | Localized axial low back stiffness and pain without radicular leg symptoms or motor deficits.<sup>3</sup> | Sharp, lancinating radicular leg pain often worsened by forward flexion and straight leg raising.<sup>3</sup> |
| **Diagnostic Findings** | Radiographs and MRI demonstrating facet hypertrophy, osteophytes, and neuroforaminal narrowing.<sup>3</sup> | Imaging demonstrating disc space height loss and facet degeneration without neural compression.<sup>3</sup> | MRI revealing focal soft-tissue intervertebral disc displacement compressing nerve roots.<sup>3</sup> |

> [!important] 
> **Clinical documentation improvement (CDI) specialists** must query providers when medical records list "**[[sciatica]]**" or "**lumbar [[radiculopathy]]**" in patients with **chronic lumbar [[spondylosis]]** without establishing an explicit link. Querying enables documentation of "**lumbar [[spondylosis]] with [[radiculopathy]]**," ensuring compliant capture of combination code **[[M47.26]]**.

---

### Manifestations & Symptom Burden  

- **Dermatomal Radicular Pain**: Burning, sharp, or electric pain radiating down the buttock, posterior/lateral thigh, lower leg, or foot in an L4, L5, or S1 pattern.<sup>3</sup>
- **Sensory Paresthesias**: Tingling, numbness, or subjective loss of sensation across corresponding lower extremity dermatomes.<sup>3</sup>
- **Motor Weakness**: Focal motor deficits including diminished quadriceps extension (L3/L4) or foot drop and great toe extension weakness (L5).<sup>3</sup>
- **Reflex Changes**: Hypoactive or absent deep tendon reflexes, particularly the patellar reflex (L4) or Achilles reflex (S1).<sup>3</sup>
- **Axial Lumbar Pain**: Chronic aching and spinal stiffness aggravated by spinal extension, prolonged weight-bearing, or facet loading maneuvers.<sup>3</sup>

> [!tip] 
> Do **not** report **standalone symptom codes for lower back pain** (**[[M54.50]]**) or **[[sciatica]]** (**[[M54.30]]**) when **[[M47.26]]** is confirmed, as these manifestations are integral to the combination code.<sup>1</sup>

When a patient is admitted for **non-operative management of intractable lumbar [[radiculopathy]] secondary to [[spondylosis]]**, **[[M47.26]]** serves as the principal diagnosis and maps directly to **MS-DRG 551 or 552** depending on the presence of a qualifying **Major Complication or Comorbidity (MCC)**. In cases where the patient undergoes an **inpatient surgical decompression** or **spinal fusion**, the **MS-DRG grouper logic shifts** assignment to higher-weighted **surgical DRGs** (**such as MS-DRGs 453-458 or 518-520**). Coders must verify surgical operative notes to ascertain whether **[[decompression]]** alone was performed or if spinal instrumentation and fusion were placed.

- Under **Medicare Local Coverage Determinations** (**e.g., LCD L36920 for Epidural Steroid Injections**), **[[M47.26]]** is listed as a covered primary diagnosis supporting the medical necessity of **lumbar transforaminal epidural steroid injections**.<sup>4</sup>
- **Coverage guidelines** mandate documented failure of at least four to six weeks of conservative non-surgical therapy (**including physical therapy or pharmacotherapy**), unless **rapid neurological deterioration** is documented, prior to approving elective interventional injections or surgical procedures.<sup>4</sup>

### NCCI Bundling Considerations  

Under the **National Correct Coding Initiative (NCCI) edit tables**, **fluoroscopic guidance** (**CPT [[77003]]**) is inherently bundled into **transforaminal epidural injections** (**[[64483]]** and **[[64484]]**) and cannot be unbundled or billed separately.<sup>2</sup> Furthermore, an **evaluation and management service** submitted on the same date as an **epidural steroid injection** requires modifier **[[-25]]** and must be supported by clinical documentation reflecting assessment and decision-making distinct from pre-procedural checks.<sup>2</sup>

## 💊 Coding Scenarios and Examples  

### Example 1  

A 64-year-old male presents to the outpatient spine clinic with intractable right L5 radicular pain refractory to eight weeks of physical therapy and pharmacotherapy. An MRI of the lumbar spine confirms severe facet arthropathy and neuroforaminal narrowing at L5–S1 with right L5 nerve root impingement. The physician performs a fluoroscopy-guided right-sided L5 transforaminal epidural steroid injection.

| **Field** | **Code** | **Rationale** |
|---|---|---|
| CPT | [[64483]]-[[-RT]] | Unilateral transforaminal epidural injection in the lumbar spine, initial level, performed on the right side under fluoroscopic guidance.<sup>2</sup> |
| PDx | [[M47.26]] | Primary diagnosis code for other spondylosis with radiculopathy, lumbar region, substantiating procedural medical necessity.<sup>1,4</sup> |

> [!tip] 
> Code [[M47.26]] is sequenced as the primary diagnosis because it reflects the structural condition directly treated by the injection. Provider documentation must note imaging corroboration of spondylotic narrowing matching the patient's radicular dermatome to satisfy Medicare local coverage rules.<sup>4</sup>

### Example 2  

A 58-year-old female with known degenerative lumbar spondylosis presents for an evaluation of worsening left lower extremity numbness, burning pain, and 4/5 motor weakness in the left extensor hallucis longus. The physician conducts a detailed neurological evaluation requiring moderate medical decision-making and decides to proceed with an urgent multi-level injection. During the same session, the physician performs left-sided L4 and L5 transforaminal epidural steroid injections with fluoroscopic guidance.

| **Field** | **Code** | **Rationale** |
|---|---|---|
| CPT | [[99214]]-[[-25]] | Established patient office visit involving moderate medical decision-making; modifier [[-25]] differentiates the evaluation from procedural care.<sup>2</sup> |
| CPT 2 | [[64483]]-[[-LT]] | Transforaminal epidural steroid injection, lumbar, single initial level, performed on the left side.<sup>2</sup> |
| CPT 3 | [[64484]]-[[-LT]] | Add-on procedural code for the second level (L5) left-sided transforaminal epidural steroid injection.<sup>2</sup> |
| PDx | [[M47.26]] | Primary diagnosis code for lumbar spondylosis with radiculopathy across the evaluated and treated segments.<sup>1</sup> |

> [!tip] 
> Appending modifier [[-25]] to CPT [[99214]] is required to bypass automated NCCI edits. Clinical notes must clearly separate the history, examination, and decision-making elements from the procedural injection documentation.<sup>2</sup>

### Example 3  

A 72-year-old male with chronic lumbar spondylosis and disabling bilateral L4 radiculopathy fails extensive non-operative management. He is admitted for elective inpatient surgical intervention. The orthopedic spine surgeon performs an open lumbar laminectomy, bilateral facetectomy, and bilateral foraminotomy at L4–L5 to decompress compressed nerve roots without spinal arthrodesis.

| Field | Code | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| CPT | [[63047]] | Primary surgical code for open lumbar laminectomy, facetectomy, and foraminotomy with nerve root decompression at a single segment.<sup>2</sup> |
| PDx | [[M47.26]] | Principal diagnosis for inpatient admission capturing lumbar spondylosis with compressive nerve root pathology.<sup>1</sup> |

> [!tip] 
> For inpatient facility billing, this case groups into surgical MS-DRG 519 (Back and Neck Procedures Except Spinal Fusion without CC/MCC). Documentation must verify that decompression was achieved without spinal fusion to prevent improper DRG assignment.

## ⚠️ Coding Pitfalls and Tips  

- **Pitfall 1**: Reporting **[[M54.16]]** (**lumbar [[radiculopathy]]**) concurrently with **[[M47.816]]** (**lumbar [[spondylosis]] without [[radiculopathy]]**); **Tips**: **Official ICD-10-CM Excludes1 conventions** require collapsing these findings into combination code **[[M47.26]]**.<sup>1</sup>
- **Pitfall 2**: Submitting an unspecified region diagnosis such as **[[M47.20]]** when medical records explicitly identify **lumbar localization**; **Tips**: Review neuroimaging and operative notes to confirm anatomical levels and report **[[M47.26]]** to avoid claim delays.<sup>1</sup>
- **Pitfall 3**: Unbundling fluoroscopic imaging guidance (**CPT [[77003]]**) alongside transforaminal epidural injections (**[[64483]]**); **Tips**: Fluoroscopic guidance is bundled into CPT **[[64483]]** under **NCCI rules** and must not be separately billed.<sup>2</sup>
- **Pitfall 4**: Adding standalone symptom codes for **[[sciatica]]** (**[[M54.30]]** or **[[M54.40]]**) alongside **[[M47.26]]**; **Tips**: **Sciatic nerve pain** is an inherent manifestation of **lumbar spondylotic [[radiculopathy]]** and should not be dual-coded.<sup>1</sup>
- **Pitfall 5**: Confusing **acute lumbar disc displacement** with **[[radiculopathy]]** (**[[M51.16]]**) with **spondylotic [[radiculopathy]]** (**[[M47.26]]**); **Tips**: Examine **radiology** reports to determine whether **nerve compression** is caused by **acute soft-tissue disc extrusion** or **chronic osseous facet arthropathy**.<sup>1,3</sup>
- **Pitfall 6**: Failing to append anatomical laterality modifiers (**[[-RT]]**, **[[-LT]]**, or **[[-50]]**) to **injection code** **[[64483]]**; **Tips**: Payer claim processing systems mandate laterality modifiers on interventional spine codes to verify anatomical concordance.<sup>2</sup>
