# 🧬 ICD-10 CM M51.16 — Intervertebral Disc Disorders With Radiculopathy, Lumbar Region

> [!success] Billable Code Confirmed
> **ICD-10 CM [[M51.16]]** is a **5-character code**: **M51** (disc disorders), .**1** (with **[[radiculopathy]]**) and **6** (lumbar region). It is valid for encounters and discharges from **10/1/2026 through 9/30/2027**, with no additional characters required.<sup>1,2,4</sup>

> [!warning] Non-Billable Parent Codes
> **M51** is the category header for **thoracic, thoracolumbar and lumbosacral disc disorders** and cannot be reported. **M51.1** is the subcategory for **disc disorders** with **[[radiculopathy]]** and also requires a **5th character** for region.<sup>3</sup>

> [!important] Clinical Context
> Choose **[[M51.16]]** only when the provider documents a **lumbar disc disorder** with **[[radiculopathy]]**. A **disc [[herniation]] without nerve root involvement** goes to **[[M51.26]]**, and **L5-S1 level disease** is **[[lumbosacral]]** (**[[M51.17]]**).<sup>3</sup>

> [!danger] Code Classification
> This is an **ICD-10-CM diagnosis code** (**Chapter 13, Musculoskeletal**). It is not a procedure code and it is not a **manifestation code.**

---

## 🔍 Code Description

**ICD-10 CM [[M51.16]]** reports **a lumbar intervertebral disc disorder, such as [[herniation]], [[protrusion]], [[extrusion]] or bulge, that is producing [[radiculopathy]]**. The **nerve root irritation** or **[[compression]]** causes radiating **pain, [[paresthesia]], numbness** or **[[weakness]]** in the affected dermatome, commonly called sciatica when it follows the **L4-S1 roots**. Classification-wise, the **[[radiculopathy]]** is treated as part of the disc disorder, which is why **[[M54.16]]** and **[[M51.26]]** cannot be added to describe the same problem.<sup>3</sup>

Workup usually includes **lumbar MRI, EMG/NCS** and a neurological exam, and treatment runs from **PT and NSAIDs** to **epidural steroid injections (ESIs)** and surgical decompression. For a **PM&R or pain coder**, this code is the typical primary diagnosis on lumbar **ESI claims**, and it appears on the **ESI LCD** article's covered diagnosis list.<sup>5</sup> For inpatient profee, it frequently pairs with **[[laminectomy]]** or **[[discectomy]]** codes such as **[[63030]]** and **[[63047]].**

> [!example] Radiculopathy vs. Displacement
> **Payers** such as the **ESI LCD** require documented radicular findings to cover **epidural injections**. If you choose **[[M51.26]]** when the provider documented **[[radiculopathy]]**, the claim can fail the **LCD** diagnosis edit.<sup>5</sup>

> [!tip]
> Look for the words "**[[radiculopathy]]**," "**radicular pain**," "**sciatica due to disc**" or documented dermatomal deficits. If they are missing, query the provider before defaulting to **[[M51.26]]**.

## ✅ Includes

- **Sciatica due to intervertebral disc disorder:** This is an inclusion term at **M51.1** and indexes to the **lumbar [[radiculopathy]] code** when the lumbar region is documented, in this case **[[M51.16]]**.<sup>3</sup>
- **Lumbar herniated nucleus pulposus with nerve root [[compression]]:** The provider-documented **[[herniation]]** plus radicular symptoms support **[[M51.16]]**.

---

## ❌ Excludes

### Excludes 1
- **[[M54.16]] ([[Radiculopathy]], lumbar region; lumbar radiculitis NOS):** The code is mutually exclusive with **[[M51.16]]** because the disc-related **[[radiculopathy]]** is already captured in **[[M51.16]]**.<sup>3</sup>
- **M54.3- (Sciatica NOS):** **Sciatica NOS** belongs here only when no disc cause is documented. **M54.3-** is a parent category, so the reportable codes are **[[M54.31]]** and **[[M54.32]]**.<sup>3</sup>

> [!danger]
> The most common **error** is reporting **[[M51.16]]** together with **[[M54.16]]** or **[[M54.31]]**/**[[M54.32]]** "**to show the sciatica.**" Because these are **Excludes1** pairs, the claim will fail edits and the extra code adds nothing, so report **[[M51.16]]** alone.

### Excludes 2
- **M50.- (Cervical and cervicothoracic disc disorders):** A patient may have a cervical disc problem and a lumbar disc problem at once, and both can be reported together.<sup>3</sup>
- **[[M53.3]] (Sacrococcygeal disorders NEC):** **[[Coccygodynia]]** and similar disorders are separate conditions, so **[[M53.3]]** can be reported alongside **[[M51.16]]** when both are documented.<sup>3</sup>

## 📋 Clinical Overview

### Radiculopathy vs. Displacement vs. Degeneration
**Lumbar disc codes** are separated by what the provider documents about the disc and the nerve. **[[Radiculopathy]]** goes to **[[M51.16]], [[myelopathy]]** to **[[M51.06]]**, displacement without **[[radiculopathy]]** to **[[M51.26]]**, and **[[degeneration]]** to the **M51.36-** codes. **M51.36-** was expanded to **6 characters** in an earlier update, so verify the current subcode in your encoder.

| **Feature** | **[[M51.16]]** | **Related [[M51.26]]** | **Related [[M51.06]]** |
|:---:|---|---|---|
| **Key documentation** | Lumbar disc disorder with radicular pain, [[paresthesia]], weakness or reflex change. | Lumbar disc displacement documented without [[radiculopathy]] or [[myelopathy]]. | Lumbar disc disorder with spinal cord involvement, which is rare at the lumbar level. |
| **Typical clinical picture** | Leg pain in a dermatomal pattern, positive straight leg raise, MRI nerve root contact. | Back pain or imaging-only herniation without neurological findings. | Cord signs such as [[spasticity]] or [[hyperreflexia]]; lumbar cord ends near L1-L2. |
| **Coding impact** | Supports ESI and surgical [[decompression]] medical necessity. | Often fails ESI LCD diagnosis criteria. | Rarely correct for a true lumbar level; query the provider. |

> [!important]
> **CDI trigger**: a note that says only "**L4-5 [[herniation]]**" with leg pain or "**[[sciatica]]**" in the HPI. Query the provider for the clinical link ("**[[radiculopathy]] due to disc [[herniation]]**") and the level, since **L5-S1 is lumbosacral ([[M51.17]])**.

### Manifestations & Symptom Burden
- **Radicular leg pain:** Burning or shooting pain follows the affected root, such as L4 (anterior thigh/medial leg), L5 (lateral leg/dorsum of foot) or S1 (posterior leg/lateral foot).
- **Sensory change:** **[[Paresthesia]]** or numbness in the dermatome supports the [[radiculopathy]] documentation.
- **Motor weakness:** **Foot drop** (L5) or plantar flexion weakness (S1) is integral to the [[radiculopathy]] and is not coded separately.
- **Reflex change:** A diminished patellar (L4) or Achilles (S1) reflex is an objective finding that strengthens medical necessity.
- **Red flags:** **Cauda equina signs**, such as saddle [[anesthesia]] or bowel or bladder dysfunction, need separate documentation and different coding.

> [!tip]
> Symptoms integral to **[[radiculopathy]]**, such as **leg pain, numbness or weakness**, are not coded separately. Report a separate symptom code only for something not routinely part of the disorder, such as **urinary retention**.

| **MS-DRG** | **Title** | **Notes** |
|:---:|---|---|
| **551** | Medical Back Problems with MCC | Medical admission with a secondary MCC |
| **552** | Medical Back Problems without MCC | Medical admission, no MCC |
| **518-520** | Back and Neck Procedures Except Spinal Fusion | Decompression such as laminectomy or discectomy |
| **459-460** | Spinal Fusion Except Cervical | Fusion with or without MCC |

As principal diagnosis, **[[M51.16]]** groups to the medical back **DRGs 551-552** per **ICDList**, and a spinal **OR** procedure moves it into the **surgical DRGs** above.<sup>6</sup> It is not a **CC** or **MCC** as a secondary diagnosis. The **surgical DRG assignments** are from my coding knowledge, so confirm them in your grouper.

- **NCD:** I found no **NCD** specific to **[[M51.16]]**. The acupuncture **NCD** for chronic low back pain applies to nonspecific **low back pain**, so a documented disc cause with **[[radiculopathy]]** likely falls outside it. Confirm in the **Medicare Coverage Database**.
- **LCD:** **LCD L36920** (**Epidural Steroid Injections for Pain Management**) and **Article A56681** list **[[M51.16]]** as a supported diagnosis. They allow one spinal region per session and no more than two levels per session across **[[64479]], [[64480]], [[64483]] and [[64484]]**.<sup>5</sup>
- **MAC check:** **LCDs** vary by **MAC jurisdiction**, so check your **MAC's current version** in the **CMS Medicare Coverage Database**.

### NCCI Bundling Considerations
Imaging guidance (**fluoroscopy or CT**) is included in **[[62323]], [[64483]]** and **[[64484]]**, so do not report it separately. **[[Decompression]]** and **[[fusion]]** at the same level can be subject to **NCCI edits**, so check the current **NCCI PTP edits** before reporting both.

## 💊 Coding Scenarios and Examples

### Example 1
> **Clinical Scenario:**
> A 54-year-old has right L5 radicular pain after failed PT, with MRI showing an L4-L5 disc herniation compressing the right L5 root. The pain physician performs a fluoroscopically guided right L4-L5 transforaminal epidural steroid injection.

| **Field** | **Code** | **Rationale** |
|---|---|---|
| CPT | [[64483]]-[[-RT]] | Single-level lumbar transforaminal injection with imaging guidance, right side. |
| PDx | [[M51.16]] | Documented lumbar disc herniation with radiculopathy supports the LCD diagnosis requirement.<sup>5</sup> |

> [!tip]
> Link the diagnosis to the injection and keep the level and side in the procedure note. Document failed conservative therapy and the radicular findings, since this supports medical necessity.

### Example 2
> **Clinical Scenario:**
> A patient with L4-L5 and L5-S1 disc herniations causing bilateral radiculopathy undergoes a single-segment laminectomy, facetectomy and foraminotomy at L4-L5 and an additional segment at L5-S1.

| **Field** | **Code** | **Rationale** |
|---|---|---|
| CPT | [[63047]] | Lumbar decompression, single vertebral segment. |
| CPT 2 | [[63048]] | Each additional segment (add-on, no [[-51]]). |
| PDx | [[M51.16]] | L4-L5 disc disorder with radiculopathy. Add [[M51.17]] for the lumbosacral L5-S1 level if the provider documents it. |

> [!tip]
> Report [[M51.16]] for the lumbar level and [[M51.17]] for the lumbosacral level when both are documented. Confirm the documented segments match the CPT units billed.

### Example 3
> **Clinical Scenario:**
> A 41-year-old has severe bilateral L5 radiculopathy from a central L4-L5 herniation with motor weakness, and undergoes bilateral L4-L5 laminotomy with microdiscectomy.

| **Field** | **Code** | **Rationale** |
|---|---|---|
| CPT | [[63030]]-[[-50]] | Bilateral laminotomy with discectomy at one interspace; check payer rules for [[-50]] versus [[-RT]]/[[-LT]] lines. |
| PDx | [[M51.16]] | Lumbar disc herniation with radiculopathy. |

> [!tip]
> Weakness and pain are integral to the radiculopathy, so don't add separate symptom codes. If the provider documents a drop foot, query whether it is a separate condition before coding it.

## ⚠️ Coding Pitfalls and Tips

- **Pitfall 1**: Reporting **[[M51.16]]** with **[[M54.16]]** or **[[M54.31]]/[[M54.32]]**; **Tips**: These are **Excludes1 pairs**, so report **[[M51.16]]** alone.
- **Pitfall 2**: Using **[[M51.16]]** for L5-S1 disease; **Tips**: L5-S1 is **[[lumbosacral]]**, so use **[[M51.17]]** unless the provider documents a lumbar-only level.
- **Pitfall 3**: Choosing **[[M51.26]]** when **[[radiculopathy]]** is documented; **Tips**: **[[Radiculopathy]]** documented with the **[[herniation]]** takes **[[M51.16]]**, and failing to report it can cause **LCD** denials on ESIs.<sup>5</sup>
- **Pitfall 4**: Assuming **disc degeneration** plus **[[radiculopathy]]** takes the **M51.36-** codes; **Tips**: Check the instructional notes and **tabular** for the current FY27 subcodes, since **M51.36-** was expanded to 6 characters in an earlier update.
- **Pitfall 5**: Billing both **[[fluoroscopy]]** and **[[62323]]/[[64483]]**; **Tips**: Imaging guidance is part of those codes.
- **Pitfall 6**: Reporting **[[64484]]** alone or for more than the **LCD** allows; **Tips**: It is an add-on to **[[64483]]** only, and the **LCD** limits levels per session.<sup>5</sup>
