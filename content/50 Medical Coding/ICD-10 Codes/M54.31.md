# 🧬 ICD-10 CM M54.31 — Sciatica, Right Side

> [!success] Billable Code Confirmed  
> **ICD-10 CM [[M54.31]]** is a fully valid, **5-character** billable diagnosis code for **FY 2027**. It accurately specifies the condition (**sciatica**) and the exact laterality (**right side**), requiring no additional characters for claim submission.

> [!warning] Non-Billable Parent Codes  
> The parent category **M54** (**Dorsalgia**) and subcategory **M54.3** (**Sciatica**) are non-billable because they lack specificity regarding the exact spinal condition and laterality required for modern claims.

> [!important] Clinical Context  
> This code is selected when a patient presents with isolated right-sided **neuropathic pain** radiating along the sciatic nerve pathway (**usually extending below the knee**) *without* concurrent generalized low back pain and *without* a confirmed underlying **intervertebral disc disorder**.

> [!danger] Code Classification  
> **ICD-10 CM [[M54.31]]** is a primary diagnosis code. It establishes medical necessity for **evaluation and management services, physical therapy, and right-sided interventional pain management procedures**.

---

## 🔍 Code Description  
**ICD-10 CM [[M54.31]]** is utilized to classify **right-sided sciatica**, a condition characterized by **pain radiating along the path of the sciatic nerve, which travels from the lower back, through the hips and right buttock, and down the right leg**<sup>1,2</sup>. The pain is typically described as **sharp, shooting, or burning**, and is often accompanied by n**eurological deficits** such as **numbness**, **tingling** (**[[paresthesia]]**), or **motor weakness** in the affected right extremity. This code strictly represents the isolated symptom of **sciatic nerve irritation** on the right side.

For accurate **clinical coding**, **[[M54.31]]** should only be reported when the provider specifically documents the laterality as "**right**" and does **not** explicitly note **concurrent low back pain (lumbago)** or a confirmed **causative structural defect** like a **herniated disc**<sup>3</sup>. If an **MRI** or other diagnostic test confirms that the right-sided **sciatica** is due to an **intervertebral disc disorder**, coders are instructed by **ICD-10 guidelines** to utilize a code from the **M51.1- subcategory** (e.g., **[[M51.16]]**) instead of **[[M54.31]]**. Adhering to these **Excludes1 guidelines** prevents **unbundling** and aligns with strict payer requirements for **interventional procedure authorizations**.

> [!example] Laterality and Medical Necessity Impact
> 
> **Payers** heavily scrutinize laterality mismatches between the diagnosis and the procedure. If a provider performs a **right-sided transforaminal epidural steroid injection (TFESI)**, submitting **[[M54.31]]** provides exact anatomical justification, whereas submitting the unspecified code **[[M54.30]]** will likely result in an **immediate medical necessity denial**.
> 
>   

> [!tip]
> 
> Always search the **History of Present Illness (HPI)** and **Physical Exam for a positive Straight Leg Raise (SLR) test** on the right, which provides excellent **clinical validation** for this specific code.
> 
>

## ✅ Includes

- **Right-sided sciatic nerve pain** radiating below the knee with documented neurological deficits1.
    
      
    
- **Pain originating in the lower back** but isolated functionally to the right buttock and right leg.
    
      
    
- **Positive straight leg raise test** on the right side without confirmed disc pathology.
    

---

## ❌ Excludes

### Excludes 1

- **[[M54.41]]** — **Lumbago with sciatica, right side.** These are mutually exclusive because **[[M54.41]]** captures a patient suffering from _both_ localized lower back pain and right-sided **sciatica** simultaneously.
    
      
    
- **[[M51.16]]** — **Intervertebral disc disorders with radiculopathy, lumbar region**. If imaging identifies a herniated or degenerated lumbar disc as the physical cause of the sciatic pain, the disc disorder code absorbs the sciatica diagnosis.
    
      
    
- **[[G57.01]]** — **Lesion of sciatic nerve, right lower limb**. This code represents a direct structural or traumatic lesion to the nerve itself rather than symptomatic irritation.
    
      
    

> [!danger]
> 
> The most common **Excludes 1 error** is assigning **[[M54.31]]** alongside a primary back pain code like **[[M54.50]]**. The **ICD-10 guidelines** strictly prohibit coding **sciatica** and **low back pain separately**; they must be combined into a single code from the **M54.4-** (**Lumbago with sciatica**) subcategory.
> 
>   

---

### Excludes 2

- **[[M62.830]]** — **Muscle spasm of back**. This can be coded alongside **right-sided sciatica** if the provider documents distinct back **muscle spasms** that are separate from the neuropathic nerve pain radiating down the leg.
    
      
    
- **[[M48.061]]** — **Spinal stenosis, lumbar region without neurogenic claudication**. If the patient has documented lumbar spinal stenosis being treated concurrently but independently from the primary right-sided sciatica, both codes can be assigned to provide a complete clinical picture.

## 📋 Clinical Overview

### Differentiating Sciatica Codes

**Code** selection relies heavily on the presence of concurrent back pain and the identification of a r**oot structural cause.**

  

|**Feature**|**[[M54.31]]**|**Related [[M54.41]]**|**Related [[M51.16]]**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Clinical Presentation**|Isolated right-sided neuropathic pain radiating down the right leg.|Right-sided sciatic pain accompanied by generalized low back pain.|Right-sided sciatic pain caused by a confirmed lumbar intervertebral disc herniation.|
|**Documentation Focus**|"Right sciatica" with positive SLR, no disc pathology noted.|"Low back pain with right leg sciatica."|MRI confirming disc herniation causing the radicular pain.|
|**Payer Criteria / LCD**|Requires documentation of conservative therapy (PT, NSAIDs) prior to advanced imaging/injections.|Similar to isolated sciatica, requires a trial of conservative PT/meds.|Often meets criteria for more advanced surgical or interventional approaches depending on symptom severity.|

> [!important]
> 
> **Clinical Documentation Improvement (CDI) teams** should query the provider if the **operative report** or **MRI notes** mention a "**right paracentral disc extrusion compressing the L5 nerve root**" but the diagnosis merely states "**right sciatica**." Clarifying the structural cause upgrades the code to a **disc disorder**, which supports higher-acuity interventions.
> 
>   

---

### Manifestations & Symptom Burden

- **Radiating Pain**: Sharp, electrical pain tracking from the right buttock into the right posterior thigh and calf.
    
      
    
- **Sensory Deficits**: Numbness or [[paresthesia]] (pins and needles) spanning the right foot or toes.
    
      
    
- **Motor Weakness**: Weakness in the right ankle or toes, potentially affecting the patient's gait.
    
      
    

> [!tip]
> 
> Ensure the provider explicitly names the side ("**right**") in the assessment and plan; do not rely solely on the HPI to establish laterality for coding purposes.
> 
>

|:---:|---|---|---|
|**551**|Medical Back Problems with MCC|Higher|Extended|
|**552**|Medical Back Problems without MCC|Lower|Standard|

For **inpatient encounters**, **[[M54.31]]** groups to the **Medical Back Problems DRGs**. Most **Medicare Administrative Contractors (MACs)** maintain strict **Local Coverage Determinations (LCDs)** for pain management procedures associated with this **DRG**. An explicit record of conservative care failure (**typically 4-6 weeks of physical therapy and NSAIDs**) is usually required in the clinical documentation before procedures like **epidural steroid injections** will be reimbursed.

## 🏷️ Modifier Reference

|**Modifier**|**Name**|**When to Apply**|
|:---:|---|---|
|**[[-RT]]**|Right Side|Apply to unilateral interventional CPT codes (e.g., [[64483]]) to indicate the injection was performed strictly on the right side, directly aligning with the right-sided diagnosis.|
|**[[-50]]**|Bilateral|Apply to unilateral procedures if they are performed on both the right and left sides during the same operative session (requires bilateral diagnosis codes).|
|**[[-25]]**|Significant E/M|Apply to an E/M code (e.g., [[99213]]) if a significantly separately identifiable evaluation is performed on the same day as a minor procedure (like a trigger point injection).|
|**[[-59]]**|Distinct Service|Apply to indicate a procedure was distinct or independent from other non-E/M services performed on the same day, preventing NCCI bundling denials.|

---

### NCCI Bundling Considerations

When coding **interventional pain management** (like **[[64483]]**), **fluoroscopic** or **CT imaging guidance** is inherently included in the primary **CPT code** and cannot be billed separately. Additionally, if multiple levels are injected, use add-on codes (like **[[64484]]**) rather than appending modifier **[[-59]]** to the primary code.

## 💊 Coding Scenarios and Examples

### Example 1

> **Clinical Scenario:**
> 
>   
> 
> An established patient presents to the physical medicine clinic complaining of right-sided sciatic pain radiating down to the calf, without localized back pain. The provider performs a detailed exam, noting a positive straight leg raise on the right. No imaging is ordered today. The provider recommends a 6-week course of physical therapy focusing on therapeutic exercises and manual manipulation.
> 
>   

|**Field**|**Code**|**Rationale**|
|---|---|---|
|CPT|[[99213]]|Established patient E/M visit for a stable, chronic issue with a moderate level of medical decision making.|
|PDx|[[M54.31]]|Captures the right-sided sciatica as the sole presenting condition.|

> [!tip]
> 
> Because the provider did not note lumbago and there is no confirmed disc herniation yet, [[M54.31]] is the most accurate and precise diagnosis available.
> 
>   

### Example 2

> **Clinical Scenario:**
> 
>   
> 
> A patient with a documented history of right sciatica that has failed 8 weeks of physical therapy and NSAID treatment comes into the pain management center. Under fluoroscopic guidance, the provider performs a right-sided transforaminal epidural steroid injection at the L4-L5 level.
> 
>   

|**Field**|**Code**|**Rationale**|
|---|---|---|
|CPT|[[64483]]-[[-RT]]|Single-level lumbar transforaminal epidural steroid injection with imaging guidance, indicated on the right side.|
|PDx|[[M54.31]]|Justifies the medical necessity for the right-sided targeted nerve block.|

> [!tip]
> 
> Ensure the pre-authorization and clinic notes explicitly state the failure of conservative therapy, as this is a strict requirement for MAC LCD approval for code [[64483]].
> 
>   

### Example 3

> **Clinical Scenario:**
> 
>   
> 
> A patient is evaluated for right-sided sciatic pain. The physician performs a thorough evaluation and decides to administer a trigger point injection into two distinct muscles in the right gluteal region to relieve associated piriformis tension, on the same day as the E/M visit.
> 
>   

|**Field**|**Code**|**Rationale**|
|---|---|---|
|CPT|[[99213]]-[[-25]]|E/M service appended with modifier [[-25]] to indicate it was significant and separately identifiable from the injection.|
|CPT 2|[[20552]]|Trigger point injection, 1 or 2 muscles.|
|PDx|[[M54.31]]|Supports the right-sided neuropathic pain symptom complex treated by both the E/M and the injection.|

> [!tip]
> 
> Modifier [[-25]] must be appended to the E/M code, not the procedure code, to bypass NCCI edits that would otherwise bundle the evaluation into the minor surgical procedure.
> 
>

## ⚠️ Coding Pitfalls and Tips

- **Pitfall 1**: Coding **[[M54.31]]** when the patient has documented **low back pain** alongside the leg pain; **Tips**: Review the note carefully. If back pain is present, **ICD-10 guidelines** mandate combining the symptoms into **[[M54.41]]** (**Lumbago with sciatica, right side**).
    
      
    
- **Pitfall 2**: Retaining the **sciatica code** after an **MRI** confirms a **herniated disc**; **Tips**: Once a structural cause is found (**like a disc disorder**), update the diagnosis to the definitive finding (e.g., **[[M51.16]]**).
    
      
    
- **Pitfall 3**: Defaulting to the unspecified code (**[[M54.30]]**); **Tips**: Always query the provider or read the physical exam to find the specific laterality. Unspecified codes frequently trigger automated payer denials.
    
      
    
- **Pitfall 4**: Billing an **interlaminar ESI** (**[[62323]]**) without appending the appropriate laterality modifier; **Tips**: While **interlaminar** **injections** are central, many payers still require the **[[-RT]]** or **[[-LT]]** modifier to match the **laterality** of the diagnosis code.
    
      
    
- **Pitfall 5**: **Missing** prior **conservative treatment documentation**; **Tips**: Payers actively **audit right-sided sciatica claims for PT and NSAID trials** prior to authorizing advanced imaging or injections.
