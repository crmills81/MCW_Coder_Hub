# 🧬 ICD-10 CM M72.6 — Necrotizing Fasciitis

> [!success] Billable Code Confirmed  
> **ICD-10-CM [[M72.6]]** is a **4-character code** (**category M72 plus one decimal digit**) with no child codes, so it is reported as written.<sup>1</sup> It is valid for discharges and dates of service 10/1/2026 through 9/30/2027.

> [!warning] Non-Billable Parent Codes  
> **M72** (**Fibroblastic disorders**) is a category header and cannot be reported b**y itself. **M70-M79** (**Other soft tissue disorders**) is a block range, not a code.

> [!important] Clinical Context  
> **[[Necrotizing fasciitis]]** is a rapidly progressive bacterial infection with **[[necrosis]]** of **fascia** and surrounding soft tissue, usually requiring urgent surgical **debridement**. Documentation of "**necrotizing**" is what separates **[[M72.6]]** from ordinary cellulitis or **[[M72.8]]** **fasciitis NEC**.

> [!danger] Code Classification  
> This is a **diagnosis code (ICD-10-CM)**, not a procedure code. **[[Debridement]]** is reported with **CPT** for pro-fee and **ICD-10-PCS** for facility.

---

## 🔍 Code Description  
**ICD-10 CM [[M72.6]]** reports **[[necrotizing fasciitis]]** regardless of body region, because the code has no site or laterality axis.<sup>1</sup> The Tabular directs a Use additional code note for the causative organism (**B95.-**, **B96.-**) when the organism is documented, for example **[[B95.0]]** for **group A Streptococcus**.<sup>7</sup> Verify that note in the **FY2027 Tabular**.

**[[Fournier gangrene]]** is classified separately to **[[N49.3]]** through the **Alphabetic Index**, so the **genitourinary** versus **musculoskeletal** fork is the biggest **[[urology]]** decision point. When **[[sepsis]]** is present, such as **[[A41.9]]**, the **[[sepsis]]** sequencing rules in **Section I.C.1.d of the Official Guidelines** apply before **[[M72.6]]** is considered.

> [!example] Terminal Code — No Site Specificity  
> **ICD-10 CM [[M72.6]]** has no site, depth, or laterality character, so anatomic detail lives in the **CPT debridement code** and the operative report. The code itself is 4 characters, so do not hunt for a **5th**.

> [!tip] 
> Do **not** substitute **[[M72.8]]** for documented "**necrotizing**" **fasciitis**, because **[[M72.8]]** carries an **Excludes1** for **[[M72.6]]** and its inclusion terms (**[[abscess]] of fascia, fasciitis NEC, other infective [[fasciitis]]**) describe different disease.<sup>7</sup>

---

## ✅ Includes  
- **Necrotizing fasciitis** is the **Index** entry (**Fasciitis, necrotizing**) that leads to **[[M72.6]]**.<sup>1</sup> No formal **inclusion** terms are printed at this code.
- **Infective necrotizing fasciitis** follows the Index path Fasciitis, infective, necrotizing, also to **[[M72.6]]**.<sup>1</sup>
- **Clinical synonyms** such as **necrotizing soft tissue infection**, **streptococcal gangrene**, and **Meleney gangrene** are documentation language, not **Tabular** inclusion terms. Confirm each in the **Alphabetic Index** before coding from it.

---

## ❌ Excludes  

### Excludes 1  
- **[[M72.8]]** — **[[M72.8]]** carries an **Excludes1 note** for **[[[necrotizing fasciitis]]]** **[[M72.6]]**, and the two codes are never reported together for the same condition.<sup>7</sup> If the provider documents **necrotizing fasciitis**, **[[M72.6]]** is the only **[[fasciitis]]** code.
- **[[N49.3]]** — This is not a printed **Excludes1** pair at **[[M72.6]]**, but the Index sends **[[Fournier gangrene]]** here. If documentation describes **necrotizing infection** spreading beyond the **genitalia** and **perineum**, query the provider and check **current Coding Clinic guidance** rather than defaulting to both codes.

> [!danger] 
> The most common **error** is reporting **[[M72.8]]** alongside **[[M72.6]]** or in place of it. A second error is coding **[[M72.6]]** for **[[Fournier gangrene]]** without checking the **Index**.

### Excludes 2  
- **[[D48.3]]** — **Retroperitoneal fibromatosis** is an **Excludes2** at category **M72**. Both may be reported if the patient truly has both conditions.
- **Chapter 13 Excludes2 (M00-M99)** — This includes **arthropathic [[psoriasis]]** (**L40.5-**) and conditions originating in the perinatal period (**P04-P96**). Both may be coded alongside **[[M72.6]]** when documented.<sup>1</sup>

## 📋 Clinical Overview  

### Necrotizing Fasciitis Versus Look-Alike Soft Tissue Codes  
The deciding factor is documented tissue **[[necrosis]]** and the anatomic site. **[[M72.6]]** applies to **[[necrotizing fasciitis]]** generally, **[[N49.3]]** captures **[[Fournier gangrene]]** of the genital region, and **[[L03.315]]** covers **perineal [[cellulitis]]** without documented **fascial necrosis**.

| **Feature** | **[[M72.6]]** | **Related [[N49.3]]** | **Related [[L03.315]]** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tissue involved** | Fascia and adjacent soft tissue with necrosis. | Genital and perineal soft tissue gangrene (Fournier). | Perineal skin and subcutaneous tissue without documented necrosis. |
| **Site specificity** | None in the code. | Genitourinary chapter code, male genital region. | Perineum only. |
| **Typical procedure** | Debridement, CPT by region or depth. | Debridement, often CPT [[11004]]. | Usually I&D or medical management. |

> [!important] 
> Documentation of "**necrotizing**," "**[[gangrene]]**," or operative findings of **[[necrotic fascia]]** should trigger a **CDI query** when the chart only says "**[[cellulitis]]**" or "**soft tissue [[infection]]**." That wording difference changes the **DRG** and the **MCC** capture.

### Manifestations & Symptom Burden  
- **Pain out of proportion to exam** is an early warning sign and often precedes visible skin changes.
- **Rapidly spreading [[erythema]], bullae, or skin [[necrosis]]** reflects advancing tissue death.
- **Crepitus or gas in tissue** suggests a gas-forming organism and supports the diagnosis when documented.
- **Systemic [[toxicity]]** includes **[[fever]], [[tachycardia]], [[hypotension]], and [[sepsis]]**, which are coded separately.

> [!tip] 
> **[[Sepsis]]** (**[[A41.9]]**, **[[A41.02]]**) and **severe [[sepsis]] with shock** (**[[R65.21]]**) are coded in addition to **[[M72.6]]** and sequenced per the guidelines, never folded into it.

|---|---|---|
| **[[M72.6]]** as PDx, medical, with MCC | 557 | Tendonitis, Myositis and Bursitis with MCC<sup>2</sup> |
| **[[M72.6]]** as PDx, medical, without MCC | 558 | Tendonitis, Myositis and Bursitis without MCC<sup>2</sup> |
| MDC 08 OR debridement (PDx M72.6) | 463-465 | Wound Debridement or Skin Graft Except Hand for MSK/CT Disorders<sup>2</sup> |

The **medical DRG** pair splits only on **MCC**, so there is **no CC tier**. When **[[M72.6]]** is a secondary diagnosis, reference data lists it as an **MCC**, but verify that and the PDX exclusion logic in the **FY2027 v44 CC/MCC** tables. Sepsis present on admission is sequenced first when it meets the definition of principal diagnosis, and **[[M72.6]]** then becomes a secondary **MCC**.

**NCD/LCD**
- **NCD 20.29 Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy** lists **progressive necrotizing infections** (**[[necrotizing fasciitis]]**) as a covered condition. It is an adjunct to standard care and applies when loss of function, limb, or life is threatened.<sup>3</sup>
- No **Noridian (JE/JF) LCD or Billing and Coding article for HBO** was verified for this note, so check the MCD before billing.

## 🛠️ Commonly Associated CPT Codes  
- **[[11004]]** — **Debridement of skin, subcutaneous tissue, muscle and fascia for necrotizing soft tissue infection** of the external genitalia and perineum.<sup>4</sup> This is the urology go-to for genital and perineal NSTI.
- **[[11005]]** — Same [[debridement]] of the abdominal wall, with or without fascial closure.<sup>4</sup>
- **[[11006]]** — Same **debridement** of external genitalia, perineum, and abdominal wall combined, with or without fascial closure.<sup>4</sup> Do not append **[[-52]]** just because genitalia were not debrided.<sup>5</sup>

- **[[11046]]** — Add-on for each additional 20 sq cm of muscle and/or fascia debridement.
- **[[99183]]** — Physician attendance and supervision of hyperbaric oxygen therapy, per session, supported by **NCD 20.29**.<sup>3</sup> Facility outpatient **HBO** reports **G0277** per 30-minute interval.

### NCCI Bundling Considerations  
**CPT** instructs **not** to report **[[11000]]/+[[11001]]** with the **NSTI** debridement codes **[[11004]]-[[11006]]**.<sup>6</sup> **NCCI bundles** other debridement codes into **[[11004]]-[[11006]]**, so a separate anatomic site needs documentation and **[[-59]] (or an X{EPSU} modifier)**.<sup>4,5</sup> Always verify against the current **NCCI PTP edits**.

## 🔬 ICD-10-PCS Crosswalk  
Excisional [[debridement]] is coded to the root operation [[Excision]], with the body part determined by the deepest tissue removed and the site.
- **0JB80ZZ** — Excision of abdomen [[subcutaneous]] tissue and fascia, open approach. It fits abdominal wall NSTI debridement limited to subcutaneous tissue and [[fascia]].
- **0JBB0ZZ** — Excision of perineum subcutaneous tissue and fascia, open approach. It fits perineal [[debridement]].
- **0JBL0ZZ** — Excision of right upper leg subcutaneous tissue and fascia, open approach. When muscle is excised, code the 0KB muscle body part for that site instead.

*Verify all PCS codes against the FY2027 PCS tables before use.*

## 💊 Coding Scenarios and Examples  

<br>

### Example 1
> **Clinical Scenario:**  
> A patient is admitted with group A streptococcal necrotizing fasciitis of the abdominal wall. The surgeon performs excisional debridement of skin, subcutaneous tissue, muscle, and fascia. No sepsis is documented.

| **Field** | **Code** | **Rationale** |
|---|---|---|
| CPT | [[11005]] | Abdominal wall NSTI debridement. |
| PDx | [[M72.6]] | Necrotizing fasciitis is the reason for admission. |
| SDx | [[B95.0]] | Organism reported per the Use additional code note. |

> [!tip] 
> Because the case is surgical, the facility claim groups to the MDC 08 debridement DRG family, not 557/558.  
> **CDI**: Confirm the operative note documents "necrotizing" and the depth of tissue removed.

### Example 2
> **Clinical Scenario:**  
> A patient has cervical necrotizing fasciitis after a dental infection. The surgeon debrides muscle and fascia of the neck, first 20 sq cm, plus an additional 20 sq cm.

| **Field** | **Code** | **Rationale** |
|---|---|---|
| CPT | [[11043]] | Muscle/fascia debridement, first 20 sq cm. |
| CPT 2 | [[11046]] | Add-on for the additional 20 sq cm. |
| PDx | [[M72.6]] | Necrotizing fasciitis of the neck. |

> [!tip] 
> If the provider documents Ludwig angina instead, the classification changes to [[K12.2]], so confirm the diagnostic statement.  
> **CDI**: Surface area in sq cm must be documented to support the add-on.

### Example 3
> **Clinical Scenario:**  
> A patient presents with MRSA necrotizing fasciitis of the right lower leg and sepsis present on admission. The surgeon debrides muscle and fascia, and hyperbaric oxygen is started as an adjunct.

| **Field** | **Code** | **Rationale** |
|---|---|---|
| CPT | [[11043]]-[[-RT]] | Right lower extremity muscle/fascia debridement. |
| CPT 2 | [[99183]] | HBO attendance and supervision, per session. |
| PDx | [[A41.02]] | Sepsis present on admission is sequenced first per the guidelines. |
| SDx | [[M72.6]] | Secondary diagnosis and MCC. |
| SDx 2 | [[B95.62]] | Documents the organism for the soft tissue infection. |

> [!tip] 
> With sepsis POA, the sepsis code leads and [[M72.6]] captures the MCC.  
> **CDI**: Query for organ dysfunction or shock, which would add [[R65.21]] and affect severity.

## ⚠️ Coding Pitfalls and Tips  

- **Pitfall 1**: Reporting **[[M72.6]]** for **[[Fournier gangrene]]**. **Tips**: The Index sends it to **[[N49.3]]**, so check the Index first.
- **Pitfall 2**: Omitting the organism code. **Tips**: Add **[[B95.0]]**, **[[B95.62]]**, **[[B96.5]]** or the documented organism code.
- **Pitfall 3**: Sequencing **[[M72.6]]** ahead of sepsis present on admission. **Tips**: Follow **Section I.C.1.d**, and sequence **[[sepsis]]** first when it meets the definition.
- **Pitfall 4**: Using **[[11000]]/+[[11001]]** or depth-based codes in place of **NSTI** codes. **Tips**: Use **[[11004]]**-**[[11006]]** for the covered regions, and **[[11043]]/[[11046]]** elsewhere.<sup>6</sup>
- **Pitfall 5**: Billing multiple **[[debridement]]** codes without **[[-59]]** and documentation. **Tips**: Document each distinct site and depth, then append **[[-59]]** (**or X{EPSU}**) per payer rules.<sup>4,5</sup>
- **Pitfall 6**: Accepting "**[[cellulitis]]**" when the operative report says **[[necrotic fascia]]**. **Tips**: Send a **CDI query**, because clinical validity of the **MCC** is a common audit target.
