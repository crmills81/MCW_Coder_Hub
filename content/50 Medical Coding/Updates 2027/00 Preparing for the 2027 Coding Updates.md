The most significant structural shift for 2027 is the **deletion of global obstetric bundles, replacing them with discrete, service-level reporting**. For inpatient profee, this fundamentally changes how delivery, operative repairs, and inpatient postpartum care are captured.

<center><h3>FY 2027 ICD-10-CM Updates (Effective Oct 1, 2026)**</h3></center>

The **FY 2027 update** introduces **190** new diagnosis codes. These are the most relevant additions for chart abstractions across urology, otolaryngology, and physical medicine:

- **Otolaryngology (ENT) & Head/Neck:**
    - `J34.830` Odontogenic sinusitis, maxillary sinus (the new *J34.83* subcategory also covers ethmoid, frontal, and sphenoid variations).
    - `C78.31` Secondary malignant neoplasm of larynx.
    - `C78.32` Secondary malignant neoplasm of pharynx.
    - `C79.83` Secondary malignant neoplasm of oral cavity.
- **Urology & Pelvic:**
    - `K6A.01` Prevesical abscess.
    - `K6A.09` Other pelvic abscess.
- **Physical Medicine & Rehabilitation (PM&R):**
    - `M67.A01` Plantar fasciitis, right foot.
    - `M67.A02` Plantar fasciitis, left foot.
    - _(Note: For PM&R charts involving multiple sclerosis, continue reporting the expanded [[G35.D]] subcategory implemented earlier this year, as the parent code remains invalid for billing)._
- **General Inpatient Rounding:**
    - `E89.830` Post bariatric hypoglycemia (includes a "Use Additional" note for hypoglycemia level).
    - `Z68.18` Body mass index (BMI) 18.4 or less, adult.
