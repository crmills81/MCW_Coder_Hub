> The **AAPC team** has scoured the fiscal year (FY) **2027 ICD- 10-CM coding update** — **which includes 238 new codes, four revised codes, and 21 deleted codes** — to bring you a comprehensive list of changes to prepare you for diagnosis coding beginning Oct. 1, 2026.

### Neoplasm and Blood Disease 


Secondary malignant coding will be more specific under **ICD- 10-CM 2027**, as the **C78.- Secondary malignant [[neoplasm]] of respiratory and digestive organs** and **C79.- Secondary malignant neoplasm of other and unspecified sites codes** are expanded. Currently, the **Table of Neoplasms** instructs you to report a secondary malignant neoplasm of the larynx, pharynx, or oral cavity with **[[C78.39]]** Secondary malignant neoplasm of other respiratory organs. 


**Beginning Oct. 1**, you will have three new codes to pinpoint the anatomical sites of these metastatic cancers more precisely: 
- **[[C78.31]]** Secondary malignant neoplasm of larynx 
- **[[C78.32]]** Secondary malignant neoplasm of pharynx 
- **[[C79.83]]** Secondary malignant neoplasm of oral cavity 


There has also been a small but highly significant addition to the **D50-D89 Diseases of the blood and blood-forming organs** and certain disorders involving the immune mechanism codes. The inclusion terms for code **D69.1 Qualitative platelet defects** are deleted and reallocated to two new codes: 
- **[[D69.11]]** Glanzmann thrombasthenia 
- **[[D69.19]]** Other qualitative platelet defects 


> [!tip]
> Under **[[D69.11]]**, you will now find Glanzmann’s disease and hemorrhagic and hereditary thrombasthenia, while **Bernard- Soulier (giant platelet) syndrome**, grey platelet syndrome, and thrombocytopathy will all be found under **[[D69.19]]**. 
> 


The last big change in oncology coding is in the **D05.- Carcinoma in situ of breast codes**. Currently, **ICD-10-CM** allows you to report a code from **C50.- Malignant neoplasm of breast** with a code from **D05.-**, as **C50.-** is subject to an **Excludes2 instructio**n under **D05.-**. Beginning Oct. 1**, the **Excludes2 instruction** is an **Excludes1**. This means you will no longer report the two codes together if a patient is diagnosed with both conditions — you will only report a **C50.- code** in those circumstances.

### Cardiac Coding Changes 

The **FY 2027 ICD-10-CM revisions** offer more specific descriptions for various diagnoses. For example, **I42.0 Dilated cardiomyopathy** will expand to **I42.00 Dilated [[cardiomyopathy]], unspecified,** **I42.01 Familial-genetic dilated cardiomyopathy**, and **I42.09 Other dilated cardiomyopathy**. 

Additionally, conditions often reported today under **I42.8** **Other cardiomyopathies** or **I49.8** **Other specified cardiac arrhythmias** will have distinct reportable codes, including: 
- **[[I42.81]]** Arrhythmogenic cardiomyopathy 
- **[[I47.22]]** Catecholaminergic polymorphic ventricular tachycardia [CPVT] 
- **[[I49.81]]** Brugada syndrome 
- **[[I49.82]]** Ventricular bigeminy 

If the provider’s documentation doesn’t support any of these more specific codes, you have these options: 
- **[[I42.89]]** Other cardiomyopathies not elsewhere classified 
- **[[I49.89]]** Other specified cardiac arrhythmias not elsewhere classified

### Odontogenic Sinusitis 

**ICD-10-CM** adds **odontogenic sinusitis diagnose**s with the **J34.83- Odontogenic sinusitis code subcategory**. You’ll use a 6th character to identify which sinus is affected by the condition (**maxillary, ethmoid, frontal, or sphenoid sinus**). Use **[[J34.839]] Odontogenic sinusitis, unspecified** for cases of the condition where the physician didn’t specify which sinus was affected.

**FY 2027 ICD-10-CM** adds **M67.A- Plantar fasciitis** with the following codes:

- **[[M67.A01]]** Plantar fasciitis, right foot 
- **[[M67.A02]]** Plantar fasciitis, left foot

For **plantar fascial fibromatosis (M72.2-)**, there are three expanded code options:

#### Osteomyelitis 

The **M86.8X- Other osteomyelitis codes** are expanded, allowing you to specify not only the body part but also laterality. You will need to choose a **7th character** to identify whether the affected anatomical structure is unspecified or is on the right or left side of the body. 

You’ll also need a **7th character** for **M86.8X8- Other osteomyelitis, other site** to identify which structure is affected, such as: 
- **[[M86.8X80]]** Other osteomyelitis, skull 
- **[[M86.8X81]]** Other osteomyelitis, face and sinuses 
- **[[M86.8X89]]** Other osteomyelitis, other site 

Another new **M code**, **[[M04.3]] VEXAS Syndrome**, was created for reporting a serious **autoinflammatory** condition that typically begins in adulthood and is characterized by the immune system targeting the body’s own tissues.

#### Toxic Effects 


There are several additions to the **toxic effects codes**, starting with **T52.81- Toxic effects of alkenes**. You will have to choose **6th and 7th characters for T52.81-**, as you will with most of the other toxic effects codes in **ICD-10-CM**. The **6th character** identifies the intent of the toxic exposure (**accidental, intentional self-harm, assault, etc.**). The **7th character** identifies encounter type for the injury. 

The new toxic effect code subcategories for **FY 2027** are: 
- **T52.82**- Toxic effects of cycloparaffins 
- **T52.89-** Toxic effects of other organic solvent

#### More Specificity Means More Sensitivity 


**In Chapter 21**: Factors influencing **health status and contact with health services (Z00-Z99)**, you’ll find a mixed bag of changes. 

Changes to **body mass index (BMI) codes** offer greater specificity. In **FY 2026, Z68.1** **Body mass index [BMI] 19.9 or less**, adult captured the entire adult **BMI range at or below 19.9** in a single code, but in FY 2027 that code splits into **Z68.18 Body mass index [BMI] 18.4 or less, adult** and **Z68.19 Body mass index [BMI] 18.5-19.9, adult.** 

**Other new codes include:** 
- **[[Z77.013]]** Contact with and (suspected) exposure to gadolinium 
- **[[Z77.32]]** Contact with and (suspected) exposure to burn pits in war theater 
- **[[Z77.33]]** Contact with and (suspected) exposure to Agent Orange 
- **Z77.4**- Contact with and (suspected) exposure to blast overpressure 
- **[[Z86.17]]** Personal history of Clostridioides difficile infection 

The **FY 2027 update** also offers more specificity and greater sensitivity in reporting changes to patients’ sex and gender by expanding **Z87.890** *Personal history of sex reassignment* to include: 
- **[[Z87.8901]]** Personal history of social gender transition 
- **[[Z87.8902]]** Personal history of medical gender transition 
- **[[Z87.8903]]** Personal history of surgical gender transition 
- **[[Z87.8904]]** Personal history of intersex surgery 
- **[[Z87.8909]]** Personal history of unspecified gender transition 
- **[[Z87.893]]** Personal history of gender detransition
