# 🧮 DRG Mapping Sheet

## 🎯 Purpose
Quick reference for how diagnoses, procedures, and CC/MCCs impact DRG assignment.

---

# 🏥 DRG Logic Overview
1. Determine **principal diagnosis**
2. Check for **OR procedures**
3. Identify **CC/MCC**
4. Assign DRG based on grouping logic

---

# 🧩 CC/MCC Impact
| Type | Impact |
|------|---------|
| **MCC** | Highest severity, increases DRG weight |
| **CC** | Moderate severity |
| **None** | Base DRG |

---

# 🛠 OR Procedure Impact
- OR procedure may **shift DRG family**
- Some procedures **don’t** impact DRG
- Device‑related procedures often shift DRG

---

# 📘 Common DRG Families
- Respiratory  
- Cardiac  
- GI  
- Renal  
- Infectious  
- Trauma  
- OB/Newborn
