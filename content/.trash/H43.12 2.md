# ICD-10-CM: H43.12

## Quick view
- **Short definition**: Vitreous hemorrhage in the left eye.
- **Body area:** Body system/region: Eye—vitreous body (left eye).[](https://www.aapc.com/codes/icd-10-codes/H43.12)
- **HCC**: not typically mapped to a CMS-HCC category

## Long definition
> A vitreous hemorrhage is blood within the vitreous body (the gel-filled posterior segment of the eye); H43.12 specifies the condition occurs in the left eye.
