>[!Note] DEFINITION of [[Nondisplaced]]
>A nondisplaced fracture is a type of bone break where the bone cracks or breaks but **retains its proper alignment**, meaning the broken pieces do not move out of place. These fractures can be partial or complete and may heal more easily than displaced fractures.
_____
>[!info]+ ETYMOLOGY of [[Nondisplaced]]
>From _[non-](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/non-#English "non-")_ +‎ _[displaced](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/displaced#English "displaced")_.
